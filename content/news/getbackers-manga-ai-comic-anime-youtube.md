## Background

*GetBackers* began as a shōnen manga by Yuya Aoki and Rando Ayamine, featuring the escapades of two “recovery agents” who retrieve lost items for a fee.  The series’ blend of action, humor, and supernatural elements earned it a dedicated following.  In 2002, a 49‑episode anime adaptation brought the characters to life, and it later found a U.S. audience through ADV Films and, more recently, Sentai Filmworks.

Tokyopop’s early English release helped introduce *GetBackers* to international readers, but the loss of rights in 2009 left many fans without an official English manga.

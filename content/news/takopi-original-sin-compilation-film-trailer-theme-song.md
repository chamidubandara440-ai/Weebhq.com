# Takopi's Original Sin Compilation Film Drops New Trailer + Theme Song – Fans Go Wild!

**In a whirlwind of excitement, the Takopi's Original Sin anime community just got a fresh burst of joy.** The official X/Twitter account of the Taizan 5‑produced anime posted a brand‑new trailer and visual for the compilation film *Takopi's Original Sin : Thank You, See You Tomorrow* (Takopii no Genzai –Arigatō, Mata Ashita‑). The clip not only showcases the film’s condensed storyline but also reveals the much‑anticipated theme song, *Rabyū* (Love You) by the iconic artist Tele. With the film set to hit Japanese theaters on **November 27**, the buzz is reaching a fever pitch.

## What We Know

- **Film Title & Release**: *Takopi's Original Sin : Thank You, See You Tomorrow* (Takopii no Genzai –Arigatō, Mata Ashita‑) will open in Japan on **November 27**.
- **Theme Song**: Tele, who performed the ending theme for the original TV anime, returns to lend her voice to the film’s new track *Rabyū* (Love You).
- **Trailer Content**: The newly released trailer offers a polished visual preview, stitching together key moments from the six‑episode series into a cohesive narrative arc.
- **Anime Origins**: The original anime ran for six episodes, debuting on **Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, ABEMA, U‑NEXT, and other streaming services** in **June 2025**.
- **Accolades**: The series earned the Jury Award for a TV series at the **2026 Annecy International Animation Film Festival** in June.
- **Creative Team**: Directed by **Shinya Iino** (known for *Dr. Stone* and *Dr. Stone: Stone Wars*), with character design by **Keita Nagahara** (key animator for *Violet Evergarden*, *Sound! Euphonium*, *Sonny Boy*). Production handled by **ENISHIYA**.
- **Voice Cast**: Kurumi Mamiya voices Takopi, Reina Ueda as Shizuka Kuze, Konomi Kohara as Marina, and Anna Nagase as Azuma.
- **Manga Background**: The source material, a Taizan 5 manga, launched on Shueisha’s *Shonen Jump+* in December 2021 and concluded in March 2022. The second and final compiled volume was released in April 2022. The manga is available in English on Shueisha’s *MANGA Plus*.
- **Awards & Rankings**: The manga was nominated for the **16th Manga Taisho Awards** and the **27th Annual Tezuka Osamu Cultural Prizes** in 2023, won the **Manga Kingdom Tottori award** at the 51st Japan Cartoonists Association Awards in 2022, and ranked **#3** on the *Kono Manga ga Sugoi!* guidebook’s top 20 manga for male readers list in 2022.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The release of the trailer and the announcement of Tele’s new theme song have set the fandom abuzz for several reasons:

1. **Tele’s Return** – Fans of the original series remember Tele’s heartfelt ending theme from the TV run. Her return to the franchise signals continuity and a promise of emotional depth.
2. **Compilation Clarity** – The six‑episode series, while beloved, left some viewers craving a more streamlined story. The film’s compilation offers a concise, polished experience, especially for those who missed the original streaming run.
3. **Award‑Winning Credibility** – The anime’s Jury Award at Annecy and the manga’s multiple nominations lend the project a prestige that fans feel proud to celebrate.
4. **Community Anticipation** – The film’s release date, only a month away, has sparked countless fan art, discussion threads, and countdown posts across social media, amplifying the sense of a shared cultural moment.

## Background

*Takopi's Original Sin* follows the misadventures of a cute pink alien octopus named Takopi who lands on Earth with a mission that seems simple at first: spread happiness across the universe. However, his attempts to help a depressed fourth‑grader, Shizuka, using advanced technology to turn back time spiral into mayhem and tragedy. The series explores themes of memory, sacrifice, and the true nature of happiness.

The franchise began as a manga by Taizan 5, serialized on *Shonen Jump+* from December 2021 to March 2022. The manga’s compelling narrative and unique art style earned it critical acclaim and a strong fanbase, leading to a six‑episode anime adaptation that premiered in mid‑2025 across multiple streaming platforms.

The anime’s director, Shinya Iino, brings a distinct visual flair and storytelling approach, while Keita Nagahara’s character designs retain the manga’s charm while enhancing expressiveness. Production by ENISHIYA ensured high‑quality animation and sound design, culminating in a series that resonated with audiences worldwide.

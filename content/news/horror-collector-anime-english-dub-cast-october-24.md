# Horror Collector Anime Drops English Dub Cast & October 24 Premiere – Fans Can’t Miss It!

The horror anime community is buzzing as Crunchyroll announced today that the English‑dub version of Midori Satō’s *Horror Collector* will debut on **October 24**. The first episode’s voice cast, production team, and release details are now fully revealed, giving fans a concrete reason to keep their eyes on the streaming platform. This isn’t just a new dub; it’s a culmination of a long‑running franchise that has spanned novels, manga, and now television—making the English debut a landmark moment for international audiences.

## What We Know

- **Anime debut**: The series premiered on NHK on **October 10, 2026**, and Crunchyroll is streaming it simultaneously as it airs in Japan.
- **Studio**: *Horror Collector* is produced by **Studio Palette**.
- **Director & Series Composition**: **Yuki Inaba**—known for *KamiKatsu: Working for God in a Godless World* and *Our Last Crusade or the Rise of a New World* season 2—directs and composes the series.
- **Character Design**: **Kaori Yoshikawa** brings the creepy, atmospheric visuals to life.
- **Music**: Composed by **Masato Kōda**.
- **Audio Direction**: Handled by **Tetsuya Gunji**.
- **English Dub Cast (Episode 1)**:
  - Brandon Allan Majella as Fushigi
  - Larry Brantley as Jimmy
  - Rebecca Danae as Himitsu
  - Patrick McAlister as Aoyama
  - Rowan Gilvie as Crimson Woman
  - Lexa Childress as Hiromi UkuLeili
  - Jonathan Lim as Raita
  - Jordan Chantha Weir as Rin
  - Chris Sanders as Sasaki
  - Zareen Afzaal as Yui
  - Jamie Lee as Yurie
- **Dub Production Team**:
  - Studio: Crunchyroll Studio
  - Producers: Justin Cook, Michael Harcourt, Nathanael Harrison
  - Producer: Samantha Herek
  - Adaptation Supervisor: Jarrod Greene
  - Adaptation: Audrey Drake Rigg
  - Adaptation Prep: Benjamin Tehrani
  - Voice Director: Helena Walstrom
  - Talent Coordination: Tara A. Williams, Brittni Hurley
  - Engineering Managers: Gino Palencia, Brandon Peters
  - Engineer: Noah Whitehead
  - Mixer: Andrew Tipps

- **Publication History**: The original *Horror Collector* novel series began in June 2015, with the 29th volume released on October 7. The manga adaptation by Niko Yuki ran in *Monthly Comic Gene* (2021‑2022) and was compiled into a single volume in March 2022. Yen Press released the sixth novel volume in May 2025 and the manga volume the same month.

## Why Fans Are Excited

For years, *Horror Collector* has been a staple of the horror‑anime community, largely because of its unique premise: internet‑age creepypastas and urban legends become tangible threats, and a mysterious boy in a red hood—who appears wherever the supernatural strikes—offers a glimmer of hope. The novel and manga adaptations already have a dedicated fanbase that has eagerly awaited a faithful visual representation.

The announcement of a full English dub is a game‑changer. Voice actors like Brandon Allan Majella and Rebecca Danae have already proven their chops in other anime dubs, and their involvement signals a high‑quality localization effort. Fans appreciate the nuance of the original Japanese dialogue, and the dub team’s roster suggests that they’re committed to preserving the series’ eerie tone.

Moreover, the fact that Crunchyroll streams the anime simultaneously with its Japanese broadcast removes the lag that often frustrates international viewers. This real‑time access is a huge plus for the community, who can discuss plot twists and character developments in the same breath.

## Background

Midori Satō’s *Horror Collector* began as a series of novels that explored the thin line between internet folklore and reality. The narrative centers on a boy in a red hood who roams town to town, appearing whenever the supernatural manifests. He’s a paradox—both a savior and a harbinger—raising questions about whether monsters are mere myths or tangible dangers.

The franchise’s growth was steady: after the novels, a manga adaptation by Niko Yuki brought the stories to a visual medium, gaining traction in *Monthly Comic Gene*. Yen Press’s English releases broadened the audience, making the series accessible to a global readership.

The anime adaptation, produced by Studio Palette, brings the chilling atmosphere to life with Yuki Inaba’s direction and Masato Kōda’s music. The production team’s experience with other horror‑heavy titles ensures that the series will maintain the unsettling tone fans expect.

## What’s Next

While the official release date for the English dub is set for October 24, the timing suggests that subsequent episodes will stream on Crunchyroll as they air in Japan. This means that fans can expect a steady stream of new content, likely following the same schedule as the Japanese broadcast.

Given the depth of the source material—over 29 novel volumes and a manga adaptation—there is ample story to adapt. Fans can speculate that the anime will cover the early volumes, focusing on the boy in the red hood’s encounters with internet‑age horrors. The inclusion of a full dub from the start indicates a long‑term commitment to the series, hinting that more episodes and possibly a second season could be on the horizon.

The involvement of prominent names like Yuki Inaba and Kaori Yoshikawa also suggests that the production values will remain high. As the anime progresses, viewers can anticipate more complex character arcs, especially for the protagonist, Fushigi, and the enigmatic Crimson Woman voiced by Rowan Gilvie.

## Closing

The English dub debut of *Horror Collector* is more than a simple voice‑over release—it’s a milestone that brings a beloved horror franchise into the hands of a global audience. With a stellar cast, experienced production crew, and simultaneous streaming, Crunchyroll is setting the stage for a truly immersive experience. Anime fans who crave atmospheric dread and nuanced storytelling should mark their calendars: **October 24** is the day the horror world expands beyond Japan. Stay tuned for more updates as the series unfolds, and get ready to dive into the unsettling universe of *Horror Collector*.

# Reincarnated as a Sword Season 2 Drops New Trailer, Cast & Visual for Seedrun Arc

The anime world is buzzing as **Reincarnated as a Sword** (Tensei Shitara Ken Deshita) just released a brand‑new trailer, a striking visual, and a lineup of fresh voice actors for the Seedrun Arc of its second season. With the new arc beginning in episode six, fans have an instant reason to dive back into the world of a sentient sword and its unlikely human partner. The buzz is real—HIDIVE fans and international viewers alike are already scrambling to catch the latest footage as the series continues to expand its mythos.

## What We Know

- **New Trailer & Visual**: The official website showcased a sleek trailer and a high‑quality visual that highlight the Seedrun Arc’s themes of power, destiny, and the bond between Fran and the sword.
- **Seedrun Arc Begins Episode 6**: The new storyline kicks off with the sixth episode of season two, promising a shift in narrative focus and a deeper dive into the world’s lore.
- **Cast Additions**:
  - *Satsuki Kokubu* voices **Fult**.
  - *Yurie Kozakai* takes on **Satya**.
  - *Hazuki Ogino* portrays **Miriam**.
  - *Sayumi Suzushiro* lends her voice to **Sellimea**.
  - *Katsuyuki Konishi* plays **Valuza**.
- **Original Anime Details**: The first season premiered on Tokyo MX in October 2022, with an early release on ABEMA in September 2022. HIDIVE streamed the series concurrently with an English dub.
- **Staff Continuity**: Shinji Ishihira returns as director, Takahiro Nagano handles scripts, Atsuko Saitō designs characters, and Yasuharu Takanashi composes the score.
- **Music & Themes**: The opening theme is performed by FZMZ, titled “Dream of Butterfly,” while the ending theme “Baribari Buddy” is sung by Hanabie.
- **Manga & Light Novel Context**: The franchise began as a light novel on Shōsetsuka ni Narou in 2015, with a manga adaptation by Tomowo Maruyama starting in 2016. Seven Seas Entertainment currently publishes the English translations.

## Background

**Reincarnated as a Sword** follows the story of a sentient sword that retains memories of its previous life but loses its name. It saves Fran, a cat‑eared beastgirl enslaved by a cruel master, and becomes her weapon. Fran’s ambition is simple: grow stronger, while the sword seeks to understand its purpose in this new world. The first season, which aired in 2022, established this dynamic and introduced a host of supporting characters.

The franchise began as a light novel series on the Shōsetsuka ni Narou platform in October 2015, later published by Gentosha with illustrations by LLO in 2016. A manga adaptation by Tomowo Maruyama debuted on Gentosha’s Denshi Birz in December 2016, and Seven Seas Entertainment has released the English translations. Audiobooks narrated by Josh Hurley were launched in August 2024, expanding the series’ accessibility.

The anime adaptation, directed by Shinji Ishihira and written by Takahiro Nagano, has been praised for its faithful adaptation of the source material, strong character design by Atsuko Saitō, and a memorable soundtrack by Yasuharu Takanashi. The first season’s success paved the way for a second season, which continues the story while exploring new territories.

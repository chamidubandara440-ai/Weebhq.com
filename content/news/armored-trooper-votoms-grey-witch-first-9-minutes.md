# Armored Trooper Votoms: The Grey Witch Films Stream First 9 Minutes – A Real Robot Revival

The moment many fans have been waiting for has finally arrived. On Wednesday, the first nine minutes of *Armored Trooper Votoms: Die Graue Hexe* (also known as *Armored Trooper Votoms: The Grey Witch*) streamed online, giving a taste of what the new Sunrise and Production I.G collaboration promises. While the clip is short and lacks English subtitles, it already signals a bold return to the gritty, grounded world of Votoms, and the excitement in the community is palpable.

## What We Know

- **Title and Format**: *Sōkō Kihei Votoms Haiiro no Hexe*, translated as *Armored Trooper Votoms: Die Graue Hexe* or *The Grey Witch*, is a two‑film project.
- **Release Schedule**: The first film opens in Japan on Friday, with the second film scheduled to follow.
- **Streaming Preview**: The opening nine minutes of the first film were made available on Wednesday. No English subtitles are included.
- **Key Cast**:
  - Miyuki Sawashiro as Ash Vamps, the infamous Grey Witch.
  - Mabuki Andou as Maki Stauffenberg, an officer investigating Ash’s past.
  - Shinichirō Miki as Alix Barrack.
  - Yūki Kaji as Ignis Schweinsteiger.
  - Yūichi Nakamura as Fritz Gersdorf.
  - Satoshi Tsuruoka as Brunhart Müller.
  - Tomokazu Sugita as Adrian Dysler.
  - Kenta Miyake as Matt Hummels.
  - Chafūrin as Soltig van Gunnar.
  - Hōchū Ōtsuka as Vid Hildebrandt.
  - Daisuke Ono as Meteusz Julian Podolsky.
  - Takayuki Sugō as Weissweiler.
  - Takahiro Sakurai as Oliver.
  - Sumire Uesaka as the Maintenance Girl.
- **Crew Highlights**:
  - **Director**: Mamoru Oshii, known for *Ghost in the Shell* and *Patlabor 2*.
  - **Supervisor**: Ryōsuke Takahashi, creator of the original 1983 *Armored Trooper Votoms* concept.
  - **Mechanical Designers**: Shinobu Tsuneki (*Ghost in the Shell: Stand Alone Complex*) and Yoshiro Sōno, artist of *Sōkō Kihei Votoms: AT Stories*.
  - **Composer**: Kenji Kawai, long‑time collaborator with Oshii.
  - **Theme Song**: “Na mo Naki Shijin no Yō ni” (Like Some Nameless Poet) performed by Mika Nakashima, written, composed, and arranged by Toshihiko Takamizawa.
  - **Character Design**: Original concepts by Kazuchika Kise; character designs by Shingo Ogiso, Takashi Kojima, and Tetsuya Nishio.
- **Production Companies**: Sunrise and Production I.G.
- **Background Footage**: The preview is part of a broader marketing push that includes a dedicated website and forum discussions on Comic Natalie.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The Votoms franchise is a cornerstone of the real robot subgenre, a genre that treats mecha as mass‑produced military hardware with realistic limitations. Fans who grew up with the 1983 series recognize the significance of seeing a modern iteration of the VOTOMS (Vertical One‑Man Tank for Offense & Maneuvers) in action. The casting of Miyuki Sawashiro as Ash Vamps—a notorious AT pilot known as the Grey Witch—has already sparked speculation about the film’s tone and narrative direction. The involvement of Mamoru Oshii, a visionary director whose previous works have redefined the genre, adds another layer of anticipation.

Moreover, the film’s opening scenes showcase the raw, battle‑scarred aesthetic that defined the original series: thin‑armored mechs, high casualty rates, and the emotional toll on pilots. For a community that values depth and realism in mecha storytelling, the preview is a promise that this new project will stay true to its roots while leveraging modern animation techniques.

The presence of seasoned voice actors and a robust creative team signals that the production is serious about delivering a quality experience. The fact that the preview was released without subtitles might also create a buzz among non‑Japanese fans who are eager to translate the content themselves, fostering a sense of collective engagement.

## Background

*Armored Trooper Votoms* debuted in 1983, setting a new standard for realism in mecha anime. It is set during the waning years of a century‑long war between the Gilgamesh Confederation and the Balarant Union. The VOTOMS units dominate the battlefield, but their fragile armor and lack of safety measures lead to staggering pilot casualties. The original protagonist, Chirico Cuvie, finds himself entangled in a conspiracy that brands him a traitor, forcing him to confront a mysterious woman.

Since then, the franchise has expanded into numerous spin‑offs: *The Last Red Shoulder*, *Big Battle*, *Armor Hunter Mellowlink*, *Red Shoulder Document – Roots of Treachery*, *Pailsen Files*, *The Heretic Saint*, and *Alone Again* (the latest entry, released in 2011). Section23 Films’ Maiden Japan label released the original series on Blu‑ray in 2019 and 2020, keeping the series accessible to new generations.

The new films represent the first major studio‑backed addition to the franchise in more than a decade. The collaboration between Sunrise, known for *Mobile Suit Gundam*, and Production I.G, famed for *Ghost in the Shell*, underscores the ambition of this project.

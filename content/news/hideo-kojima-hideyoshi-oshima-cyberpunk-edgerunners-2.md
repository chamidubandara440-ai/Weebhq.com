# Hideo Kojima Returns as Hideyoshi Oshima in Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 – A Must‑See Cameo

The cyberpunk world is buzzing louder than ever as the legendary game director Hideo Kojima announces his return to the franchise, this time in the brand‑new Netflix anime *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2*. In a surprise that has fans scrambling for screenshots and speculation, Kojima will reprise his role as the braindance producer Hideyoshi Oshima— a character he first voiced in the 2020 game *Cyberpunk 2077*. The announcement came with a handful of photos from his voice‑recording sessions, proving that the director is not just a name on the credits but an active participant in the series’ creative process.

## What We Know

- **Kojima’s cameo**: Hideo Kojima will voice Hideyoshi Oshima again in *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2*, the sequel to the 2022 Netflix series produced by Studio Trigger.
- **Character background**: Hideyoshi Oshima is a braindance producer—a key figure in the cyberpunk universe, involved in creating immersive virtual experiences.
- **Previous appearances**: Kojima first voiced Oshima in the 2020 video game *Cyberpunk 2077*, which is based on the long‑running tabletop role‑playing game series created by Mike Pondsmith.
- **Recording photos**: The director posted several images of his voice‑recording session on X/Twitter, giving fans a behind‑the‑scenes look at the process.
- **Release timeline**: *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2* premiered on Netflix on October 20, 2026, just a few weeks after Kojima’s announcement.
- **Franchise lineage**: The original tabletop game started in 1988, with editions such as *Cyberpunk 2013*, *Cyberpunk 2020*, *Cyberpunk V3.0*, and the latest *Cyberpunk Red*. The 2020 video game and the 2022 anime both drew heavily from these roots.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The sheer fact that Kojima himself is stepping into the anime’s voice booth is a rare treat. Fans of the *Cyberpunk* universe have long revered Kojima for his visionary storytelling and intricate world‑building. Seeing him return as a voice actor—rather than merely a behind‑the‑scenes director—creates a sense of personal investment that feels almost like a secret handshake between the creator and the audience.

Moreover, the cameo is a nod to the deep interconnectivity of the franchise. Fans who have followed *Cyberpunk 2077* since its early days will recognize Hideyoshi Oshima’s distinctive voice and the lore surrounding braindance technology. The character’s presence in the anime ties together the narrative threads from the game, the original tabletop series, and the animated adaptation, offering a satisfying continuity that rewards long‑time followers.

The photos of Kojima recording his lines also add a layer of authenticity. In an era where voice‑over work is often outsourced or done remotely, seeing the director himself in the studio underscores the importance he places on the anime’s quality. It also fuels speculation about whether Kojima might be involved in future projects, potentially hinting at a larger *Cyberpunk* multimedia strategy.

## Background

*Cyberpunk: Edgerunners* debuted on Netflix in September 2022, produced by Studio Trigger and based on the *Cyberpunk 2077* video game. The series expanded the universe with new characters and a fresh narrative while staying true to the gritty, neon‑lit aesthetic that fans love. It was a critical success, praised for its animation style and faithful adaptation of the game’s themes.

The original *Cyberpunk 2077* game was released in 2020 and was heavily influenced by Mike Pondsmith’s tabletop RPG series. The game’s world, characters, and technology—such as braindance—draw directly from the tabletop lore. Hideyoshi Oshima, as a braindance producer, plays a pivotal role in that narrative, providing a bridge between virtual experiences and the harsh realities of Night City.

Now, with *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2* premiering on Netflix, the franchise is taking another leap forward. Studio Trigger’s involvement ensures that the animation quality remains top‑tier, while Kojima’s direct participation promises a storyline that stays true to the source material.

## What's Next

While no new release dates or plot details have been announced beyond the October 20 premiere, the announcement opens up several avenues for speculation—grounded in the facts we have. First, the inclusion of Hideyoshi Oshima hints that the anime may explore deeper aspects of the braindance industry, possibly revealing new technologies or ethical dilemmas that tie back to the game’s narrative.

Second, Kojima’s active voice work could suggest that he might be involved in future cross‑media projects. Whether that means additional anime seasons, spin‑off games, or even a cinematic adaptation remains to be seen, but the director’s presence is a strong indicator that the *Cyberpunk* brand is still evolving.

Finally, the release timing—just weeks after Kojima’s announcement—suggests a coordinated marketing push. Fans can expect a surge in merchandise, behind‑the‑scenes content, and perhaps a Q&A session with Kojima on the platform, though no official event has been confirmed.

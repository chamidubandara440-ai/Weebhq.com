**Star Detective Precure!** has just shattered the Precure franchise’s box‑office ceiling, becoming the highest‑grossing standalone film in the long‑running series. In a single month, the movie has pulled in an estimated **¥1.24 billion** (about **US$7.8 million**) from **977,000** tickets, eclipsing the 2024 hit *Wonderful Precure! The Movie!* The news hit WeebHQ.com just 1.6 hours after the announcement, and the reaction across fandoms is electric.

## What We Know
- **Opening**: The film premiered in Japan on **September 18** and quickly climbed to **#3** in its opening weekend.
- **First‑three‑days performance**: 358,000 tickets sold, generating **¥446 million** (≈ US$2.82 million).
- **First‑month total**: 977,000 tickets, **¥1.24 billion**.
- **Storyline**: The Detective Precures investigate the “X Mark Incident.” A fairy named **Rain** leads them to **Karin’s Garden**, which is overrun by tiny “Dame Dame” creatures. The team uncovers a hidden secret within the garden.
- **Voice cast**: Akari Kitō (Karin), Nobunaga Shimazaki (Alan), Kōki Uchiyama (Rain), with past Precure characters from *You and Idol Precure!* and *Wonderful Precure!*.
- **Crew**:
  - Director: **Hanako Ueda** (also directed *You and Idol Precure!* and *Summer Time Rendering*).
  - Script: **Yoshimi Narita** (known for *Eiga HappinessCharge Precure!* and *Precure All Stars New Stage 3*).
  - Music: **Erika Fukasawa** and **Misaki Umase**.
  - Character design & chief animation: **Emiko Miyamoto**.
  - Ending theme: “FIRST PRISM” by Yūka Masui & Chihaya Yoshitake; insert song “First Step” by Ami Ishii.
- **Anime series**: *Star Detective Precure!* debuted on **February 1** on ABC TV, TV Asahi, and affiliates at 8:30 a.m. JST. It’s streamed worldwide on **Crunchyroll**.
- **Plot of the series**: Anna Akechi, a second‑year middle‑schooler from Makoto Mirai Town, travels back from 2027 to 1999, meets detective‑dreamer Mikuru Kobayashi, and together they battle the Phantom Thieves to protect smiles.

## Why Fans Are Excited
The numbers speak for themselves, but the excitement extends beyond the box‑office. For a franchise that has spanned over two decades, achieving a new financial milestone is a testament to the enduring charm of the Precure brand. Fans are thrilled to see the *Detective Precures*—a fresh take on the magical‑girl formula—receive such robust support. The inclusion of beloved characters from earlier seasons and the high‑profile creative team give the film a sense of continuity and prestige that resonates with long‑time viewers.

The rapid sales also suggest a strong overseas interest, as the movie is already available on Crunchyroll. With the anime’s global streaming footprint, international audiences can feel the momentum, creating a worldwide fan‑base that is buzzing over the film’s success.

## Background
The Precure franchise, short for “Pretty Cure,” has been a staple of the magical‑girl genre since 2004. Each new entry typically releases a feature film that ties into the season’s storyline, often featuring cross‑overs and guest appearances from past Precure teams. *Star Detective Precure!* is the 23rd main anime installment and introduces a detective‑themed narrative, a departure from the usual battle‑centric plots.

The film’s storyline—centered on a garden, a fairy, and mysterious creatures—mirrors the series’ emphasis on mystery and teamwork. The production team boasts a roster of seasoned veterans: director Hanako Ueda, scriptwriter Yoshimi Narita, and composer Erika Fukasawa, all of whom have contributed to multiple high‑profile Precure films.

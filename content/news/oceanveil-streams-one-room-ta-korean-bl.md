# OceanVeil Drops Korean BL Anime 'One-Room TA' on Nov 13 – First Korean Animation on the Platform!

In a move that’s sending ripples through the anime community, OceanVeil has announced it will begin streaming the Korean-animated boys‑love series **One‑Room TA** on **November 13**.  This marks the platform’s first foray into Korean animation, expanding its catalog beyond the Japanese anime that has dominated its library.  With an uncensored version scheduled to air weekly through December 25, fans have a full month of fresh BL content to dive into.

## What We Know

- **Release date**: November 13, 10:00 a.m. PST / 1:00 p.m. EST.
- **Platform**: OceanVeil.
- **Series**: *One‑Room TA*.
- **Origin**: Adaptation of Jibung’s webtoon, first released in South Korea on Laftel on August 28, 2026.
- **Production**: Laftel.
- **Voice cast**: Beom‑sik Shin as U‑yun Song, Seong‑woo Kang as Jin‑hyeong Woo, Eui‑jin Jung as Eun‑oh Ji.
- **Plot hook**: University student U‑yun Song confesses to graduate teaching assistant Jin‑hyeong Woo, they begin dating, and move into a one‑room apartment where Jin‑hyeong’s BL‑novel obsession surfaces.
- **Streaming details**: Uncensored version, weekly episodes until December 25.
- **English webtoon**: Available on Lezhin.

All of these facts come straight from OceanVeil’s October 6 announcement and the Anime News Network report dated October 20.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The excitement is palpable for several reasons.  First, OceanVeil’s decision to license a Korean BL series is a bold step that signals the platform’s willingness to diversify beyond its traditional Japanese anime focus.  For a community that has largely been served by Japanese titles, this opens a new avenue of storytelling and cultural representation.

Second, the source material—Jibung’s webtoon—has already cultivated a dedicated following in South Korea, and the English version on Lezhin has attracted international readers.  Fans are eager to see how the adaptation will capture the webtoon’s quirky romance tropes, especially the dynamic between U‑yun and the eccentric Jin‑hyeong.

Third, the voice cast includes notable names like Beom‑sik Shin and Seong‑woo Kang, whose previous work has been praised for emotional depth.  The promise of an uncensored version also appeals to viewers who want the full BL experience without content cuts.

Finally, the timing—premiering just weeks after its Korean debut—means the series is fresh in the public eye, and the weekly schedule through December 25 creates a binge‑worthy window that aligns with the holiday season.

## Background

*One‑Room TA* is produced by Laftel, a studio that has been gaining traction for its high‑quality animation in recent years.  The series adapts a webtoon that has already proven successful online, indicating that the story has a proven audience.  OceanVeil’s acquisition of the series is part of a broader strategy to incorporate more Korean content, a trend that has been growing as Korean media gains global popularity.

The premise—two university students navigating romance in a cramped apartment—offers a relatable, slice‑of‑life setting that has resonated with BL fans worldwide.  By focusing on the interplay between U‑yun’s earnest confession and Jin‑hyeong’s BL‑novel fandom, the series promises both heart‑warming moments and comedic subversion of romance tropes.

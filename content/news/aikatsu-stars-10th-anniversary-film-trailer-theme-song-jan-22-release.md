# Aikatsu Stars! 10th Anniversary Film Drops Trailer, Theme Song, Jan 22 Release!

Anime fans, the countdown has officially begun. Bandai Namco Pictures just released the full trailer for **Aikatsu Stars! Hoshiboshi no Progress (The Progress of the Stars)**, the film that marks the 10th anniversary of the beloved franchise. The teaser not only confirms a January 22 opening, but also reveals the energetic main theme **“Five Stars”** and the heartfelt ending **“Anata e”** (To You), performed by the iconic quartet of Sena, Rie, Miki, Nanase, and Kana. With the film’s premiere just weeks away, the buzz is reaching fever‑pitch levels across fan communities worldwide.

## What We Know

- **Title & Theme** – *Aikatsu Stars! Hoshiboshi no Progress* (The Progress of the Stars) celebrates the franchise’s decade‑long journey.
- **Release Date** – The movie opens in Japan on **January 22, 2027**.
- **Music** – Main theme **“Five Stars”** and ending theme **“Anata e”** are performed by the five original idol voices: Sena, Rie, Miki, Nanase, and Kana.
- **Tagline** – “Our (rainbow‑colored) dream isn’t over yet.”
- **Plot Hook** – The story begins just before Yume and her friends graduate, when they receive an invitation from Neo Venus Ark’s Elza to perform at the world‑class **Venus Jamboree**. A mysterious passenger ship, the Petit Venus Ark, appears, setting the stage for a new adventure.
- **Creative Team** – Director **Teruo Sato** returns, along with scenario writer **Yūko Kakihara** and character designer **Yukiko Aikei**.
- **Distribution** – Bandai Namco Pictures will distribute the film.
- **Franchise Context** – The original *Aikatsu Stars!* TV series ran from April 2016 to March 2018 (100 episodes). The first *Aikatsu Stars!* film debuted in August 2016. The franchise has expanded through *Aikatsu Friends!* (2018‑2019), *Aikatsu on Parade!* (2019‑2020), *Aikatsu! Planet* (2021), and *Aikatsu! Academy!* (YouTube‑exclusive VTuber project, 2024). The overall 10th‑anniversary film for the *Aikatsu!* franchise, *10th Story ~Mirai e no Starway~*, opened in January 2023.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The announcement has lit up social media, fan forums, and streaming platforms. For long‑time followers, the return of the original *Aikatsu Stars!* team—director, writer, and designer—promises a nostalgic yet fresh take on the series’ signature style. The inclusion of the five original idol voices for both the main and ending themes reaffirms the franchise’s commitment to its core identity.

Fans are also thrilled by the film’s narrative premise. The invitation to the **Venus Jamboree** and the appearance of the mysterious **Petit Venus Ark** suggest a story that blends high‑energy idol performances with unexpected twists. The tagline, “Our dream isn’t over yet,” resonates with viewers who have followed the characters through their growth and graduation, hinting at a continuation that honors their journey.

The release timing—just after the holiday season—positions the film as a fresh start for the new year, a theme that aligns perfectly with the franchise’s message of perseverance and ambition.

## Background

*Aikatsu!* began as an arcade card game in 2012, inspiring a 2012‑2016 TV anime and three films. The *Aikatsu Stars!* spin‑off, launched in April 2016, introduced Yume Hoshizora and her friends, who compete in the Neo Venus Academy. The series concluded in March 2018 with 100 episodes, but its legacy continued through subsequent installments, each with its own arcade card game.

The 10th‑anniversary film for the entire *Aikatsu!* franchise, *10th Story ~Mirai e no Starway~*, celebrated the series’ history in January 2023, bringing together the original *Aikatsu!* creative team. Now, *Aikatsu Stars!* is taking the spotlight again, this time with a film that not only commemorates a decade but also pushes the narrative forward.

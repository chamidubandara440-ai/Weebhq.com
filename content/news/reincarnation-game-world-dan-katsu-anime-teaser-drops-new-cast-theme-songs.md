## What We Know

- **New Cast Members**: The teaser introduced four key voices:
  - *Ayaka Nanase* as Hanna, the alchemist’s daughter and childhood friend of Zephyrus.
  - *Kotone Izumi* as Sierra, a noble‑family beauty.
  - *Hikaru Iida* as Lana, a princess.
  - *Chihaya Asakura* as Ester, Lana’s guardian knight.
- **Theme Songs**: The opening track, *"Play the Play"*, is performed by **MindaRyn**, while the ending theme, *"My Life Story"*, is by **LINKL PLANET**.
- **Voice & Staff**: The protagonist, Zephyrus, is voiced by *Yūto Uemura*.  The series is directed by *Nagisa Miyazaki* (known for *Negima!*, *Eternal Alice*, *Final Examination Kujira*) at **STUDIO MASSKET**, with scripts by *Yūji Kobayashi* (credited for *Pretty Guardian Sailor Moon Crystal: Season III*, *Kusunoki's Garden of Gods*).
- **Premiere**: The anime will debut in **January 2027**.
- **Source Material**: The story originates from Kaede Nishikigi’s light novel series, first launched on Kakuyomu in January 2021.  TO Books began publishing the novels in March 2022, with illustrations by Shuri.  The 16th volume shipped on August 15.  A manga adaptation by Hiroshi Asagi started on TO Books’ Corona EX site in April 2022, with the first compiled volume released in September 2022 and the fifth in October 2025.

## Background

*Reincarnation in the Game World Dan‑Katsu* began as a light novel on the free‑reading platform Kakuyomu, where Kaede Nishikigi first shared the concept in January 2021.  The story quickly gained traction, leading TO Books to publish the novels in March 2022.  With each volume, the world-building deepened: the protagonist, Zephyrus, is a seasoned gamer who suddenly finds himself in the very game he has spent millennia mastering.  The narrative explores themes of identity, destiny, and the intersection of virtual and real worlds.

The manga adaptation, launched on TO Books’ Corona EX platform in April 2022, has already released five compiled volumes, further expanding the franchise’s reach.  The adaptation’s art, handled by Hiroshi Asagi, captures the vibrant dungeon landscapes and character designs that fans love.

Now, with the anime adaptation announced, the story is set to reach a broader audience.  STUDIO MASSKET, known for its dynamic animation style, will bring the game world to life, while the staff’s experience promises a faithful yet fresh take on the source material.

The arrival of Yūsuke Kobayashi in the television adaptation of Nao Iwamoto’s *Marronnier Ōkoku no Shichinin no Kishi* (The Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom) is sending shockwaves through the anime community. Fans who have followed the fantasy romance series since its 2016 launch in Shogakukan’s *Flowers* magazine are already buzzing as the voice actor steps onto the screen in episode 7, bringing a new layer of intrigue to the already complex narrative. The timing is perfect—just a week after the manga’s 11th volume shipped and with Amazon Prime Video’s global stream, the announcement feels like a carefully placed plot twist that will keep viewers glued to their screens.

## What We Know
- **Character & Debut**: Yūsuke Kobayashi voices the “mysterious boy,” a newcomer who appears in episode 7 on Saturday. The character has an equally mysterious plan for the seven knight brothers.
- **Current Cast**: The anime already features a robust lineup:
  - Kengo Takanashi as Nemukunai (Insomniac)
  - Makoto Tanikawa as Hakuai (Fraternity)
  - Haruki Ishiya as Atsugariya (Feverish)
  - Satoshi Inomata as Samugariya (Chilly)
  - Kiyoto Mizuno as Kemono Tsukai (Beast‑Tamer)
  - Taishi Murata as Tsurugi Jiman (Swordsman)
  - Reiji Kawashima as Harapeko (Hungry)
  - Akeno Watanabe as Baribara
  - Show Hayami as Narrator
- **Staff & Production**:
  - Director: Kiyoko Sayama (known for *Skip Beat!*, *Vampire Knight*, *To Your Eternity* seasons 2‑3)
  - Writer/Series Scripts: Shinzō Fujita (*To Your Eternity* seasons 1‑3, *The World Is Still Beautiful*)
  - Character Designer: Yuriko Maeda (chief animation director for *Requiem of the Rose King*, *One Punch Man* TV 2)
  - Music Composer: Yoshiaki Fujisawa (*Apocalypse Hotel*, *Land of the Lustrous*, *Love Live! School Idol Project*)
  - Sound Director: Jin Aketagawa (*Dr. Stone*, *Fire Force*)
- **Broadcast & Streaming**: Premiered on NHK Educational channel on October 3 at 6:25 p.m. JST (5:25 a.m. EDT). Amazon Prime Video streams the anime worldwide and offers an English dub.
- **Manga Background**: The source manga, written by Nao Iwamoto, launched in 2016 and has shipped 11 volumes as of June 10. It topped the 2018 *Kono Manga ga Sugoi!* guidebook list for female readers.
- **Licensing**: Viz Media licensed the manga on October 1 and released the first two chapters digitally that same day.

## Background
*The Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom* centers on seven brothers who serve as knights of the Marronnier Kingdom, a realm positioned at the heart of a cluster of eight kingdoms. Each brother is dispatched to a different kingdom as an ambassador, embarking on adventures to find their destined partners. This blend of fantasy, romance, and adventure has resonated with readers, especially after the manga’s 2018 accolade in *Kono Manga ga Sugoi!*.

The anime adaptation, directed by Kiyoko Sayama, brings the story to life with vibrant character designs by Yuriko Maeda and a soundtrack that captures the series’ sweeping emotional arcs. The production team’s experience—ranging from *One Punch Man* to *Love Live!*—ensures that the adaptation stays true to the source material while adding fresh elements that appeal to both longtime fans and newcomers.

# The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady's Treasure Film Drops New Video Revealing Kajadan Pirates Cast

The world of **The Apothecary Diaries** just got a major upgrade. On Tuesday, TOHO Animation released a brand‑new character trailer for the upcoming anime film *The Apothecary Diaries: The Late Lady's Treasure* (Kusuriya no Hitorigoto: Bо̄hi no Hihо̄), and the teaser is already sending shockwaves through the fanbase. The trailer spotlights the infamous Kajadan river pirates and, for the first time, reveals the voice actors behind the group’s leaders. With the film slated to hit Japanese theaters on December 11, this announcement feels like a countdown to a cinematic event that’s already on everyone’s radar.

## What We Know

- **TOHO Animation** streamed the new character trailer on its official channel on Tuesday.
- The trailer focuses on the **Kajadan river pirates** and introduces the cast for the group’s leaders.
- **Kenshō Ono** voices **Suija**, the current head of the Kajadan who is on a quest to find his late father Kaja’s hidden treasure.
- **Ryōta Takeuchi** voices **Eishō**, Suija’s loyal subordinate.
- **Jirō Saitō** voices **Kaja**, the former leader who died a year ago and left behind a hidden treasure.
- A visual for **Seisei**, Suija’s older sister and Kaja’s daughter, was also shown, though her voice actor has not yet been announced.
- The film’s website hinted at an upcoming character video for **Ruo Qing** and **Yi Shan** (romanizations not confirmed) to be released tomorrow.
- The story follows **Maomao** and **Jinshi** as they travel to the hometown of a concubine who died five years ago in the imperial harem, where they encounter pirates, hidden treasures, the secret of the Empress' remains, and a mysterious boy who holds the key to the story.
- The film will open in Japan on **December 11** and features a brand‑new story by the original novel series’ author **Hyuganatsu**.
- **Mariya Ise** joins the cast as the key character **Mu Qing** (Bi clan son). **Nobuo Tobita** plays **Birō** (Bi clan head), **Kumiko Watanabe** plays **Shūu** (Birō’s wife), and **Hirofumi Nojima** plays **Bisen** (Bi clan’s eldest son). All name romanizations are not confirmed.
- **Crunchyroll** has acquired the North American and worldwide rights (excluding Asia but including India) to the film. Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment will announce the release date at a later time.
- The third television anime season premiered on October 2 in the “Friday Anime Night” block on NTV and 29 affiliated channels, airing for a first cours. The second cours will premiere in April 2027.
- The first season premiered in October 2023 with the first three episodes and aired for two cours. The second season premiered in January 2025 on Nippon TV’s FRIDAY ANIME NIGHT block and aired for two consecutive cours.
- **Crunchyroll** streamed both seasons as they aired in Japan and also streamed English dubs.
- **J‑Novel Club** publishes the novel series digitally, and the manga adaptation is published by **Nekokurage** in Square Enix’s Monthly Big Gangan magazine. Square Enix Manga & Books is releasing the manga adaptation in English.
- **Viz Media** is releasing Minoji Kurata’s separate manga adaptation titled *The Apothecary Diaries: Maomao’s Notes on the Inner Palace*.

## Background

*The Apothecary Diaries* began as a light novel series serialized on **Shōsetsuka ni Narō** in October 2011 by **Hyuganatsu**. The series was later published in print by **Shufunotomo** in August 2014, with illustrations by **Touko Shino**. A manga adaptation started in May 2017 under **Nekokurage** in *Monthly Big Gangan*, and the manga has been licensed in English by **Square Enix Manga & Books**.

The story centers on **Maomao**, an unassuming girl with a sharp mind and extensive medical knowledge, who is kidnapped and sold into service at the imperial harem. There, she becomes a lady‑in‑waiting to the Emperor’s favorite consort, using her medical expertise to uncover poisons. Her partnership with the head eunuch **Jinshi** forms the core dynamic of the series.

The anime adaptation began airing in October 2023, with two seasons completed by early 2025. The series has been streamed by **Crunchyroll**, which also offers English dubs, making it accessible to a global audience.

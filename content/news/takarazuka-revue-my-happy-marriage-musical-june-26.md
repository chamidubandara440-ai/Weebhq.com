**All‑female Takarazuka Revue’s Flower Troupe is stepping onto a new stage, literally, with a live‑action musical adaptation of the beloved light‑novel series *My Happy Marriage*.  From June 26 to August 8 in Hyōgo and then from August 28 to October 10 in Tokyo, the troupe will bring the story’s sharp wit, dramatic twists and heartfelt romance to the Takarazuka Grand Theater and the Tokyo Takarazuka Theater.  The news is a game‑changer for fans who have followed the series through novels, manga, anime, and even a live‑action film—now the narrative is finally taking the form of a full‑scale musical production.  The timing is perfect: the announcement comes just weeks before the first run begins, and the hype is already reaching a fever pitch across fandom forums, social media, and streaming platforms.

## What We Know
- **Performance dates and venues**: The Flower Troupe will perform at the Takarazuka Grand Theater in Hyōgo from **June 26 – August 8** and at the Tokyo Takarazuka Theater from **August 28 – October 10**.
- **Cast**: *Sea Towaki* stars as Kiyoka, the cold, heartless soldier, while *Misaki Hoshizora* takes on the role of Miyo, the young noblewoman caught between duty and desire.
- **Director**: The musical is helmed by **Daisuke Tabuchi**, whose vision will translate the novel’s layered relationships into dynamic stage choreography and musical numbers.
- **Co‑showing**: The production will run alongside the **TAKARAZUKA SPECTACULAR『MANHATTAN FOLLIES』－Seize The Day－** show, ensuring that audiences experience two distinct yet complementary Takarazuka performances in a single visit.
- **Anime and OVA background**: The first season of the *My Happy Marriage* anime premiered on television in July 2023 and was available worldwide on Netflix from the same month.  An OVA titled *Shiawase no Katachi* (The Shape of Happiness) was bundled with the eighth novel volume in March 2024 and streamed globally on Netflix in November 2024.
- **Second season and special**: The second season aired on Japanese networks in January 2025 and was simultaneously released on Netflix.  An anime special was broadcast on TV Tokyo on October 25 and streamed on Netflix the same day.
- **Other adaptations**: The manga adaptation by Rito Kohsaka debuted on Gangan Online in December 2018, with Square Enix Manga & Books releasing it in English.  A live‑action film opened in March 2023, topping the Japanese box office on opening weekend with 479,700 tickets sold and 654 million yen in revenue.

## Background
*My Happy Marriage* (Japanese: *Watashi no Shiawase na Kekkon*) began as a light‑novel series written by Akumi Agitogi and illustrated by Tsukiho Tsukioka in January 2019.  The story follows Miyo, a noblewoman raised by an abusive stepmother, who is married off to Kiyoka, a soldier whose previous fiancées fled within three days of engagement.  With no home to return to, Miyo slowly opens her heart to Kiyoka, navigating a rocky introduction toward potential true love and happiness.

The series quickly expanded beyond the page.  Yen Press licensed the novels for English‑speaking audiences.  In December 2018, Rito Kohsaka’s manga adaptation launched on Square Enix’s Gangan Online, and Square Enix Manga & Books later released the manga in print and digital English editions.  A live‑action film premiered in March 2023, becoming a box‑office hit and cementing the franchise’s popularity.

The anime adaptation, produced by a studio that remains unnamed in the source, aired its first season in July 2023.  With 12 episodes, the series captured the story’s comedic and emotional beats, and Netflix’s worldwide release helped the series reach a global fanbase.  The second season continued the narrative in January 2025, while a special episode aired in October 2024, further expanding the storyline.

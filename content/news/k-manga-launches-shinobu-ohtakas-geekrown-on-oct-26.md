# K Manga Launches Shinobu Ohtaka’s ‘Geekrown’ on Oct. 26 – A Must‑Watch for Anime Fans

**The moment you’ve been waiting for is finally here.** Kodansha’s K MANGA platform has just announced that the long‑awaited manga *Geekrown* (also known as *Geek and Crown*) will be added to its service on **October 26**, with the series going live on the *Magazine Pocket* app the following day. For fans of Shinobu Ohtaka’s work—best known for *Magi*—this is a huge win: a new, high‑profile title from a creator who has proven time and again that he can deliver epic, political thrillers with razor‑sharp pacing.

## What We Know

- **Launch Dates** – *Geekrown* will appear on K MANGA on **October 26** and on the *Magazine Pocket* app on **October 27**.
- **Story Premise** – The series follows a treasonous military officer caught in the middle of a succession crisis, promising a blend of political intrigue and action.
- **Author Background** – Shinobu Ohtaka, creator of *Magi*, first introduced *Geekrown* (originally titled *Orient*) in *Weekly Shōnen Magazine* in May 2018. The series moved to *Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine* in February 2021.
- **Completion** – The manga entered its final arc in July 2023 and concluded in October 2024. Kodansha released the 22nd and final compiled volume in November 2024.
- **English Release** – Kodansha USA Publishing has been releasing the series in English, with the 20th volume hitting shelves on April 14.
- **Anime Adaptation** – The first cour of the anime premiered in Japan in January 2022. Crunchyroll streamed both the original and English dub. The second cour aired on TV Tokyo in July 2022, covering the “Awajishima Gekitō-hen” (Fierce Battle at Awaji Island Arc), and was also streamed by Crunchyroll with an English dub.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The announcement hit the community like a thunderclap. Fans of Ohtaka’s *Magi* and *Orient* have been clamoring for more content from the same creative mind, and the fact that *Geekrown* is now accessible on K MANGA means that the series is finally within reach of a broader audience. The platform’s user base—known for its love of shōnen and seinen titles—will appreciate the political depth and the high‑stakes narrative that Ohtaka is renowned for.

The timing is also perfect. With the anime’s second cour having just finished airing, there is a renewed interest in the source material. Viewers who enjoyed the “Awajishima Gekitō-hen” arc are eager to see how the manga expands on the world, characters, and political machinations. The launch on *Magazine Pocket* ensures that mobile readers can catch up on the latest chapters in real time.

Moreover, the fact that Kodansha USA has already released 20 volumes in English means that the series is fully localized. English‑speaking fans can now dive into the story without waiting for new translations, making the K MANGA launch even more compelling.

## Background

Shinobu Ohtaka first made waves with *Magi*, a series that blended adventure, fantasy, and political intrigue. After that success, he turned his attention to *Orient* (later *Geekrown*), a series that explores the brutal realities of war, betrayal, and the struggle for power. The manga’s progression—from *Weekly Shōnen Magazine* to *Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine*—mirrors its growing popularity and the author’s ambition to reach a wider demographic.

The story’s central figure—a treasonous military officer—serves as a vehicle for exploring themes of loyalty, ambition, and the cost of power. Ohtaka’s narrative style is fast‑paced and layered, ensuring that each chapter feels like a new battlefield. The manga’s conclusion in October 2024, with the 22nd volume released in November, marked the end of a long, satisfying arc that kept readers on the edge of their seats.

The anime adaptation, which premiered its first cour in January 2022, brought the series’ intense action to life. Crunchyroll’s streaming of both the original and English dub broadened the audience, while the second cour’s focus on the “Awajishima Gekitō-hen” arc showcased the series’ capacity for large‑scale battles and complex political maneuvering.

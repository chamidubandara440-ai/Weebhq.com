Hey there, fellow manga and anime enthusiasts! Weeb here, bringing you some absolutely massive news to kickstart your week. If you’ve been keeping up with the thrilling world of manga, you are going to want to sit down for this double whammy of an announcement.

The critically acclaimed and intensely gripping manga series, **Manchuria Opium Squad**, is officially making the leap to the small screen! That’s right—an anime adaptation has been greenlit. 

But the hype train doesn't stop there. We also have major news regarding the original source material. 

### The Manga Resumes Serialization This Monday!

For fans who have been eagerly anticipating the return of the printed series, the wait is officially over. The *Manchuria Opium Squad* manga is scheduled to resume its serialization this coming Monday. 

### A Brand-New Artist Takes the Reins

When the manga makes its highly anticipated return on Monday, readers will experience the story through a fresh creative lens. A new artist, **Tsurushima**, is officially taking over the illustration duties for the series moving forward. 

With an anime adaptation now confirmed and the manga returning to active serialization with Tsurushima at the artistic helm, there has never been a more exciting time to be a fan of this franchise.

**A brand‑new teaser for Yuka Ōshima’s *Haru to Gen ~70‑sai, Hajimete Kosodate~* just dropped, and the anime community is already on edge.** The short‑format television adaptation has revealed a host of new voice actors and a catchy ending song that promises to set the tone for the heart‑warming story of a 70‑year‑old grandfather learning to raise his granddaughter for the first time. With a premiere slated for January 4 on TV Kanagawa, this is the kind of announcement that turns a quiet title into a must‑watch phenomenon.

## What We Know

- **New cast members**: Jin Urayama voices Ginji, Hitomi Shogawa voices Kaoru, Nao Shiraki voices Ai, and Akiko Seri voices Ūpii. These additions expand the ensemble that supports Gen’s journey.
- **Ending theme**: “Chokin‑bako Mitai na♡Song” (A Piggy Bank‑Like♡Song) performed by Nono‑chan (Nonoka Murakata). The music is produced by Kuricorder Quartet, with lyrics by Reo Rinozuka and composition by Masaki Kurihara. Producers Makoto Kimura (BLUE RIGHTS) and Naho Fujisawa (Nippon Columbia) oversee the track.
- **Main cast**: Bin Shimada lends his voice to Gen, the 70‑year‑old protagonist. Hiromi Igarashi voices Haru, Gen’s three‑year‑old granddaughter. Mayuko Kazama portrays Sakura, Gen’s late daughter. Kōdai Satō (Tom to Jerry Gokko) directs the series.
- **Production team**: The anime is a collaboration between IMAGICA Infos, Imageworks Studio, and Studio Nanahoshi. Sound direction by Taeko Miura (Kanagawa Elves, Hyakki Yakou Shou), sound effects by Maki Takuma (After‑School Hanako‑kun, Love Live! Nijigasaki High School Idol Club), and music composition by bermei.inazawa (Potion, Waga Mi o Tasukeru, Marukano). RD handles sound production, while Kazuya Takahashi (Love and Lies, Saiyuki Reload Blast) serves as executive producer.
- **Broadcast details**: Premieres on TV Kanagawa (tvk) on January 4 at 9:55 p.m. (7:55 a.m. EST).
- **Manga background**: The story follows Gen, a divorced 70‑year‑old who, after his daughter’s death, is tasked with raising his three‑year‑old granddaughter Haru. The manga began on Kadokawa’s TATESC Comic platform in December 2022, with the first compiled volume released in March 2024.

## Background

Yuka Ōshima’s *Haru to Gen* is a relatively new property that has already carved out a niche. The manga’s full‑color, vertical‑scroll format on Kadokawa’s TATESC Comic platform showcases Ōshima’s signature blend of slice‑of‑life storytelling and emotional depth. The first compiled volume, released in March 2024, received praise for its realistic portrayal of aging and family dynamics.

The decision to adapt the manga into a short‑format television anime is strategic. Short‑form series allow for tighter storytelling, focusing on key scenes without filler arcs—a format that suits the manga’s concise, poignant narrative. By pairing this format with a high‑profile production team, the studio aims to preserve the manga’s emotional core while making it accessible to a broader audience.

The story itself is a fresh take on the “grandparent as caregiver” trope. Gen’s journey—from estranged husband and father to devoted grandfather—offers a unique lens on intergenerational relationships. The manga’s exploration of grief, responsibility, and the unexpected joys of raising a child at 70 has already sparked conversations about aging and family roles in Japan.

## Urgent: Legendary Voice Actor Hiroshi Tsuchida Dies at 54

Fans of anime and tokusatsu are reeling today as the industry mourns the loss of a prolific voice behind some of its most iconic characters. Hiroshi Tsuchida, whose career spanned over two decades, passed away on **October 11** from heart failure at the age of **54**. The news, released by his agency **81 Produce**, has sparked an outpouring of tributes from fans, colleagues, and industry insiders alike. With a body of work that includes *Ninja Sentai Kakuranger*, *Mobile Suit Gundam*, *Pokémon*, and even the Japanese dubs of Hollywood blockbusters, Tsuchida’s passing is felt across multiple fandoms and genres.

## What We Know

- **Name**: Hiroshi Tsuchida
- **Birth**: February 8 1972, Tokyo
- **Death**: October 11 2026, due to heart failure
- **Age at death**: 54
- **Agency**: 81 Produce
- **Funeral**: Private service attended only by close relatives, per family wishes
- **Notable roles**:
  - **Ninja Blue / Saizō** in *Ninja Sentai Kakuranger*
  - **AI unit HANE2** in the 2022 *Ultraman Decker*
  - **Kōichirō Iketani** in the *Initial D: Legend* film series
  - **Hyūi** in *Fist of the North Star: Legend of Yuria* OAV
  - **J.J. Sexton** in the four‑episode *Mobile Suit Gundam Thunderbolt* net anime and *Mobile Suit Gundam Thunderbolt: December Sky*
  - **Nadim Samaya** in *Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury – Prologue* special
  - **Platane** in the four‑part *Pokémon: Mega Evolution Special*
  - **Kurumi Risu Father** in *Sylvanian Families: Mini Story*
  - **Susumu Yamazaki** in *Space Battleship Yamato 2199* and the *Star Blazers: Be Forever Yamato – Rebel 3199* film series
  - **Trainer Okita** in the 2023 *Uma Musume Pretty Derby: Road to the Top* net series and its 2024 compilation film
  - **Shippo Marimo** in *Mebius Dust* (premiered July 9)
  - **King Lionel** in *The Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom* (premiered October 3)
  - **Damnatio Kira** in *Black Clover* season 2, episode 173
  - **Japanese voice of Lightning McQueen** in *Cars*
  - **Japanese voice of Cade Yeager** in *Transformers: Age of Extinction* and *Transformers: The Last Knight*
- **Community response**: Oricon News forums feature at least two posts discussing his passing, and the news has been shared across multiple fan sites and social media platforms.

## Why Fans Are Excited

Tsuchida’s career touched an astonishing array of franchises that span from classic tokusatsu to modern anime, video games, and even Hollywood dubs. For fans, his voice was a familiar presence in countless scenes—whether he was delivering the heroic lines of Ninja Blue, the strategic banter of J.J. Sexton, or the playful banter of Platane. The breadth of his work means that his loss reverberates across multiple communities:

- **Tokusatsu lovers** remember his iconic role in *Ninja Sentai Kakuranger*, a series that remains a staple of the Super Sentai legacy.
- **Gundam enthusiasts** recall his contributions to the *Thunderbolt* series and *The Witch from Mercury* prologue, both of which are beloved spin‑offs of the long‑running Gundam universe.
- **Pokémon fans** still chuckle at Platane’s quirky lines, and *Pokémon: Mega Evolution Special* remains a favorite among younger viewers.
- **Anime purists** who followed *Initial D*, *Fist of the North Star*, and *Black Clover* appreciate his nuanced performances.
- **Western‑anime crossover fans** recognize his work as the Japanese voice of Lightning McQueen and Cade Yeager, bridging the gap between Hollywood and Japanese dubbing.

The suddenness of his death has amplified the emotional response. With a private funeral and no public memorial, many fans are turning to online forums and social media to share memories, highlight reels, and tribute posts, creating a virtual space for collective mourning and celebration of his legacy.

## Background

Hiroshi Tsuchida entered the voice acting scene in the early 1990s, quickly establishing himself as a versatile talent capable of embodying both heroic and comedic characters. Born in Tokyo, he began his career with 81 Produce, a prominent talent agency that has represented many notable voice actors. Over the years, Tsuchida built a reputation for his expressive vocal range and his ability to bring depth to even minor roles.

His breakout role as **Ninja Blue** in *Ninja Sentai Kakuranger* cemented his status in the Super Sentai community. From there, he diversified into anime, video game dubs, and live‑action film dubbing, becoming a familiar voice across multiple mediums. His recent work in *Mebius Dust* and *The Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom* demonstrates his continued relevance and adaptability in the evolving anime landscape.

## What's Next

While Tsuchida’s passing leaves a void, several projects that he recently contributed to are already in the public eye:

2. **The Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom** debuted on October 3, and his role as King Lionel will remain a highlight in the series’ early episodes.
3. **Black Clover** season 2 aired its latest episode featuring Damnatio Kira on the same day as the news release, prompting discussions about potential future seasons.

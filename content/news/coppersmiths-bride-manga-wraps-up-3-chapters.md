Right now, the anime and manga community is buzzing with a bittersweet announcement: **The Coppersmith's Bride** manga will conclude after only three more chapters. Creator **namo** revealed on X/Twitter last Friday that the final chapter will appear in this month’s issue of Kadokawa’s *Harta* magazine on **October 15**, and if everything proceeds as scheduled, the story will wrap up in **December**. For fans who have followed Shiina and Shuu’s quiet romance for over four years, this news feels like the end of a long, gentle summer. The countdown has begun, and the stakes are higher than ever.

## What We Know

- **Launch and Publication** – The series debuted in *Harta* magazine in **May 2020** and has been serialized continuously since.
- **English Release** – J‑Novel Club has been releasing the manga in English, with the seventh compiled volume hitting the market on **June 3**. The series is also available on Crunchyroll Manga.
- **Final Chapter Timeline** – The upcoming chapter will be featured in the October 15 issue of *Harta*. If there are no unforeseen delays, the final chapter will be released in December, concluding the series.
- **Story Premise** – The narrative follows **Shiina**, a college freshman in Niigata Prefecture, who enjoys a cozy life with her coppersmith boyfriend, **Shuu**. She has grown accustomed to the rhythmic sound of his hammer, jokingly dubbing him the “smithing nut.” Suddenly, he proposes, thrusting Shiina into a dilemma: can she navigate a marriage into a prestigious smithing family while still being a fresh‑out‑of‑high‑school student?
- **Volume Releases** – Kadokawa published the eighth compiled book volume on **May 15**.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The news has sparked a flurry of reactions across social media platforms, with fans expressing a mix of excitement, nostalgia, and a touch of melancholy. The community’s enthusiasm can be traced back to several key factors:

1. **Long‑Running Arc** – Since its May 2020 launch, the series has built a dedicated readership. The prospect of a definitive conclusion after four years of storytelling generates a sense of closure that many fans are eager to experience.
2. **Character Chemistry** – The subtle, everyday romance between Shiina and Shuu has resonated with readers who appreciate realistic, slice‑of‑life narratives. The sudden proposal creates a dramatic pivot that fans anticipate will bring the characters to a meaningful climax.
3. **Cultural Context** – Set in Niigata Prefecture, the story incorporates traditional Japanese craftsmanship. Fans of cultural authenticity find the setting and the focus on copper smithing particularly appealing.
4. **English Accessibility** – With J‑Novel Club’s English releases and Crunchyroll Manga’s availability, international audiences have had consistent access to the series. The announcement that the final chapters will also be translated ensures that the global fanbase can witness the conclusion.

The excitement is further amplified by the fact that the final chapters are scheduled for release in the near future. Fans are already planning reading marathons, discussion threads, and fan art projects to celebrate the series’ ending.

## Background

**The Coppersmith's Bride** (Japanese title: *Cuprum no Hanayome*) is a romance manga that blends everyday life with the artistry of metalwork. Created by **namo**, the series has carved out a niche within the broader landscape of contemporary romance manga. Its focus on a quiet, domestic setting contrasts with more action‑heavy titles, offering a refreshing perspective.

The series’ publication history is straightforward:

- **May 2020**: Debut in Kadokawa’s *Harta* magazine.
- **May 15, 2026**: Kadokawa releases the eighth compiled volume.
- **June 3, 2026**: J‑Novel Club releases the seventh volume in English.
- **October 15, 2026**: The upcoming chapter appears in *Harta*.

Kadokawa World Entertainment (KWE), a wholly owned subsidiary of Kadokawa Corporation, is the majority owner of Anime News Network, LLC. This relationship underscores the series’ integration within the Kadokawa Group of Companies.

The Korean stage scene is buzzing as ShowNote announces the premiere of the musical adaptation of the wildly popular webtoon *The Siren: Becoming the Villain's Family*. Scheduled to open on March 24, 2027, at Seoul’s Charlotte Theater and run until June 13, 2027, this production brings the romance‑fantasy saga of Lloyd, the cursed grand duke, and the siren Aria to life in full musical spectacle. Fans and industry insiders alike are already calling it the most anticipated musical of the year.

## What We Know
- **Production company**: ShowNote, a Korean musical production firm.
- **Venue**: Charlotte Theater, Seoul.
- **Run dates**: March 24 – June 13, 2027.
- **Source material**: The musical is based on Sol Leesu’s romance‑fantasy web novel *Siren: I Became a Contract Family with the Villain*.
- **Webtoon adaptation**: Poya’s version, *The Siren: Becoming the Villain's Family*, has amassed over 220 million views on Kakao Page and Kakao Webtoon.
- **Plot focus**: Lloyd, the grand duke of the Valentine family, is plagued by a generational demonic curse, while Aria, a siren whose voice can move hearts, holds the key to lifting it.
- **Availability**: The English version of the webtoon is available on Tapas; the original web novel has no official English release.

## Why Fans Are Excited
The sheer scale of the webtoon’s popularity—over 220 million views—has already cultivated a massive fanbase eager to see their favorite characters perform live. The musical promises to translate the story’s emotional depth and dramatic twists into a visceral, auditory experience, with Aria’s enchanting voice and Lloyd’s tragic arc set to be highlighted through original songs and choreography. Moreover, the timing of the release, just a few months before the end of 2027, positions it as a marquee event for the Korean theater season, drawing attention from both domestic and international audiences.

## Background
Sol Leesu’s original web novel introduced the world of the Valentine family and the mysterious siren Aria, weaving romance with fantasy in a way that resonated deeply with readers. Poya’s adaptation into a webtoon expanded the reach, offering vivid artwork and pacing that further captivated the audience. The story’s core—an ancient curse threatening a noble lineage—provides a rich narrative for musical storytelling, where music can amplify the emotional stakes and the supernatural elements. ShowNote’s decision to adapt this particular work underscores the growing trend of translating successful digital comics into live theater, a move that has proven lucrative and artistically rewarding in recent years.

## What's Next
While the official announcement has not yet disclosed details such as the creative team, cast, or musical score, fans can anticipate a blend of contemporary and classical musical styles to mirror the story’s dual nature of romance and demonic lore. The production’s run from March through June offers a window for international tourists and streaming platforms to secure tickets, and the possibility of a subsequent tour or digital release remains a tantalizing prospect. Additionally, the success of the musical could pave the way for further adaptations of Sol Leesu’s works, potentially expanding the *Siren* franchise into films or anime.

The stage is set, the music is ready, and the curtain is about to rise on a story that has already captured the hearts of millions. For those who have followed Lloyd’s journey from the pages of the web novel to the panels of the webtoon, the upcoming musical promises a culmination that is as emotionally resonant as it is spectacular. Stay tuned—this is one event that will be hard to miss.

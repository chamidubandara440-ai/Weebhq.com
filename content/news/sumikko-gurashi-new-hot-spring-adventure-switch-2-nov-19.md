## Urgent News: Sumikko Gurashi’s New Hot‑Spring Game Is Arriving This Thursday!

If you’ve been waiting for a new Sumikko Gurashi title, the wait is almost over. On Thursday, November 19, a brand‑new Nintendo Switch 2 game, **Sumikkogurashi Welcome to Poka Poka Onsen** (Sumikkogurashi Pokapoka Onsen ni Yо̄koso), will launch. The game lets players run a hot‑spring with the beloved Sumikko characters, complete with furniture, decorations, outfits, collectibles, and minigames. The release comes at a time when the franchise is expanding across multiple media, so it’s a must‑play for fans of the adorable corner‑dwelling creatures.

## What We Know

- **Title & Platform** – *Sumikkogurashi Welcome to Poka Poka Onsen* launches on the Nintendo Switch 2.
- **Release Date** – Thursday, November 19, 2026.
- **Gameplay** – Players manage a hot‑spring resort with the Sumikko. The game features:
  - Customizable furniture and decorations.
  - Various outfits for the characters.
  - Collectibles scattered throughout the onsen.
  - Multiple minigames tied to the hot‑spring theme.
- **Franchise Context** – The game follows *Sumikkogurashi Create a Wonderful Sumikko Island!* (released for Switch in November 2025), where players collaborate to develop an island, complete quests, and grow crops across four seasons.
- **Film Tie‑Ins** – The Sumikko Gurashi film series is active:
  - 2nd film, *Eiga Sumikko Gurashi: Aoi Tsukiyo no Mahō no Ko* (The Magical Child of the Blue Moonlit Night), received a Switch game in December 2021.
  - 4th film, *Eiga Sumikko Gurashi: Sora no Ōkoku to Futari no Ko* (The Sky Kingdom and the Two Children), opened in theaters in October 2025.
  - A 5th anime film is slated for 2027.
- **Franchise History** – Sumikko Gurashi debuted in 2012 as slightly negative characters who prefer to stay in the corner of a room. The core cast includes:
  - *Shirokuma* – a polar bear sensitive to cold.
  - *Penguin?* – a penguin unsure of its identity.
  - *Tonkatsu* – a piece of pork cutlet left uneaten.
  - *Neko* – a shy cat.
  - *Tokage* – a dinosaur hiding its nature.

## Background: The Sumikko Gurashi Franchise

Since 2012, Sumikko Gurashi has cultivated a unique niche. The characters’ tendency to stay in corners resonated with audiences who appreciate subtle, off‑beat humor. Over the years, the franchise has expanded into merchandise, anime films, and video games. The Switch titles—*Create a Wonderful Sumikko Island!* and now *Welcome to Poka Poka Onsen*—represent the company’s strategy to bring the characters into interactive experiences.

The 2025 film, *Sora no Ōkoku to Futari no Ko*, and the upcoming 2027 film demonstrate the franchise’s continued growth. These releases keep the brand fresh and maintain a steady stream of content for fans.

### Shōzen Anime Unleashes New Trailer & Cast – Fans Are Already Buzzing
The moment has finally arrived for anime lovers: the television adaptation of Jiang Jia Meizi’s Chinese manhua *Shang Shan*—titled **Shōzen**—has released a brand‑new trailer and key visual, and the full cast list is now public.  The announcement came just 0.8 hours after the press release, and the excitement in the community is already at a fever pitch.  With the series slated to debut on Fuji TV’s B8station block in April 2027, this is the most urgent update we’ve seen in weeks.

## What We Know
- **Title & Source**: *Shōzen* is the TV anime adaptation of Jiang Jia Meizi’s *Shang Shan* manhua.
- **Release Window**: The show will premiere on Fuji TV’s B8station programming block (and other venues) in April 2027.
- **New Trailer & Key Visual**: A fresh trailer and a striking key visual were unveiled in the Sunday press release.
- **Cast Highlights**:
  - **Kana Hanazawa** as **Ko Kuka**, Dōzen’s childhood friend who shares a special bond with him.
  - **Rina Kawaguchi** as **Shōzen**.
  - **Hiroshi Kamiya** as **Dōzen**.
  - **Daisuke Sakuma** (Snow Man) as **Ryojin**.
  - **Aoi Yūki** as **Kensei**.
  - **Ayumu Murase** as **Chiro**.
  - **Itsurō Kawasaki** (True Cooking Master Boy, Lady Jewelpet) serves as **director** and **scriptwriter**.
- **Creative Team**:
  - **Eri Osada** (Ensemble Stars!, The World’s Finest Assassin) – character designer & chief animation director.
  - **Takurō Iga** (Magical Girl Raising Project, Oshi no Ko) – composer.
  - **Tomohiro Matsukawa** – assistant director.
  - **Kenji Yamazaki** & **Yūichi Karasawa** – monster/prop design.
  - **Keiko Kōno** & **Shihoko Shirai** – sub‑character design.
  - **Yoshihiro Kanno** – action director.
  - **Kazunori Ozawa** – effects supervisor.
  - **Seita Takahashi** – logo design.
  - **Itsuki Ōishi** – art director.
  - **Takashi Nagata** – color key artist.
  - **Yūsaku Ishimi** – visual design.
  - **Iori Yokoi** – compositing director of photography.
  - **Daisuke Hiraki** – editor.
  - **Itsurō Kawasaki** – sound director.
  - **Sachiko Nishi** – sound effects.
  - **Aoni Production** – sound production.
- **Publisher**: Jiang Jia Meizi’s *Shang Shan* is ongoing on bilibili Manga.

## Background
*Shōzen* follows the journey of **Dōzen**, a Taoist priest who encounters a young girl raised by supernatural beings during his travels. Naming her **Shōzen**, he adopts her as his disciple and sets out to teach her about human emotion and love. This narrative foundation—combining mysticism with heartfelt mentorship—draws inspiration from Jiang Jia Meizi’s original manhua while promising a fresh, anime‑style interpretation.

The adaptation is a joint effort by **Dangan Pictures** and **Cannon Code**, with Itsurō Kawasaki at the helm as director and scriptwriter. Eri Osada, known for her work on *Ensemble Stars!* and *The World’s Finest Assassin*, brings a distinctive character design approach that fans expect to translate into high‑quality animation under her chief animation director role.

Musically, Takurō Iga’s composition is anticipated to blend traditional and contemporary elements, mirroring the series’ thematic fusion of ancient Taoist philosophy and modern storytelling.

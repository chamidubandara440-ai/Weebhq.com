In a shocking turn of events that has sent waves through the anime and music communities, SPITZ’s own drummer, Tatsuo Sakiyama, was rushed to the hospital after sustaining serious injuries in a traffic accident. The incident, reported just under two hours ago, has left fans and industry insiders alike scrambling for information and hoping for a swift recovery. The news was released by the band’s official website, a key source for updates on their activities.

## What We Know
- **Injury Severity**: Sakiyama suffered serious injuries across his entire body, but the injuries are not life‑threatening. He is currently hospitalized to focus on treatment and recovery.
- **Band’s Response**: SPITZ will use pre‑existing recordings of Sakiyama’s performances for upcoming television appearances. The other three members will continue promotional activities for their upcoming album *kettle*.
- **Release Schedule**: His hospitalization will not affect the release schedule for previously recorded material.
- **Future Events**: Grasshopper, the band’s management, will monitor Sakiyama’s condition and announce decisions regarding the band’s future appearances at events such as the “Be Fes” event in February and the “SPITZ Jamboree Tour 2027 ‘kettle’” event in March.
- **Band’s Anime Contributions**: SPITZ has performed music for several anime projects, including the opening theme for *Spy×Family* season 3, the theme song for *Detective Conan: Kurogane no Submarine* (Iron Submarine) film, and the theme for the live‑action film of Fumi Yoshinaga’s *What Did You Eat Yesterday?* manga.

The rapid spread of this news has ignited a flurry of reactions across social media platforms. ## Background
SPITZ, a Japanese rock trio formed in 1992, has become a staple in the anime music scene over the past decade. Their distinct blend of hard‑rock riffs and melodic hooks has led to collaborations on high‑profile projects, including the opening theme for the third season of *Spy×Family* and the theme for the *Detective Conan: Kurogane no Submarine* film. The band’s versatility and ability to cross genres have cemented their status as a go‑to group for anime soundtracks.

Tatsuo Sakiyama, the band’s drummer, has been a foundational part of SPITZ’s sound since its inception. His energetic drumming style has driven many of the band’s hits, making his sudden absence a significant blow to the group’s live dynamic. The band’s management, Grasshopper Co., Ltd., has historically handled their public relations and event scheduling, and they are now tasked with navigating this unprecedented situation.

While the immediate focus is on Sakiyama’s recovery, the band’s future plans are already in motion. Grasshopper will monitor his condition closely and provide updates on whether Sakiyama will be able to participate in the “Be Fes” event scheduled for February.

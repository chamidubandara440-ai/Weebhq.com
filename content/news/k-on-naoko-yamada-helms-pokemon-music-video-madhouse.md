### Urgent Opening
The Pokémon franchise just dropped a bombshell that’s sending shockwaves through the anime and music communities. On Wednesday, the official Pokémon YouTube channel announced that Naoko Yamada—renowned for her work on *K‑ON!* and *The Colors Within*—has taken the helm of a brand‑new music video for Official Hige Dandism’s latest single, *Evergreen*. The video is slated to premiere on YouTube on Monday, November 30 at 11:00 p.m. (9:00 a.m. EST), and the track will drop digitally on December 1. This collaboration is the centerpiece of Pokémon’s 30th‑anniversary “Onward with Pokémon” celebration, and the buzz is already off the charts.

## What We Know
- **Director and Animation Studio**: The music video is directed by Naoko Yamada, with animation produced by the legendary studio MADHOUSE.
- **Featured Artists**: Official Hige Dandism performed the original song *Evergreen*, created specifically for this partnership.
- **Release Schedule**: The video will debut on Pokémon’s YouTube channel on November 30 at 11:00 p.m. (9:00 a.m. EST), and the single will be available for digital download on December 1.
- **Anniversary Context**: The project is part of the “Onward with Pokémon” campaign, marking the franchise’s 30th anniversary. Earlier this year, Pokémon unveiled a series of 1,025 anniversary logos, one for each Pokémon released during the games’ 30‑year history.

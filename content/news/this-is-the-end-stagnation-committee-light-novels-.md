### What is *This Is the End Stagnation Committee* About?

For those unfamiliar with the source material, the original light novel series is penned by the talented creator **Kien Aien**.

When the world faces its final days and threatens to grind to a halt, these young protagonists must band together to confront the end of existence itself.

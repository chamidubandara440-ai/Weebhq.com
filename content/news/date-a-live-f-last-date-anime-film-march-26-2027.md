# Date A Live F Last Date Announces Film Format, March 26, 2027 Release – Final Arc Teaser Drops!

Anime fans across the globe are buzzing this very moment as the long‑awaited finale of the Date A Live saga is officially set to hit the big screen. At the Fantasia Bunko Grand Thanksgiving Festival 2026 Online, Kadokawa announced that *Date A Live F: Last Date* will be presented as a feature‑length film, slated for a theatrical release on **March 26, 2027**. The teaser trailer, released alongside the announcement, gives a tantalizing glimpse of the returning staff and voice cast, and confirms that this film will bring the anime series to a close.

## What We Know

- **Format & Release:** The final arc of the *Date A Live* anime will be a film, opening in theaters on **March 26, 2027**.
- **Teaser:** A new teaser trailer and a second teaser visual were unveiled during the event, showcasing the familiar faces and the production team.
- **Staff Returning:** 
  - **Scriptwriter:** Fumihiko Shimo, who worked on *Date A Live IV* and *V*, returns to pen the story.
  - **Character Designer:** Naoto Nakamura, also from the last two seasons, will design the characters.
  - **Director:** Junji Nishimura (known for *Vladlove*, *Extreme Hearts*, *Mechanical Marie*) will helm the project at Fugaku.
- **Voice Cast:** The film will feature the entire ensemble from previous seasons: Nobunaga Shimazaki, Marina Inoue, Misuzu Togashi, Ayana Taketatsu, Iori Nomizu, Asami Sanada, Maaya Uchida, Sarah Emi Bridcutt, Minori Chihara, Ayumi Mano, Hitomi Nabatame, Akari Kageyama, and Aya Endō.
- **Distributor:** ANIMEC will handle theatrical distribution.
- **Streaming History:** Crunchyroll streamed the series as it aired, including an English dub, and Funimation also streamed the first two seasons. The first season was released on Blu‑ray/DVD in June 2014, and the second season in March 2016.
- **Past Film:** The earlier theatrical film, *Gekijōban Date A Live: Mayuri Judgment*, opened in Japan in August 2015.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The announcement comes at a time when the *Date A Live* community is still reeling from the emotional arcs of *Season V*, which premiered in April 2024. Fans have long speculated whether the franchise would end with a series finale or a cinematic conclusion. The decision to package the final story as a feature film feels like a fitting tribute to the series’ cinematic moments, especially given the emotional depth and dramatic stakes that have defined the franchise.

The teaser’s high‑energy visuals and the return of beloved staff give fans a sense of continuity. Seeing familiar names like Fumihiko Shimo and Naoto Nakamura reassures audiences that the storytelling and art style will remain true to the anime’s legacy. Moreover, the inclusion of the full voice cast—especially iconic voices like Maaya Uchida and Sarah Emi Bridcutt—has sparked a flurry of discussion on fan forums, with many praising the dedication of the production team to deliver a satisfying conclusion.

The fact that ANIMEC is handling distribution also excites fans, as the company has a track record of delivering high‑quality anime releases. The film’s March 2027 release date aligns with the end of the year’s anime season, positioning it as a potential blockbuster that could attract both long‑time fans and newcomers.

## Background

*Date A Live* began as a light novel series written by Koushi Tachibana with illustrations by Tsunako, first published in March 2011. Kadokawa’s Fantasia Bunko imprint released the 22nd and final volume in March 2020. The series has since spawned multiple manga adaptations and video games, and the anime adaptation has been a steady presence on streaming platforms.

The anime’s first season aired 12 episodes in 2013, followed by a 10‑episode second season in 2014. Funimation streamed both seasons, and the series was later released on Blu‑ray/DVD with English dubs. The first theatrical film, *Gekijōban Date A Live: Mayuri Judgment*, opened in August 2015. The third season premiered in January 2019, and the fourth season followed in April 2022, with Crunchyroll streaming each season as they aired and offering English dubs.

The franchise also includes spin‑off light novels, such as *Date A Live Fragment: Date A Bullet*, which inspired a two‑part theatrical anime adaptation in 2020. Additionally, Tachibana and Tsunako’s *King’s Proposal (Ōsama no Propose)* is slated for an upcoming anime adaptation.

Given the film’s status as the “concluding arc,” it is reasonable to expect that the story will resolve the main conflict involving the protagonist, Shido, and the various spirits, while also providing closure for key side characters. The return of the entire voice cast and core staff suggests that the film will maintain the same tone and quality that fans have come to love.

As the release date approaches, distributors may begin marketing campaigns, and the franchise’s official social media channels will likely share more teasers, character posters, and behind‑the‑scenes footage.

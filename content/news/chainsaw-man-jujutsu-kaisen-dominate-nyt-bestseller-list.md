# Chainsaw Man & Jujutsu Kaisen Dominate NYT Bestseller List – Anime Fans Celebrate!

The New York Times just released its monthly Graphic Books & Manga bestseller list, and the results are sending shockwaves through the anime‑manga community. **Chainsaw Man’s 22nd volume** shot straight to **#4**, while **Jujutsu Kaisen’s first volume** landed at **#10**. These rankings confirm that the two juggernauts are not only topping sales charts in Japan but also capturing the attention of English‑speaking readers worldwide. The news came just hours after the list was posted, and fans are already flooding social media with reaction posts, fan art, and speculation about what this means for future releases.

## What We Know

- **Chainsaw Man** Volume 22, by Tatsuki Fujimoto, is the **#4** ranked manga on the NYT monthly Graphic Books & Manga bestseller list for early November.
- **Jujutsu Kaisen** Volume 1, by Gege Akutami, secured the **#10** spot.
- The list also includes **The Summer Hikaru Died** Volume 8 at **#14** and **The Guy She Was Interested In Wasn’t a Guy at All** Volume 4 at **#15**.
- In October, **SPY x FAMILY** Volume 16 reached **#6**, and the second hardcover volume of **Witch Hat Atelier: Grimoire Edition** was ranked **#15**.
- The NYT’s Graphic Books & Manga bestseller list was first updated in October 2019, making it a relatively new but increasingly influential barometer for manga popularity in the West.

These figures are drawn directly from the NYT’s official list, which is compiled from sales data across a wide range of retailers, including Amazon, Barnes & Noble, and independent comic shops.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The immediate reaction from the community is one of pure excitement. Fans of **Chainsaw Man** are celebrating the series’ continued commercial success, noting how the manga’s blend of dark humor, visceral action, and complex characters keeps readers coming back for more. The fact that volume 22 is already in the top five is a testament to the series’ staying power.

Similarly, **Jujutsu Kaisen**’s debut volume hitting the top ten reaffirms the series’ global reach. Since its debut in Japan, the manga has seen a surge in popularity overseas, and this ranking confirms that its English‑language releases are resonating with a broad audience.

The inclusion of titles like **The Summer Hikaru Died** and **The Guy She Was Interested In Wasn’t a Guy at All** highlights the diversity of the manga market. While these titles are not as widely known as the blockbusters, their presence on the list signals that niche genres and indie works are also finding a foothold.

The ranking of **SPY x FAMILY** in October and **Witch Hat Atelier: Grimoire Edition** in November demonstrates that the trend of high‑profile manga crossing over into mainstream bestseller territory is not a one‑off event. It’s a clear sign that the Western market is increasingly embracing manga as a serious literary and entertainment medium.

## Background

**Chainsaw Man** is a manga created by Tatsuki Fujimoto, published by Shueisha and distributed in English by Viz Media. The series follows Denji, a young man who merges with his pet devil to become a chainsaw‑armed demon hunter. Its unique blend of horror, comedy, and action has earned it a dedicated fanbase and multiple awards.

**Jujutsu Kaisen**, by Gege Akutami, follows the story of a high‑school student who becomes involved in the world of curses and sorcery. The manga’s dynamic art style and fast‑paced storytelling have made it a staple in the shōnen genre.

Both series have seen successful anime adaptations, further boosting their visibility and sales. The anime releases have helped introduce new audiences to the source material, creating a virtuous cycle of interest and sales.

The NYT’s Graphic Books & Manga list is a relatively new addition to the newspaper’s bestseller categories, first introduced in October 2019. Its inclusion underscores the growing importance of manga in global pop culture and provides a benchmark for publishers and creators to gauge international reception.

3. **Cross‑Media Synergy** – With anime adaptations driving manga sales, we may see increased collaboration between animation studios and publishers. This could mean more simultaneous releases or bundled merchandise deals aimed at boosting both anime viewership and manga sales.

4. **Potential for More Rankings** – As the NYT list continues to evolve, other popular titles—such as **SPY x FAMILY** and **Witch Hat Atelier**—could climb higher in future months, especially if they release new volumes or special editions.

### The Moment Anime Fans Have Been Waiting For Just Got Real
In a headline‑making announcement that has already lit up forums and Twitter threads, Manga Mavericks Books and digital manga platform Omoi have officially licensed Man Izawa and Yumiko Igarashi’s beloved classic *Georgie* for an English release.  The duo will bring the story to life in two staggered waves: a digital launch in spring 2027 followed by a print debut in August 2027.  For fans of the 1980s‑era slice‑of‑life tale, this means that the heart‑warming journey of a spirited Australian girl to England will finally be available in full, faithful translation.

## What We Know
- **Licensing partners**: Manga Mavericks Books (the publishing arm of the Manga Mavericks website and podcast) and Omoi (formerly Azuki).  
- **Release schedule**: Digital version in spring 2027; print edition in August 2027.  
- **Story premise**: Georgie, a lively young girl living with her mother and two older brothers in rural Australia, encounters a young English noble named Lowell.  Their meeting sparks a quest that leads Georgie to England to uncover her family roots.  
- **Original run**: Serialized in Shogakukan’s *Sho-Comi* magazine from 1982‑1984.  
- **Print history**: Fairbell released the series in print, shipping the final volume in April 2014.  
- **Anime adaptation**: *Lady Georgie* aired in 1983.  
- **Manga Mavericks Books**: Founded in April 2025 to license, localize, and distribute works from indie Japanese creators worldwide.  
- **Omoi**: Launched in June 2021 under the name Azuki, rebranded November 2025.  The platform offers simulpub of chapters shortly after Japanese release and maintains a library of titles.  It previously partnered with Kodansha USA until the partnership ended in January 2023.

## Background
*Georgie* was first introduced to readers in the early 1980s, a period when Shogakukan’s *Sho-Comi* magazine was a hub for romance and slice‑of‑life narratives.  The manga’s charming depiction of a young girl’s adventure from the Australian outback to England captured the imaginations of a generation.  Its adaptation into the anime *Lady Georgie* in 1983 further cemented its place in anime history.

Decades later, the series saw a print revival through Fairbell, which released the final volume in April 2014.  However, no English translation had been officially released until now.  The launch of the English edition marks the first time the entire series will be available in a language that reaches a global audience.

Manga Mavericks Books, established in April 2025, has positioned itself as a bridge between indie Japanese creators and the worldwide market.  Its partnership with Omoi, a platform that offers simulpub and a growing library of titles, signals a strategic move to bring niche, beloved works to a broader audience.

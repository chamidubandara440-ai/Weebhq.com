## Urgent: Kakao & OpenAI Deliver a 7‑Minute AI‑Generated Anime That’s Shaking the Community

In a move that feels like a sci‑fi plot twist, Kakao Entertainment and OpenAI have just released *Ryan Begins*, the first AI‑generated short animation starring the beloved Kakao Friends character Ryan. The 7‑minute clip, produced in collaboration with the tech giant, is already sparking a frenzy on social media, forums, and streaming platforms. Fans are scrambling to watch the clip, and the event that previewed it sold out in a single day. If you missed the premiere, you’re in for a wild ride—this isn’t just another animated short; it’s a glimpse into the future of storytelling.

## What We Know

- **Title & Format**: *Ryan Begins*, a 7‑minute short animation.
- **Source Material**: Based on the webtoon of the same name, which launched last year and is the first storyline‑driven Kakao Friends series.
- **Production Partners**: Kakao Entertainment and OpenAI.
- **Creative Team**: Director Jiwon Han (known for Netflix’s *Lost in Starlight*) and AI supervisor Young‑jun Kim.
- **AI Tools**: ChatGPT’s image‑generation feature was used to create initial images from scene sketches and detailed prompts.
- **Prompt Volume**: The team used roughly 1,500 prompts for a single scene to nail the desired direction and emotional tone.
- **Human Touch**: Artists manually refined and revised the AI‑generated images to reach the intended visual quality.
- **Premiere**: A special advance screening and Q&A session on November 8 at Movieland in Seongsu‑dong, Seoul, followed by an exhibition showcasing the animation’s production process.
- **Ticket Sales**: The Q&A tickets sold out within a day of going on sale.
- **Narrative Approach**: The team focused on conveying Ryan’s emotions without dialogue or exaggerated facial expressions, encouraging viewers to interpret the story.
- **Availability**: No English version of the original webtoon is currently available.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The excitement is twofold: the novelty of AI‑generated animation and the beloved character at the center. For many, the sheer scale of prompts—1,500 for a single scene—highlights the meticulous effort behind the scenes. It’s not a lazy “AI‑only” production; it’s a hybrid model where human creativity steers the machine. This transparency has won fans over, as they see the iterative process and the hands‑on adjustments that bring Ryan’s subtle emotions to life.

Social media is buzzing with clips and screenshots from the preview. Comment sections are filled with praise for the animation’s detail, the smooth motion, and the way Ryan’s feelings are expressed through body language rather than words. The Q&A session’s sold‑out status is a testament to the community’s eagerness to understand how AI can collaborate with traditional storytelling.

## Background: The Rise of Kakao Friends and Ryan

Kakao Friends began as a set of cute characters that appeared in KakaoTalk stickers. Over the years, they’ve expanded into merchandise, theme parks, and a variety of media. The webtoon *Ryan Begins* marks a departure from the usual slice‑of‑life stories that have dominated the Kakao Friends franchise. It introduces a structured narrative that explores Ryan’s universe in a deeper, more cinematic way.

Director Jiwon Han brings experience from the Netflix animated film *Lost in Starlight*, which is known for its atmospheric visuals and emotional depth. His involvement signals Kakao’s commitment to high‑quality production values, even when experimenting with AI tools.

## What’s Next? Groundwork for a New Era

While the short itself is a one‑off for now, the production team’s approach sets a precedent. The fact that they used 1,500 prompts for a single scene shows that AI can be a powerful brainstorming partner, but the final polish still relies on human hands. This hybrid model could become the new standard for future animated projects, especially in the webtoon‑to‑anime pipeline.

The event’s success—sold‑out tickets, a live Q&A, and an exhibition—demonstrates a market appetite for AI‑enhanced storytelling. We can expect Kakao to explore longer formats, perhaps a series or even a feature‑length film, leveraging the same AI workflow. Moreover, the lack of an English webtoon version may prompt Kakao to consider international releases, especially if the short gains traction on global platforms.

## Closing Thoughts

*Ryan Begins* is more than a novelty; it’s a proof‑point that AI can augment, not replace, creative storytelling. The collaboration between Kakao and OpenAI has opened a door for future projects that blend human intuition with machine efficiency. For fans of Kakao Friends, anime, and cutting‑edge tech, this short is a must‑watch. And for the industry, it’s a signal that the next wave of animation will likely be co‑crafted by artists and AI.

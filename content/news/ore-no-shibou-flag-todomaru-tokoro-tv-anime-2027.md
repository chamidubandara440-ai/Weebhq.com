## Background: Web Manga to Anime

Web manga, serialized online on platforms such as Pixiv, Niconico Seiga, and various independent sites, have long been a testing ground for new ideas. Creators can gauge reader interest in real time, allowing them to refine characters and story arcs before committing to a longer format. When a series gains traction, it often catches the eye of production studios looking for fresh IP.

The transition from web manga to TV anime is a well‑trodden path. Historically, series like *Re:Zero* and *Bungo Stray Dogs* began as web‑serials before receiving studio backing. The process typically involves a collaboration between the original creator, a studio, and a production committee that manages funding, marketing, and distribution.

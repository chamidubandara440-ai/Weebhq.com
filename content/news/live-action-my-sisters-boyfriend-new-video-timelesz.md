## Urgent: A New Video Drops for *My Sister’s Boyfriend*—The Film’s Theme Song by timelesz Is Here!

Just two hours after the announcement, the official website for the live‑action film *My Sister’s Boyfriend* (Japanese title: *Onē‑chan no Midori‑kun*) released a brand‑new special video that showcases the film’s theme song, **“Hontō no Koto o Iu to”** (If I Tell the Truth).  The track is performed by the idol group **timelesz**, and the clip already has fans buzzing on social media.  With the film slated to open in Japanese theaters on **December 4**, this video is the first real taste of the on‑screen romance and humor that has captivated readers of Amu Meguro’s manga for years.

## What We Know

- **Release date**: The film opens in Japan on **December 4**.
- **New video**: The official website’s special video highlights the theme song *“Hontō no Koto o Iu to”* by timelesz.
- **Cast**:
  - **Masaki Hashimoto** (timelesz member, known from *Summer Complicity*) plays **Midori Yukishiro**.
  - **Runa Nakajima** (*Uchi no Otōto‑domo ga Sumimasen*) portrays **Sui Sanae**.
  - **Rinka Kumada** (*Otonanajimi*, *Kimi ni Todoke*) stars as **Momo Sanae**, Sui’s older sister and Midori’s girlfriend.
  - **Reo Matsumoto** (*I Cannot Reach You*, *Propaganda Game*) is **Ayame Mizukawa**, Sui’s childhood friend.
  - **Taisuke Niihara** (*At 25:00 in Akasaka*, *Young & Fine*) plays **Amamiya**, a new assistant producer.
- **Crew**:
  - **Mahito Kimura** (director, known from *Strobe Edge*, *Blue Spring Ride*) helms the project.
  - **Nami Kikkawa** (scriptwriter, known from *Honey Lemon Soda*) writes the screenplay.
  - **Shochiku** is the production planner and distributor.
- **Music**: The theme song *“Hontō no Koto o Iu to”* is performed by timelesz.
- **Manga background**:
  - Created by **Amu Meguro**, originally launched in **Shueisha’s Bessatsu Margaret** in **2022**.
  - The 11th compiled volume was released on **April 23**.
  - Meguro previously ended *Hinata no Blue* in **July 2021** and launched *Honey So Sweet* (August 2012–November 2015) in the same magazine.
  - **Viz Media’s Shojo Beat** has released the manga in English, spanning eight volumes.
  - The series was named one of Japanese bookstore employees’ top recommended comics in **2014**.
  - A prior live‑action adaptation of the manga opened in Japan in **March 2018**.

## Background: From Manga to Live‑Action

*My Sister’s Boyfriend* follows **Sui Sanae**, a third‑year junior high student who clashes with her older sister’s boyfriend, **Midori‑kun**.  Midori‑kun’s patronizing attitude shifts when he becomes Sui’s tutor, revealing a deeper side of his character.  The story explores sibling dynamics, teenage romance, and the awkwardness of growing up—themes that resonate strongly with a young adult audience.

Amu Meguro’s manga debuted in 2022, quickly gaining popularity for its relatable characters and heartfelt humor.  The series’ 11th volume hit shelves on April 23, and the English edition by Viz Media’s Shojo Beat has broadened its reach globally.

The 2018 live‑action film adaptation set a precedent, showing that the manga’s charm could translate to the big screen.  Shochiku’s involvement in the new project signals a commitment to high production values, while the choice of a contemporary idol group for the theme song indicates a strategy to attract both manga readers and broader pop‑culture audiences.

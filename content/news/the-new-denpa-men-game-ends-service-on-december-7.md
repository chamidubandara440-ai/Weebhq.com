Hey there, gaming family. Weeb here, and today I have some sad news to share with fans of quirky RPG adventures. If you have been spending your free time diving into the colorful world of *The New Denpa Men*, you will want to brace yourselves for this upcoming closure. 

It has been officially confirmed that the free-to-play game is scheduled to end its service on December 7. 

### A Journey From July 2024 to December 2026

For those who might need a quick refresher, *The New Denpa Men* first made its grand debut as a free-to-play title back in July 2024. The game offered players a distinct experience, but like many free-to-play titles in the modern gaming landscape, its online journey must eventually come to an end. 

With the final curtain call set for December 7, players have only a limited amount of time left to enjoy everything the game has to offer. 

### What This Means for Players

It is always tough to see a dedicated service come to a close, especially for those who have been active since the game's initial launch in July 2024. If you want to say your final goodbyes to the game, make sure to log in and get your final sessions in before the servers officially go dark this December.

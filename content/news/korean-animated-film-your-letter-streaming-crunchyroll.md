## Urgent: Your Letter Is Live on Crunchyroll—Don’t Miss This Animated Webtoon Masterpiece!

If you’ve been waiting for a fresh, heart‑warming Korean animation to light up your screens, the moment has finally arrived. On **October 19**, the Korean‑animated film *Your Letter*—the faithful adaptation of Hyeon A Cho’s beloved 2018‑2019 webtoon—began streaming on Crunchyroll. The film is available in Korean with English subtitles (and several other language options), meaning that anime fans worldwide can dive into a story about bullying, friendship, and a hidden world beneath a middle school. The release is a hot ticket right now, and the buzz is already building on social media, fan forums, and streaming platforms.

## What We Know

- **Title & Source**: *Your Letter* is based on the 2018‑2019 webtoon by Hyeon A Cho that ran on Naver WEBTOON and has an English version on WEBTOON.
- **Production Credits**: The film was directed by Yong‑Hwan Kim and produced by Studio N and Studio LICO.
- **Release History**: It premiered in South Korean theaters on **October 1, 2025**. In the U.S. and Canada, it screened through Crunchyroll Anime Nights on **August 17**.
- **Streaming Availability**: The film is now streaming on Crunchyroll (October 19) in Korean with English subtitles, plus additional language subtitles.
- **Plot Snapshot**: The story follows Sori, a kind, principled girl who faces relentless bullying in middle school. After transferring to a new school, she encounters the same harassment, but a mysterious trail of letters leads her on a magical scavenger hunt in a hidden world beneath the school. The letters are the work of an anonymous guardian angel, guiding Sori toward friendship, understanding, and self‑discovery.
- **Print Edition**: Ize Press released the webtoon as a single‑volume print edition in July 2024.

## Background

*Your Letter* is part of a growing trend of Korean webtoon adaptations that are finding success in both domestic and international markets. The webtoon ran from 2018 to 2019 on Naver WEBTOON, a platform known for its vast library of serialized stories. The transition from webtoon to film involved collaboration between Studio N and Studio LICO, two studios with a track record of bringing complex stories to life.

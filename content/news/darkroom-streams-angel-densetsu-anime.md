If you have been looking to dive into some retro anime gems, you are in luck. The streaming platform Darkroom has officially added the *Angel Densetsu* anime to its streaming library! 

For those who want a quick history lesson: *Angel Densetsu* was originally released as a two-part OAV (Original Anime Video) all the way back in September 1996. Now, decades after its initial mid-90s debut, fans can finally experience this legendary release easily via online streaming.

Finding older, classic OAVs on modern platforms can sometimes feel like searching for hidden treasure, making this streaming addition a major win for the preservation and accessibility of retro anime. Whether you are a seasoned veteran of 90s animation or a curious newcomer looking to explore the rich history of OAVs, this is one release you will definitely want to add to your watchlist.

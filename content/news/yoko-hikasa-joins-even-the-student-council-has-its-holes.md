Yōko Hikasa, a name that has become synonymous with energetic and emotive performances, has just been announced as the voice of Masaki Kotobuki, Hisako Kotobuki’s mother, in episode 7 of the television adaptation of Muchimaro’s hit manga *Even the Student Council Has Its Holes!* The news dropped just in time for the weekend premiere, and fans are already buzzing on social media.

## What We Know

* **Voice Cast Update** – Yōko Hikasa will portray Masaki Kotobuki, the mother of the council president Hisako Kotobuki, in episode 7.
* **Staff Lineup** – The anime is directed by Naoyuki Tatsuwa, known for his work on *Nisekoi* and *Promise of Wizard*. Character design is handled by Ryo Imamura, the creative force behind *ONIMAI: I’m Now Your Sister!* and the chief animation director. Masahiro Yokotani, who has written for *Re:ZERO* and *The Devil Is a Part‑Timer!*, is overseeing and scripting the series.
* **Animation Crew** – Main animators include Kay Yu, Kona Ming, Asatoshi Oki, mockingbird, SanzashiAme, and Mushiki. Art direction is by Hiromichi Itō, color design by Arisa Komatsu, and the director of photography is Junpei Takatsu.
* **Music & Sound** – The opening theme, “Kaze no Naka wa Hashirukkya Inai,” is performed by Sangatsu no Phantasia, while sound direction is handled by Satoshi Motoyama and the score is composed by Tomoki Kikuya.
* **Broadcast & Streaming** – The series premiered on October 3 and airs on Tokyo MX, BS11, Gunma TV, and Tochigi TV. It is available for streaming on d Anime Store, AnimeFesta (both on‑air and unrestricted versions), and OceanVeil, which offers English subtitles and an English dub.
* **Manga Background** – The original four‑panel manga, created by Muchimaro, began serialization in Kodansha’s *Weekly Shōnen Magazine* in 2022. The 13th compiled volume shipped on August 17, while Seven Seas released the first volume in English on September 8. K MANGA also publishes the series digitally.
* **Accolades** – The manga won the print category at the ninth Next Manga Awards (Tsugi ni Kuru Manga Taisho) in 2023, and it received an animated advertisement in July 2024.

The announcement of Yōko Hikasa’s involvement is a major talking point for several reasons:

4. **Community Engagement** – The anime’s streaming partners, especially OceanVeil’s English dub, mean that international fans can jump in right away, amplifying the buzz across global fan communities.

## Background

*Even the Student Council Has Its Holes!* is a slice‑of‑life comedy that follows Mizunoe Ume, a freshman who joins the student council to avoid repeating a grade. The council is a motley crew: an airhead, a scary member, a pervert, and others, all of whom bring chaos and charm. The manga’s charm lies in its quirky, slightly naughty humor, and its ability to endear even the most eccentric characters to readers.

The anime adaptation, produced by Passione, has already showcased the series’ signature humor and visual style. With a talented crew—director Naoyuki Tatsuwa, character designer Ryo Imamura, and writer Masahiro Yokotani—the adaptation promises to capture the manga’s spirit while adding its own flair.

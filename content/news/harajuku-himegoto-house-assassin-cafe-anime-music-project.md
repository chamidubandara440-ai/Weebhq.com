## What We Know
- **Title & Format**: *Harajuku Himegoto House* will be a television anime, accompanied by music releases and additional media.
- **Premise**: The story follows an aspiring hitman who stumbles into a rumored hideout for assassins in Harajuku, only to find a café run by an eclectic, high‑fashion, eccentric crew.
- **Voice Cast**:
  - **Yūya Hirose** as Mizuki Otokozawa, the aspiring hitman.
  - **Tetsuei Sumiya** as Mero Togariishi, a high‑tension positive “gal.”
  - **Sōhei Horikane** as Takara Rokurokusō, a foul‑mouthed gothic lolita doll.
  - **Masaru Yoshimura** as Kentarō Jinbayama, a centaur who is the ideal man.
  - **Takuma Nagatsuka** as Yuki Inubo, a man who wants to cure his blue mood.
  - **Nobuhiko Okamoto** as Hitoshi Haritsukekawara.
  - **Showtaro Morikubo** as Shō Dogenzaka, a flashy 50‑year‑old store manager with a 2 trillion yen debt.
- **Production Team**: Plott is handling planning and production.
- **Music**: Bandai Namco Music Live (Lantis) is providing the music.
- **Promotion**: Animate is collaborating on promotion.
- **Visuals**: Aki Akane (aka Akane Aki) drafted the original character illustrations.
- **First Group Song**: Set to debut on Sunday.

# TMS Streams Lupin III vs. Detective Conan Film & 4th Episode Collection – Watch Now on YouTube & Netflix

The anime community is buzzing this week as TMS Entertainment drops a free, limited‑time stream of the legendary *Lupin III vs. Detective Conan* film on its YouTube channel, while Netflix announces the launch of the fourth specially curated *Detective Conan* episode collection on November 1.  For fans who have long waited for an accessible way to relive the crossover that first hit Japanese theaters in 2013, this is a once‑in‑a‑lifetime opportunity that is not to be missed.

## What We Know

- **Free YouTube Stream** – Starting **October 24**, TMS Entertainment will stream the English‑dubbed version of *Lupin III vs. Detective Conan The Movie* on its official YouTube channel for a limited time.
- **Netflix Episode Collection** – Netflix will begin streaming the fourth specially curated selection of *Detective Conan* episodes on **November 1**.
- **Plot Highlights** – In the film, Kid the Phantom Thief pulls off a diamond heist using a real gun and a yakatabune houseboat.  Conan’s skateboard is split by Goemon Ishikawa XIII, and it is only then that Conan realizes Kid is actually Lupin the 3rd in disguise.
- **Discotek Releases** – Discotek released the film on Blu‑ray with an English dub featuring the classic Lupin cast and the newer Detective Conan theatrical cast.  A 2015 DVD release in Japanese with English subtitles followed.
- **Crossover History** – The film opened in Japanese theaters in December 2013, and a television special aired in 2009.  Discotek also released the special on home video with an English dub.
- **Curated Collections Timeline** – The first collection, *Conan vs. The Black Organization*, launched on Netflix and Crunchyroll in July 2025.  The second, *Rivals of the Great Detective*, began in November 2025, and the third, *FBI Intervention*, started on May 1.  Each collection introduced new English dubs by Studio Nano.
- **Franchise Roots** – Gosho Aoyama’s manga began in 1994, the anime debuted in January 1996, and Viz Media publishes the manga under the title *Case Closed*.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The crossover between *Lupin III* and *Detective Conan* is a rare event that has only happened twice in the franchise’s history.  Fans have long debated which of the two detectives would win in a showdown, and the film’s release has reignited that conversation.  The fact that the film will be available for free on YouTube—and in English—means that a global audience can finally see the clash without a paywall.

Moreover, Netflix’s new episode collection continues the trend of making classic *Conan* episodes more accessible.  The Studio Nano dub offers a fresh listening experience that differs from the older Bang Zoom! and Funimation dubs, giving long‑time viewers something new to appreciate.  The curated approach—grouping episodes by themes such as the Black Organization, rivals, and FBI interventions—provides a narrative arc that helps new fans dive in with context.

The combination of a free film and a new collection also taps into the nostalgia wave that has swept anime communities recently.  Older fans who grew up watching *Detective Conan* in the early 2000s and *Lupin III* in the 80s and 90s will appreciate the chance to revisit these classics, while younger viewers can experience the crossover for the first time.

## Background

*Detective Conan* (originally *Case Closed* in English) follows Shinichi Kudo, a high‑school detective who shrinks to a child’s size after being poisoned.  Under the alias Conan Edogawa, he works with his father Kogoro Mori and Ran to solve mysteries while secretly hunting the Black Organization that caused his condition.

The *Lupin III* franchise, created by Monkey Punch, centers on the gentleman thief Arsène Lupin III.  The 2009 television special and the 2013 theatrical film were the first times the two universes intersected, with Lupin infiltrating the *Conan* world and vice versa.  Both franchises have maintained a dedicated fanbase for decades, and their crossover is a testament to the enduring appeal of detective‑themed storytelling.

## What’s Next

While the immediate next steps are the film’s YouTube run and Netflix’s episode launch, the momentum suggests a few possibilities:

1. **Extended Streaming Window** – If the free YouTube stream proves popular, TMS might extend the availability or offer a paid version on other platforms.
2. **Additional Curated Collections** – Following the success of the first three collections, Netflix could curate more themed sets, potentially covering the 2009 television special or newer *Conan* arcs.
3. **New Cross‑Over Projects** – The partnership between the two franchises may inspire future collaborations, whether in anime, manga, or live‑action adaptations.
4. **Home‑Video Releases** – Discotek’s Blu‑ray release and the earlier DVD show a pattern of re‑issuing content for collectors, so a new limited‑edition set could appear in the coming months.

In any case, the current window is the perfect time for fans to experience the crossover that has been a dream for many.  The free YouTube stream and the fresh Netflix collection provide a rare convergence of accessibility, nostalgia, and new content.

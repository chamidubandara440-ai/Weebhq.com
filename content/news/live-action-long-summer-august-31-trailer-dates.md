# Live‑Action "The Long Summer of August 31" Drops Trailer, Cast, Disney+ Streaming – Jan. 8 Premiere

WeebHQ’s front page just lit up with the most urgent anime news of the week: the live‑action adaptation of Ikkado Ito’s beloved time‑loop manga *The Long Summer of August 31* has just unveiled a special trailer that not only showcases the cast and crew but also confirms that the series will stream on Disney+ immediately after its TV broadcast. With a premiere set for January 8 at 24:52 (effectively 12:52 a.m. on January 9), fans have a new reason to start counting down the days.

## What We Know

- **Cast**: Ryūga Satō (ACEes, *Tomodachi Game R4*, *The High School Heroes*) takes on the role of Takaya Suzuki, while Runa Takashima (known from *Tore!* and *Tokyo Taxi*) portrays Kana Takagi. Both actors bring a fresh energy that fans of the manga will recognize and appreciate.
- **Staff**: The series is directed by Tatsunori Satō, Kōji Ono, and Meru Tsukada. Scripts are written by Moral, and Moto Kawabe is on board as the composer. These names suggest a blend of seasoned experience and fresh creative vision.
- **Broadcast**: The show will air on TV Tokyo, TV Osaka, TV Aichi, TV Setouchi, TV Hokkaido, and TVQ on January 8 at 24:52, with a BS TV Tokyo broadcast following on January 11.
- **Streaming**: After each television episode airs, it will be available on Disney+ the same day. Producer Kanata Nakajima has hinted that the platform will likely host the series worldwide, a detail that has fans already planning their viewing schedules.
- **Manga background**: The manga debuted in Kodansha’s *Morning* magazine in 2023. By August 21 of that year, volume 13 had been released, and Seven Seas Entertainment published volume 7 on November 10. The story follows high‑schoolers caught in an endlessly repeating August 31, with Suzuki’s quest for a girlfriend and first experience driving the comedic tension.
- **Additional adaptations**: SHAFT is slated to produce a television anime adaptation, adding another layer of excitement for the franchise.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The combination of a live‑action format, a star‑studded cast, and Disney+ streaming is a rare trifecta that taps into multiple fan communities. Ryūga Satō’s recent popularity from *Tomodachi Game R4* and Runa Takashima’s rising profile in *Tore!* have already generated buzz on social media. The fact that the series will stream globally on Disney+—a platform that has been expanding its anime catalog—means that international viewers can jump in without waiting for a local broadcast. Finally, the time‑loop premise, which has become a favorite trope in recent anime and manga, promises a blend of humor and heart that resonates with both casual viewers and dedicated fans.

## Background

Ikkado Ito first introduced *The Long Summer of August 31* in 2023, quickly gaining a following for its witty take on the high‑school summer experience. The manga’s humor stems from the characters’ awareness of their endless loop and their attempts to break the cycle. The story’s central conflict—Suzuki’s unfinished summer mission to secure a girlfriend and lose his virginity—offers a relatable teenage goal that is both comedic and touching. The series has already seen significant sales, with volume 13 released in August and volume 7 available in English through Seven Seas Entertainment’s Ghost Ship imprint.

The adaptation process has been carefully handled. The official website for the live‑action series, as well as the series’ Twitter account, have been releasing teaser footage and key visuals that hint at the show’s tone. The inclusion of a staff trio—Tatsunori Satō, Kōji Ono, and Meru Tsukada—suggests a collaborative approach that could balance fidelity to the source material with fresh storytelling techniques.

## What's Next

While the trailer does not disclose specific plot details beyond the cast and staff, the confirmed release schedule allows fans to plan their viewing. The January 8 premiere means that the first episode will air at the very end of the day, a strategic slot that could capture late‑night viewers. Following the TV broadcast, Disney+ will make the episode available, potentially drawing in an international audience who may not have access to the Japanese networks.

Given the upcoming SHAFT anime adaptation, viewers can anticipate a broader exploration of the *Long Summer* universe. Fans might also look forward to potential cross‑media promotions, such as special manga releases or exclusive behind‑the‑scenes content on the official website. The collaboration with Seven Seas and Kodansha’s K MANGA service ensures that the manga remains accessible both in print and digitally, which could boost sales as the live‑action series gains traction.

# Natsume Ono Announces KORORE Spinoff of ACCA‑13 and BADON – New Manga Hits Big Gangan Feb 25

Anime fans, grab your bookmarks and set your calendars—there’s a brand‑new adventure in the Dowa Kingdom that’s about to hit the shelves on **February 25, 2027**. The legendary mangaka Natsume Ono, who brought us the beloved *ACCA‑13 Territory Inspection Department* and the gritty *BADON*, is launching a fresh spinoff titled **KORORE** in *Monthly Big Gangan*’s third issue of 2027. The news dropped from the official X/Twitter account of Square Enix’s *Monthly Big Gangan* and is already sending ripples through the anime community.

## What We Know

- **Title & Publication**: *KORORE* is a new manga spinoff of Ono’s *ACCA‑13 Territory Inspection Department* and *BADON*, scheduled to debut in *Monthly Big Gangan*’s third 2027 issue on **February 25, 2027**.
- **Setting**: Like *BADON*, *KORORE* is one of the 13 territories in the Dowa Kingdom, the shared world that began with *ACCA‑13‑Territory Inspection Department*.
- **Creator**: Natsume Ono, the prolific mangaka behind *House of Five Leaves*, *Ristorante Paradiso*, *Gente – Ristorante no Hitobito*, *Tesoro*, *Not Simple*, *Dan za*, *Lady and Oldman*, and *Have a Great Sunday*.
- **Historical Context**: *ACCA‑13 Territory Inspection Department* ran in *Monthly Big Gangan* from June 2013 to October 2016, spanning six compiled volumes. Yen Press released the manga in English, and it inspired a 2017 television anime (streamed by Crunchyroll) and a 2020 OVA sequel, *ACCA: 13‑Territory Inspection Dept. Regards*.
- **BADON Legacy**: The *BADON* manga launched in January 2019, concluded in March 2024, and saw its ninth volume released in April 2024. Square Enix published the series.
- **Other Works**: Ono’s debut was *La Quinta Camera – 5 Banme no Heya* (2003). Viz Media published her North American titles, while Kodansha USA handled *Dan za*. She also provided character designs for Shingo Natsume and Madhouse’s upcoming theatrical anime *Ghost – End of Night* (opening February 11) and directed the adaptation of *ACCA‑13*.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The announcement of *KORORE* feels like a seismic shift for the Dowa Kingdom fanbase. Fans of *ACCA‑13* and *BADON* have long speculated about deeper lore beyond the original 13 territories. With *KORORE* set to explore a new region, expectations are sky‑high. The fact that Ono, who has consistently delivered compelling world‑building and sharp character dynamics, is back on the page adds a layer of anticipation that’s hard to match.

Moreover, *Monthly Big Gangan* is celebrating its 15th anniversary with a flurry of new projects, including five fresh serials and a special giveaway of QO cards. *KORORE* is positioned at the heart of this celebratory wave, promising to tie into the magazine’s larger narrative push. For a community that thrives on interconnected stories, the addition of a new territory in the Dowa Kingdom is a golden ticket.

The excitement is not just about a new story; it’s about the continuation of a beloved universe. The *ACCA‑13* anime, which aired in 2017 and the 2020 OVA, garnered a cult following. The *BADON* series, running from 2019 to 2024, kept fans engaged with its gritty tone. *KORORE* is poised to blend the investigative grit of *ACCA‑13* with the darker atmosphere of *BADON*, offering a fresh yet familiar experience.

## Background

### The Dowa Kingdom and the 13 Territories

The Dowa Kingdom is a sprawling world that was first introduced through *ACCA‑13 Territory Inspection Department*. The story revolves around the 13 territories that make up the kingdom, each with its own culture, politics, and mysteries. The series delved into the bureaucratic and investigative aspects of the kingdom’s governance, providing a unique lens on both the mundane and the supernatural.

### Natsume Ono’s Career

Since her debut in 2003, Ono has carved out a niche for herself with a blend of culinary themes (*Ristorante Paradiso*), slice‑of‑life introspection (*House of Five Leaves*), and supernatural intrigue (*Badon*). Her works have not only been successful in Japan but have also found a dedicated following in North America, thanks to Viz Media’s translations. Her ability to create richly detailed worlds and complex characters makes her return with *KORORE* a highly anticipated event.

### Past Adaptations

*ACCA‑13* received a 2017 anime adaptation that was streamed by Crunchyroll, followed by a 2020 OVA sequel. *BADON* has also inspired an anime adaptation, further cementing the franchise’s cross‑media appeal. These adaptations have broadened the audience and set high expectations for any new material.

## What's Next

While the official announcement stops short of revealing plot details, several grounded speculations can be made:

1. **New Territory Spotlight**: *KORORE* will focus on a territory not previously explored in *ACCA‑13* or *BADON*, potentially expanding the geopolitical landscape of the Dowa Kingdom.
2. **Continuity with Existing Characters**: Given Ono’s history of weaving interconnected stories, it’s plausible that characters from *ACCA‑13* or *BADON* will make cameo appearances or serve as mentors to new protagonists.
3. **Thematic Shift**: The title *KORORE*—which in Japanese can mean “to be thin or lean”—might hint at a more streamlined narrative or a focus on the underbelly of the kingdom’s politics, similar to *BADON*’s gritty tone.
4. **Cross‑Media Potential**: With the success of previous anime adaptations, it’s reasonable to anticipate that *KORORE* could eventually receive its own anime series or OVA, especially if the manga performs well in *Monthly Big Gangan*.

The next few weeks will be critical: the first chapter’s release, fan reactions, and sales numbers will all shape the trajectory of *KORORE*. Keep an eye on *Monthly Big Gangan*’s February 2027 issue and the official X/Twitter feed for updates.

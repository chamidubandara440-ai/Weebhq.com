## Background

### The Ghost of Tsushima Legacy

**Ghost of Tsushima** began as a critically acclaimed action‑adventure game set in 1274 Japan, during the Mongol invasions.  The game’s narrative follows Jin Sakai, a samurai who must abandon his rigid code to become a “ghost” and protect his homeland.  Its success led to a dedicated fan base that craved more stories from that universe.

### From Game to Anime

The decision to adapt the game into an anime series was announced by Netflix and Sucker Punch Productions in early 2024.  The adaptation promised to explore side characters and subplots that the game only hinted at.  Initial teasers highlighted a blend of high‑quality animation and the game’s signature sword‑swinging action, generating anticipation among both anime purists and gamers.

### Production Timeline

The original schedule slated the series for a 2027 release, with a handful of cast and crew names revealed at the time.  The latest update pushes the premiere to 2028, a full year later.  While the exact reasons for the delay are not disclosed, it’s not uncommon for large‑scale anime projects—especially those tied to a major franchise—to adjust timelines to accommodate animation quality, licensing, or global distribution logistics.

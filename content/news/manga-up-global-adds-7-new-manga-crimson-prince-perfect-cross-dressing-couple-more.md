## Urgent! Manga UP! Global Just Dropped 7 Brand‑New Titles – Fans Are Already Flocking to the Platform
The anime community has a new reason to celebrate: Square Enix’s Manga UP! Global has just announced a massive influx of fresh manga, adding **seven** new titles to its English‑language catalog over the last ten days. From a demon prince’s high‑school escapades to a cross‑gender romance that turns heads, the lineup is as diverse as it is tantalizing. If you’ve been waiting for a fresh binge‑worthy series, now is the perfect moment to dive in.

## What We Know
Below is a quick‑look at each of the seven new releases, complete with creators, release dates, and a taste of the storylines that have fans talking.

| Title | Creator(s) | Release Date | Summary |
|-------|------------|--------------|---------|
| **Crimson Prince (Kurenai Ōji)** | Sōta Kuwahara | October 22 | Demon prince Koujirou Sakura lands on Earth, enrolls in high school, and searches for the human who will threaten the demon realm by stealing souls. With no clue about the target, he struggles to survive in a crowded boys’ dorm and ends up living off‑camp with a girl—an unexpected twist that sets the stage for chaos and comedy.
| **The Perfect Cross‑Dressing Couple (Josō Danshi to Dansō Joshi wa Oniai Couple)** | Mikaduki Lamp | October 23 | Shion, a handsome “girl” (actually male), and Yukari, a striking “guy” (actually female), form a dating duo that proves opposites attract. Their romantic comedy is a fresh take on gender roles and the chemistry that sparks when two contrasting personalities collide.
| **Not Quite Love (Koi to Yobu ni wa Sasayaka Desu ga)** | Daisuke Hagiwara | October 24 | High‑schooler Umi Sayama stays with the neighboring Hiroi family due to family circumstances. She reunites with Shoya, a childhood friend who has now become a homebody. Hagiwara’s story explores the blurry line between friendship and love in a relatable, slice‑of‑life setting.
| **The Evil Princess Will Kill No More (Aku no Kōjo wa Mō Dare mo Korosanai)** | Yakiimohokuhoku (author), Otyabomizu (artist) | October 26 | Candace, once a tyrannical “Demon Princess,” is executed and wakes up time‑traveled. Determined to avoid her fatal fate, she abandons her quest for love and adopts a completely different persona, showcasing a dramatic shift in character arc.
| **The Witch of Dawn (Yoiake no Majo)** | Sayuco Shingyōji | October 28 | Gwnrose, the “Witch of Dawn,” was reckless yet kind. After her death, her disciple Slei must partner with the “Witch’s Heart” left behind to search for the other thirteen witches, launching a dark‑fantasy adventure.
| **Reading Our Way to a Happy New World (Yomutabi ni Shiawase o!)** | Chac Choa | October 30 | In a post‑apocalyptic setting, Mekuri and Kiyoshi embark on a quest to collect and decipher all the manga they can, blending survival with the joy of reading in a world where stories become a lifeline.
| **My Spooky Storyteller Is an Adorable Klutz (Watashi no Kaidan‑shi wa Ponkotsu Kawaii: Kaidan Shūshū wa Date ni Fukumaremasu ka?)** | Ougi Ishikawa (story), kodamazon (character designer), Reiri (artist) | October 31 | Ao Shiroharu, a high‑school girl with supernatural gifts, meets Uta Natsume, a loner who wants to become a ghost‑storyteller. Their midnight shrine adventure turns unexpectedly perilous, setting the stage for spooky camaraderie.

## Why Fans Are Excited
The buzz surrounding these releases isn’t just about new content; it’s the unique blend of genres and fresh takes on familiar tropes that fans can’t ignore.

- **Demon Prince Meets High School**: The concept of a demon prince navigating the everyday challenges of high‑school life is a classic “isekai” mash‑up that promises humor, drama, and supernatural intrigue.
- **Cross‑Gender Romance**: With a growing appetite for LGBTQ+ representation, *The Perfect Cross‑Dressing Couple* offers a light‑hearted, gender‑fluid romance that resonates with audiences looking for stories that break conventional molds.
- **Slice‑of‑Life with a Twist**: *Not Quite Love* brings a familiar, relatable narrative—roommates, childhood friends, blurred emotions—yet the addition of a homebody protagonist adds a fresh comedic angle.
- **Time‑Travel Redemption**: The premise of a tyrant who must rewrite her destiny in *The Evil Princess Will Kill No More* taps into the enduring appeal of second‑chance narratives.
- **Dark Fantasy with a Mentor‑Disciple Bond**: The quest in *The Witch of Dawn* introduces a compelling mentor‑disciple dynamic, a staple in fantasy that fans love to see evolve.
- **Post‑Apocalyptic Manga Quest**: The idea that manga itself becomes a survival tool in *Reading Our Way to a Happy New World* is meta‑fun and invites speculation about the role of stories in rebuilding society.
- **Ghost‑Storytelling Duo**: *My Spooky Storyteller Is an Adorable Klutz* merges supernatural elements with the charm of an unlikely partnership, promising both chills and laughs.

Across the board, these titles showcase a blend of humor, romance, supernatural stakes, and heartfelt storytelling that aligns perfectly with current anime‑manga trends. The fact that all are available in English on Manga UP! Global makes them instantly accessible to a worldwide audience.

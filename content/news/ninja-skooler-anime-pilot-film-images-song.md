# Ninja Skooler Anime Drops Pilot Film, Images & Theme Song—A Must-See for Fans

The anime world is buzzing this week as **Kinema Citrus** finally releases the long‑anticipated pilot film for its original project *Ninja Skooler*.  The film, a playful slice‑of‑life take on the ninja genre, is accompanied by a set of crisp character images and the opening track *Knots*, performed by Takashi Iwami.  For fans who have been waiting since the project’s announcement at Anime Central in 2024, this is the moment that turns anticipation into reality.

## What We Know

* **Director & Production** – The project is helmed by **Shunsuke Takarai**, who will also supervise the animation.  The concept originates from **Kinema Citrus’s** Makoto Kimura and is developed through the company’s BLUE RIGHTS division.
* **Music** – The theme song *Knots* was written and performed by **Takashi Iwami** and was digitally released alongside the pilot.
* **Character Design** – The visual style is crafted by **Hami**, with background art also contributed by Hami and Shunsuke Takarai.
* **Key Animators** – The animation team includes **Yuka Kuroda**, **Franziska van Wulfen**, and **Shunsuke Takarai** himself.
* **Supporting Staff** – A large roster of animators, color artists, sound directors, and production managers are listed in the press release, underscoring the project’s robust production pipeline.
* **Plot Snapshot** – The story follows **Rappa**, an orphan who idolizes fictional ninjas.  After a snowy rooftop fall, he is rescued by a real ninja named **Okachimachi** and his master.  Rappa joins their household, and years later dedicates himself to becoming a ninja capable of protecting their town.  The narrative also touches on the hidden pain of the retired master, adding depth to the seemingly light‑hearted premise.
* **Release Context** – The anime was first announced by Kinema Citrus at the Anime Central event in Rosemont, IL, in May 2024.  This pilot film marks the first visual and auditory glimpse into the series.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The *Ninja Skooler* announcement has ignited a wave of enthusiasm across social media and fan forums.  The combination of a beloved studio, a fresh ninja concept, and a charismatic director has created a perfect storm of anticipation.  Fans are particularly thrilled by:

1. **The Light‑Hearted Tone** – In an era where many anime lean toward dark or high‑stakes drama, *Ninja Skooler* offers a breezy, family‑friendly atmosphere that feels like a breath of fresh air.
2. **The Relatable Protagonist** – Rappa’s journey from orphan to ninja aspirant resonates with viewers who appreciate stories of growth, mentorship, and community.
3. **The Visuals** – The pilot’s animation quality, coupled with Hami’s clean character designs, showcases a polished aesthetic that fans expect from Kinema Citrus.
4. **The Original Song** – *Knots* adds an emotional layer, and its digital release allows fans to immediately engage with the series’ musical identity.

The sheer breadth of the production team—spanning animators, sound directors, and international collaborators—has also sparked speculation that *Ninja Skooler* will be a high‑production‑value series.

## Background

Kinema Citrus is renowned for titles such as *Made in Abyss*, *The Seven Deadly Sins*, and *The Rising of the Shield Hero*.  Their reputation for blending imaginative world‑building with heartfelt storytelling sets high expectations for *Ninja Skooler*.  The studio’s partnership with the BLUE RIGHTS anime planning company, led by Makoto Kimura, signals a strategic move to diversify their portfolio with original content that can stand on its own.

Shunsuke Takarai, who will direct *Ninja Skooler*, brings a fresh perspective to the studio’s lineup.  While the press release does not detail his prior works, his role as both director and supervising animator suggests a hands‑on approach that could shape the series’ tone and pacing.

The story’s premise—a young orphan discovering the reality of ninjas and forming a new family—echoes classic themes of mentorship and self‑discovery, yet it does so within a comedic, slice‑of‑life framework that is rare in the ninja subgenre.

## What's Next

Based on the information released, the following can be reasonably anticipated:

1. **Series Announcement** – Following the pilot, Kinema Citrus will likely confirm a full‑series order, potentially scheduling a premiere in the 2027 anime season.
2. **Episode Count & Format** – While the pilot is a standalone piece, the studio may opt for a 12‑ or 24‑episode cour, depending on the reception and production timeline.
3. **Marketing Rollout** – Additional character images, behind‑the‑scenes footage, and soundtrack releases are expected to keep the buzz alive as the series approaches its official launch.
4. **Merchandising** – Given the family‑friendly tone, we can anticipate a range of merchandise such as figurines, apparel, and possibly a collaboration with the master’s traditional Japanese candy shop.

The production team’s extensive list of international staff, including Korean animators and Japanese sound engineers, hints at a global distribution strategy.  Therefore, viewers worldwide should be able to stream *Ninja Skooler* with subtitles or dubbing relatively soon after its official debut.

## Closing

The pilot film, images, and theme song for *Ninja Skooler* have finally landed in the hands of fans, turning months of speculation into tangible excitement.  With a talented director, a robust production crew, and a heartwarming premise, this anime is poised to carve a niche in the crowded ninja landscape.  Keep your eyes peeled for the official series announcement and get ready to follow Rappa, Okachimachi, and the master on their journey from rooftop rescues to town‑protecting adventures.  The ninja training has just begun, and it promises to be a fun, family‑friendly ride you won’t want to miss.

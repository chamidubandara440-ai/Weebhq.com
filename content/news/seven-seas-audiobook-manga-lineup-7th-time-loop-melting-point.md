## Urgent: Seven Seas Drops a Record‑Breaking Slate of Audiobooks and Manga – Fans Are in a Frenzy!

Just hours after the press release hit the web, the anime community exploded into a frenzy. Seven Seas Entertainment, the powerhouse behind so many beloved titles, announced a jaw‑dropping lineup that includes two new audiobooks, a brand‑new manga series, a manhwa omnibus, and a deep‑cut audio collection that promises fresh perspectives on familiar stories. With release dates ranging from this month to the next year, the buzz is real, and the clock is ticking for fans who want to be first to dive into these new worlds.

## What We Know

**Audiobook 1 – *7th Time Loop: The Villainess Enjoys a Carefree Life Married to Her Worst Enemy!***
- *Format:* Short story collection audiobook
- *Creators:* Tōko Amekawa & Wan Hachipisu
- *Narration:* Sarah Gage
- *Release Date:* November 19 (Siren imprint)
- *Synopsis:* The collection features dozens of side‑stories that explore tender moments between Arnold and Rishe, glimpses of overlooked characters, and a brand‑new longest story where the duo goes undercover as a married couple. Fans will get to see Rishe’s past lives from the perspectives of those close to her.

**Audiobook 2 – *The Dangers in My Heart – The Novel***
- *Creators:* Kota Nozomi, Norio Sakurai & sune
- *Narration:* Clint Bickham & Katelyn Barr
- *Release Date:* November 26 (Siren imprint)
- *Synopsis:* The narrative shifts focus to side characters—Moeko, Kanzaki, and Yamada Anna’s mother—providing a fresh lens on Ichikawa Kyotaro’s unrequited love story. An exclusive story covers the early summer of Ichikawa and Yamada’s relationship.

**Audiobook 3 – *Beacon of Light in the Dark Sea***
- *Creator:* Softcoral
- *Narration:* James Aaron Oh
- *Release Date:* December 17 (volume 1, Siren imprint)
- *Synopsis:* Set in the North Pacific International Undersea Station, the story follows dentist Muhyeon as he confronts the station’s eerie silence and crew’s unstable behavior. The narrative promises a blend of quirky and unsettling moments.

**Manga – *Led by Stars***
- *Creator:* Abi
- *Release Date:* November 2027 (volume 1, Ghost Ship imprint)
- *Synopsis:* In Elwood, Arc and Lily face a new threat: the “Goblin Queen.” Arc must decide whether to return to adventure to protect the town—and Lily, who is hiding a secret that could change everything.

**Manhwa – *Melting Point***
- *Creator:* Pibi
- *Release Date:* April 2028 (Season 1 omnibus, Seven Seas Webtoons)
- *Synopsis:* Former ice‑hockey star Eunsan, now in sports marketing, is pulled back into the spotlight when his rival Ryu Ian reappears with an irresistible offer. The story explores rekindled rivalry and old wounds.

## Background

Seven Seas Entertainment has long been a staple in the anime and manga community, known for its high‑quality translations and diverse catalog. The Siren imprint, in particular, has carved out a niche for audiobooks that bring beloved manga and light novels to life with professional narration. The Ghost Ship imprint focuses on original manga titles that push creative boundaries.

*7th Time Loop* and *The Dangers in My Heart* are both part of the *Siren* collection, a series that has previously included fan‑favourite titles like *The Rising of the Shield Hero* and *The Rising of the Shield Hero 2*. These new releases continue that tradition by offering deeper dives into side stories and alternate perspectives.

The manga *Led by Stars* marks a significant addition to the Ghost Ship line, positioning Seven Seas as a serious competitor in the original manga space. Meanwhile, *Melting Point* brings the label into the manhwa arena, tapping into the growing popularity of Korean comics in the West.

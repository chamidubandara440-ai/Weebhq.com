## Urgent News: Arc System Works Opens New Studio Takumi on November 2

Arc System Works, the studio behind fan‑favorites such as *Guilty Gear* and *BlazBlue*, has just announced a seismic shift in its development strategy. On Thursday, the company revealed that it will open a new Tokyo studio—named **Takumi**—on **November 2**. The move comes with a bold promise: to dramatically strengthen its development capabilities and deliver an “overwhelming sense of ultimate excitement” to players worldwide. The first project slated for Takumi is a new fighting game that has already been selected for Japan’s Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) “IP360” subsidy program, a program that supports the creation of cutting‑edge IPs.

This announcement is not just a corporate expansion; it signals a strategic pivot toward a more collaborative, globally‑oriented development model. Arc System Works described the studio as a “strategic cornerstone for going beyond existing conventional game development frameworks” and a hub that will connect creators across continents. In line with this vision, the company is also expanding its recruitment of creators with the opening of Takumi.

## What We Know

- **Studio Launch Date**: Takumi will officially open on **November 2**.
- **First Project**: A new fighting game that has been selected for the **IP360** subsidy program.
- **Strategic Goals**: Strengthen development capabilities, break free from conventional frameworks, and deliver ultimate excitement to players.
- **Collaboration Hub**: Takumi is envisioned as a global collaboration center.
- **Recruitment Expansion**: Arc System Works is hiring more creators to support the new studio.
- **Recent Releases**: 
  - *Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls* 4v4 tag‑team fighter launched on **PlayStation 5** and PC (Steam/Epic) on **August 6** (August 7 in Japan).
  - *Donutal* released on PC via Steam on **September 3** (September 4 in Japan).
  - *BlazBlue: CentralFiction* announced for **PlayStation 5** release on **October 10**.
  - The *Guilty Gear Strive* franchise inspired the television anime *Guilty Gear Strive: Dual Rulers*, which debuted in **April 2025**.

The addition of a new studio dedicated solely to fighting games signals that Arc System Works is committed to delivering fresh, high‑quality content to its audience.

## Background

Arc System Works has built its reputation on a handful of iconic franchises. *Guilty Gear* debuted in 1998 and has evolved into a complex, anime‑inspired series that balances fast gameplay with deep narrative. *BlazBlue*, launched in 2008, brought a more cinematic approach to the fighting‑game genre, featuring a large cast of characters and a branching storyline.

The studio has also proven adept at cross‑industry collaborations. The partnership with PlayStation Studios and Marvel Games on *Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls* demonstrates its ability to blend beloved IPs into a new fighting‑game context. The recent release of *Donutal* and the announced *BlazBlue: CentralFiction* PS5 launch further illustrate the studio’s commitment to expanding its reach across platforms.

The IP360 subsidy program, part of Japan’s Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) initiative, is designed to support the development of innovative intellectual properties. By securing this subsidy, Arc System Works has access to resources that can accelerate development timelines, enhance technical capabilities, and foster creative risk‑taking.

Hey there, manga enthusiasts! Weeb here, bringing you some exciting news fresh off the press. If you've been craving a brand-new supernatural story to add to your weekly reading list, you are in for an absolute treat this week.

Kazu Kakazu, the talented manga creator known for *Our Blood Oath*, is officially making a grand return with a brand-new manga series titled ***Yosuga Tagurite***.

### A Brand-New Supernatural Journey

This upcoming series centers around a half-*yōkai* boy. While further details about his journey remain to be seen, fans of Kakazu’s previous work can certainly look forward to the creator's signature storytelling style and supernatural themes.

### Launching on Jump Toon

In a fantastic update for modern readers who prefer scrolling on their mobile devices, *Yosuga Tagurite* will be launching on the vertical-scrolling service **Jump Toon**. This format is perfect for delivering dynamic, fast-paced action sequences directly to your screen.

Mark your calendars, because *Yosuga Tagurite* is officially scheduled to make its big debut this coming Friday!

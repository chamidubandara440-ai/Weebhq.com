**Just in:** Haruka Kawachi’s heart‑warming *Rainy Day Serenade* ( *Namidaame to Serenade* ) is getting a brand‑new side‑story manga that will launch in Kodansha’s *Kiss* magazine on **November 25**.  Fans of the original series are already buzzing as the spinoff, titled *Namidaame to Serenade : Gaiden* (*Rainy Day Serenade Side Story*), promises fresh adventures featuring the beloved protagonist Kikunoshin.

## What We Know

- **Spinoff Title & Launch Date** – *Namidaame to Serenade : Gaiden* will appear in the *Kiss* magazine’s next issue, released on **November 25**.
- **Plot Hook** – The new story follows a heartbroken Kikunoshin as he encounters a sudden commotion at the Fuji Sakura café, a spot he frequents.
- **Original Series Timeline** – Kawachi began *Rainy Day Serenade* in *Kiss* in **2014** and wrapped it up in **November 2025**.  The 14th and final compiled volume was shipped in **February**.
- **International Licensing** – Kodansha USA Publishing has licensed the manga and is releasing it in two‑in‑one omnibus volumes.
- **Future Adaptations** – An anime adaptation of the original series is slated for **2027**.
- **Other Works by Kawachi** – Kawachi’s *Musashino Rondo* debuted in **2020** (first compiled volume in September 2021, fifth in July 2025) and inspired a live‑action series in April 2025.  *Natsuyuki Rendezvous* received an anime adaptation on Fuji TV’s *Noitamina* block in 2012, the same year the manga ended.  *Request o Yoroshiku* launched in September 2014, ended in 2019, with its fifth volume published in 2020.  *Sekine’s Love* was published in English by JManga.

## Background

Haruka Kawachi has built a reputation for crafting relatable, character‑driven stories that blend everyday life with gentle romance.  *Rainy Day Serenade* began in *Kiss* magazine in 2014 and quickly became a fan favorite for its realistic portrayal of heartbreak and healing.  The series ran for 14 volumes, concluding in November 2025, and was followed by a two‑in‑one omnibus release by Kodansha USA.

Kawachi’s other works further illustrate his versatility.  *Musashino Rondo* (2020) not only achieved a live‑action adaptation but also demonstrated his ability to weave musical themes into narrative arcs.  *Natsuyuki Rendezvous* and *Request o Yoroshiku* showcased his knack for blending humor with emotional depth, earning anime adaptations and solid fan followings.

The *Kiss* magazine platform has consistently supported Kawachi’s projects, providing a dedicated readership that appreciates the nuanced storytelling found in his series.  The spinoff’s placement in the December issue underscores the magazine’s commitment to delivering new content to its audience.

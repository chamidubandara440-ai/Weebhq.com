## Background

*Ranma ½* is a beloved anime that originally aired in the early 1990s and was adapted from Rumiko Takahashi’s manga. The series follows the martial artist Ranma Saotome, who transforms into a girl when splashed with cold water and back into a boy with hot water. The show is known for its blend of action, romance, and comedy, as well as its satirical take on gender roles and societal norms.

The **Kuno siblings**, Kodachi and Tatewaki, are a pair of eccentric relatives who live in a massive mansion. They are infamous for their over-the-top antics, obsessive behavior, and the bizarre household that serves as a backdrop for many of the series’ most memorable moments. Their dynamic—Kodachi’s manipulative schemes and Tatewaki’s naïve, almost childlike desire—provides a recurring source of humor and plot twists.

Season 3, which is now streaming on Netflix, continues to build on the series’ established themes while introducing new visual gags and character interactions. Episode 6, in particular, showcases the Kuno siblings’ signature blend of **dark humor and physical comedy**, pushing the boundaries of what fans have come to expect from *Ranma ½*.

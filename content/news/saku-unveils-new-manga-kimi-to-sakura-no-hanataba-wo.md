# Saku Unveils New Manga 'Kimi to Sakura no Hanataba wo' on Magazine Pocket – Launching Nov 1

In a whirlwind announcement that has already set the WeebHQ community abuzz, renowned artist **saku**—the creative force behind the hit *Yome ga Kuru!*—has revealed that he will be launching a brand‑new series titled **Kimi to Sakura no Hanataba wo** (A Bouquet of Cherry Blossoms and You) on Kodansha’s popular digital platform **Magazine Pocket**. The debut is slated for **November 1**, giving fans just over a month to gear up for what promises to be a fresh chapter in saku’s storied career.

## What We Know

- **Title & Platform**: *Kimi to Sakura no Hanataba wo* will launch on **Magazine Pocket**.
- **Release Date**: The series will debut on **November 1**.
- **Creator**: The manga is written and illustrated by **saku**, who previously collaborated with Yasunori Mitsunaga on *Yome ga Kuru!*.
- **Previous Works**: saku’s portfolio includes *Betsusekai no Kirakira Dōki ga Ecchi na Illust o Kaiteiru* (2021), *Senpai! Waza to Janain Desu!* (2022), *Kimi ni Koisuru San Shimai* (2023), and *Boku o Shiritasugiru Shimizu-san* (April 2024).
- **Yome ga Kuru! Milestones**: The *Yome ga Kuru!* manga debuted in Shueisha’s *Grand Jump Mucha* in April 2025, with the second compiled volume shipped on September 17.
- **Image Credit**: The teaser image for the new series was shared via Magazine Pocket’s X/Twitter account and is credited to **saku** and **Kodansha Ltd.**

These facts were confirmed by Magazine Pocket’s official X/Twitter account, which posted the announcement on Saturday, October 27.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The excitement surrounding *Kimi to Sakura no Hanataba wo* stems from several key factors that resonate deeply within the anime and manga community:

1. **Saku’s Proven Track Record**: Fans of *Yome ga Kuru!* have already seen how saku blends romantic storytelling with engaging art. His previous works showcase a versatility that promises a fresh yet familiar experience.
2. **Romantic Themes**: The title itself—*A Bouquet of Cherry Blossoms and You*—evokes classic Japanese romance tropes that many readers have come to love. The cherry blossom motif is a recurring symbol in anime and manga, hinting at a narrative rich in emotional depth.
3. **Digital Platform Momentum**: Magazine Pocket has become a go-to destination for serialized manga, offering instant access to new chapters. The platform’s growing user base means the series will reach a wide audience right from day one.
4. **Community Buzz**: Social media threads, especially on X/Twitter, have already exploded with speculation. Fans are debating potential story arcs, character dynamics, and how this series will compare to saku’s previous romance-heavy works.
5. **Strategic Timing**: Launching in early November positions the manga to capture the fall reading season, a period when many readers are actively seeking new stories.

Together, these elements create a perfect storm of anticipation that is palpable across fan forums, Reddit threads, and Discord servers.

## Background

### Saku’s Rise to Stardom

Saku’s journey began in 2021 with *Betsusekai no Kirakira Dōki ga Ecchi na Illust o Kaiteiru*, a title that showcased his distinctive illustration style and knack for storytelling. Over the next few years, he continued to refine his craft with titles like *Senpai! Waza to Janain Desu!* (2022) and *Kimi ni Koisuru San Shimai* (2023), each building a loyal readership.

In April 2024, saku released *Boku o Shiritasugiru Shimizu-san*, further cementing his reputation as a versatile mangaka capable of handling both comedic and heartfelt narratives. The culmination of these efforts was the collaboration with Yasunori Mitsunaga on *Yome ga Kuru!*—a series that debuted in Shueisha’s *Grand Jump Mucha* in April 2025. The success of the first volume, and the subsequent shipment of the second volume on September 17, demonstrated saku’s ability to maintain momentum in a competitive market.

### Magazine Pocket’s Role

Magazine Pocket, operated by Kodansha, has rapidly become a hub for serialized manga, offering readers instant access to new chapters across a wide range of genres. The platform’s partnership with saku for *Kimi to Sakura no Hanataba wo* signals Kodansha’s confidence in his drawing power and storytelling appeal.

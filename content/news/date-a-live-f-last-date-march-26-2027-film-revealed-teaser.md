# Date A Live F Last Date Sets March 26, 2027 Release: Final Film Revealed in Teaser

The Date A Live universe is about to hit its ultimate crescendo. During the Fantasia Bunko Grand Thanksgiving Festival 2026 Online, the franchise’s newest project was unveiled: **Date A Live F Last Date**, the definitive conclusion to the anime saga. The film will debut in Japanese theaters on **March 26, 2027**, and a teaser trailer has already dropped, leaving fans buzzing across every fan forum and social media platform.

## What We Know

- **Format & Release**: The project is a **feature‑length anime film** that serves as the *concluding arc* for the series. It is scheduled to open in Japan on **March 26, 2027**.
- **Teaser Trailer**: A short teaser video was released during the festival, showcasing the film’s visual style and hinting at the emotional stakes.
- **Staff & Voice Cast**: The film brings back key personnel from the latest seasons:
  - *Scriptwriter*: **Fumihiko Shimo** (formerly of Date A Live IV & V)
  - *Character Designer*: **Naoto Nakamura** (also from IV & V)
  - *Director*: **Junji Nishimura** (known for *Vladlove*, *Extreme Hearts*, *Mechanical Marie*)
  - *Voice Actors*: **Nobunaga Shimazaki, Marina Inoue, Misuzu Togashi, Ayana Taketatsu, Iori Nomizu, Asami Sanada, Maaya Uchida, Sarah Emi Bridcutt, Minori Chihara, Ayumi Mano, Hitomi Nabatame, Akari Kageyama, Aya Endō** all return to reprise their beloved roles.
- **Distributor**: **ANIMEC** will handle the theatrical release.
- **Production**: The film is being produced at **Fugaku**, with the creative team announced during the festival.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The announcement hit fans hard for several reasons:

1. **A Final Chapter** – After more than a decade of light novels, anime seasons, and spin‑offs, the franchise finally offers a *complete* narrative arc in film form. The sense of closure is palpable.
2. **Star‑Studded Return** – The return of beloved voice actors and key staff from the latest seasons ensures continuity in tone and character development, which is a major draw for the community.
3. **High‑Profile Director** – Junji Nishimura’s involvement signals a potentially darker, more sophisticated visual treatment, a trend that fans have been craving since the *Mayuri Judgment* film.
4. **Teaser Impact** – The teaser’s cinematic quality and the emotional weight of the scenes have sparked countless fan theories and speculation about how the story will culminate.
5. **Community Momentum** – The announcement came during the Fantasia Bunko festival, a gathering that already brings together the fanbase. The synergy between the event and the film’s reveal amplified excitement.

## Background

The **Date A Live** franchise began as a light novel series by **Koushi Tachibana** in March 2011, illustrated by Tsunako. Kadokawa’s Fantasia Bunko imprint released 22 volumes, with the final volume hitting shelves in March 2020. The novels have spawned manga adaptations, video games, and several anime installments:

- **Season 1** (2013) – 12 episodes, streamed by Crunchyroll.
- **Season 2** (2014) – 10 episodes, also on Crunchyroll.
- **Film: Gekijōban Date A Live: Mayuri Judgment** – Opened in Japan in August 2015.
- **Season 3** (2019) – 12 episodes, streamed by Crunchyroll.
- **Season 4** (2022) – 12 episodes, streamed by Crunchyroll.
- **Spinoff** – *Date A Live Fragment: Date A Bullet* led to a two‑part theatrical adaptation in 2020.
- **Other Projects** – Tachibana and Tsunako’s *King’s Proposal* (Ōsama no Propose) is currently in anime development.

Yen Press has released the novels in English, and Crunchyroll has provided simulcasts and English dubs for the anime, ensuring a global fanbase.

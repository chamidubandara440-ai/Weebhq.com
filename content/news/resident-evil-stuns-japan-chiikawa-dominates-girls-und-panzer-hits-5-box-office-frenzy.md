## Urgent Opening: The Box‑Office Storm Hits Japan Now
The Japanese cinema screens are buzzing with a three‑fold surge that is rewriting the summer’s box‑office narrative. On a single weekend, the live‑action reboot of **Resident Evil** stormed to **#2** in Japan, **Chiikawa** reclaimed the coveted **#1** spot for a record‑setting 12th week, and the **fifth installment of *Girls und Panzer das Finale*** slipped into **#5**. Fans and industry insiders alike are scrambling to decode why these titles are tearing through the charts, and why the anime‑film community is on a collective high‑octane. The numbers are in, and the stakes have never been higher.

## What We Know

### Resident Evil (Live‑Action)
- **Opening day**: 356,000 tickets sold, 626,567,150 ¥ (~US$3.97 M) in first three days.
- **First four days** (including Monday, Oct 12 holiday): 470,000 tickets, 813 M ¥ (~US$5.15 M).
- **Release dates**: Japan – Oct 9; United States – Sep 18.
- **US opening**: #1 spot, ~US$60 M.
- **Cast**: Austin Abrams as Bryan; Zach Cherry, Kali Reis, Paul Walter Hauser.
- **Director**: Zach Cregger.
- **Production**: Sony Pictures Digital Productions.

### Chiikawa – *Eiga Chiikawa: Ningyo no Shima no Himitsu*
- **12th weekend**: Re‑ascended from #4 to #1.
- **Friday‑Sunday**: 375,000 tickets, 562,647,900 ¥ (~US$3.56 M).
- **Cumulative**: 12.29 M tickets, 17,807,643,600 ¥ (~US$112 M).
- **Year‑to‑date**: Biggest film in Japan this year, 12th all‑time.
- **Opening**: Jul 24, #1 opening weekend, 990 M ¥ (~US$6.05 M) on day one.
- **First 3 days**: 1,508,500 tickets, 2,240,333,500 ¥ (~US$13.70 M).
- **Special screenings**: 4DX (Sep 5), cheering (Sep 11), Penlight (Sep 23).
- **Source**: Nagano’s manga arc “Siren/Island Arc”; Nagano wrote script; Kei Oikawa directed; QTORY inc. managed production; TOHO distributed.

### Girls und Panzer das Finale – Film 5
- **Opening**: Oct 9, #5 in Japan.
- **First 3 days**: 267,301,400 ¥ (~US$1.69 M).
- **Screening formats**: Dolby Cinema, Dolby Atmos, 4DX, MX4D – first time the series uses Dolby Cinema.
- **Plot**: After Ōarai Girls High’s semi‑final win, they face St. Gloriana Girls’ College, led by Darjeeling; Alice from St. Gloriana seeks a rematch with Miho.

### Star Detective Precure! – *Fushigi na Niwa to Futari no Himitsu*
- **Opening**: Sep 18.
- **Fourth weekend**: Dropped from #5 to #9.
- **First 3 days**: 358,000 tickets, 446 M ¥ (~US$2.82 M).
- **First 6 days (until Sep 23)**: 606,000 tickets, 746 M ¥ (~US$4.71 M).
- **Key crew**: Director Hanako Ueda; scriptwriter Yoshimi Narita; music by Erika Fukasawa & Misaki Umase; character design by Emiko Miyamoto.
- **Voice cast**: Akari Kitō (Karin), Nobunaga Shimazaki (Alan), Kōki Uchiyama (Rain).

## Why Fans Are Excited
The live‑action **Resident Evil** surge has fans buzzing because it’s a rare crossover: a video‑game‑based horror franchise now commanding mainstream cinema in Japan, matching the success it already enjoyed in the U.S. The fact that it’s pulling in 470,000 tickets in just four days—while still a national holiday—signals a broader appetite for high‑budget, franchise‑driven blockbusters.

Chiikawa’s return to **#1** after a 12‑week reign is a cultural moment. The series, rooted in a beloved manga, has become a generational touchstone. Fans celebrate its record‑breaking cumulative earnings and the innovative 4DX, cheering, and Penlight screenings that keep the community engaged beyond the screen. The film’s ability to sustain momentum for so long is a testament to its universal charm and the power of nostalgia.

For the *Girls und Panzer* community, the shift to **Dolby Cinema** is a game‑changer. The franchise’s die‑hard fans have long demanded higher‑fidelity experiences, and the first Dolby‑Cinema screenings fulfill that wish. The #5 ranking, while modest compared to Chiikawa’s dominance, still reflects strong interest in a series that blends mecha, school life, and competitive tank battles.

Star Detective Precure!’s steady performance keeps the *Precure* fanbase engaged. Even as it slipped to #9, the film’s early earnings and the involvement of high‑profile creators like Hanako Ueda and Yoshimi Narita keep the community excited for future installments.

## Background
- **Resident Evil**: A long‑running survival horror game series by Capcom, now re‑imagined as a live‑action film by Sony. The franchise’s cinematic adaptation has drawn attention from both gaming and film audiences.
- **Chiikawa**: Originating from Nagano’s manga, the anime adaptation has become a cultural phenomenon in Japan, known for its cute characters and heartwarming storytelling.
- **Girls und Panzer das Finale**: The sixth‑part film series continues the story of tank‑driving girls, expanding into new cinematic formats and deepening character arcs.
- **Star Detective Precure!**: The 23rd main anime entry in the *Precure* franchise, blending detective mystery with magical girl tropes.

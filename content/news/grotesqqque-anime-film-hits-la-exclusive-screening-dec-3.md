# Grotesqqque Anime Film Hits LA: One‑Week Exclusive Screening on Dec 3!

The animation world is buzzing, and for good reason: **Atsushi Nishigori’s omnibus masterpiece, *Grotesqqque* (formerly *Grotesque*), is finally landing in Los Angeles for a one‑week run that starts on **December 3**.  The Alamo Drafthouse Cinema downtown is the only theater in the U.S. to show the three‑segment film, and tickets are already selling fast on the theater’s website.  For anime fans who crave high‑quality storytelling, this is a once‑in‑a‑lifetime chance to experience a work that has already carved out a niche on the international stage.

## What We Know

- **Film & Venue**: *Grotesqqque* is an omnibus anime film consisting of three distinct stories—**"A(E)liens," "Yoroshiku★Girl" (also known as "4649 Girl"), and **"Nocturne: Nuit Grotesque"**—screened at the Alamo Drafthouse Cinema in Downtown Los Angeles for **one week only** starting December 3.
- **Ticketing**: Tickets are on sale via the theater’s website.  Each ticket includes a **free, randomly distributed illustration card** featuring exclusive art by the character designers from each segment.
- **Director & Production**: Atsushi Nishigori not only directed the film but also wrote the screenplay and designed the characters.  The project is a JOEN collaboration, a joint venture between Aniplex (Sony’s media subsidiary), CloverWorks, Wit Studio, and Shueisha.
- **Music**: The opening theme, **"Shoot Shoot Shoot,"** is performed by METALVERSE, while the ending theme, performed by Haoto, carries lyrics by momosu momosu.
- **Cast Highlights**:
  - *A(E)liens*: Kana Ichinose (Ellie), Hoshino Kento (Kento Kurikura), Akio Ōtsuka (Space Agency Bureau Director)
  - *Yoroshiku★Girl*: Sara Minami (Stella), Ikumi Hasegawa (Unknown), Saori Hayami (Hikarin), Maaya Uchida (Gorgeous Mika), Chika Anzai (Sword), Miyu Tomita (Chiffon), Rumi Ōkubo (Papico), Rie Kugimiya (Poyomi)
  - *Nocturne: Nuit Grotesque*: Nagi Inoue (Ayame), Aruno Nakanishi (Shion), Miku Ichinose (Momo), Nao Tomisato (Canna)
- **Soundtrack Contributors**: 35 musical acts contribute across the segments, ranging from Mika Ichinose and VaVa to the likes of Natsukawa, Yuyoyuppe, and Reol.
- **Release & Reception**: The film opened in Japan on November 6, ranking #8 in the box office and earning ¥47,185,800 (~US$297,000) in the first three days.  The *Yoroshiku★Girl* segment was streamed on Netflix in Japan from October 23 to November 5.
- **Premieres**: *Grotesqqque* had its world premiere at the Fantasia International Film Festival in Montreal (July 16‑August 2) and a U.S. premiere in collaboration with CloverWorks and Aniplex of America at the Japan Society in New York City on October 8, where Director Nishigori gave a pre‑screening introduction.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The hype around *Grotesqqque* isn’t just about the film’s artistic pedigree—though that alone would be enough to ignite fan enthusiasm.  Fans are thrilled by several unique aspects:

1. **Exclusive U.S. Screening**: The Alamo Drafthouse is the sole U.S. venue to showcase the film, making it a rare treat for American anime lovers.  The one‑week exclusivity heightens the urgency—miss it, and you’ll have to wait for a potential future release.

2. **Free Illustration Cards**: Each ticket comes with a randomly distributed illustration card featuring original art by the character designers of the three stories.  For collectors, this adds tangible value beyond the cinematic experience.

3. **Star‑Studded Cast & Music**: With voice actors such as Maaya Uchida, Rie Kugimiya, and the members of Nogizaka46, and music from METALVERSE and Haoto, the film promises a crossover of top talent that resonates with a wide demographic.

4. **CloverWorks & JOEN Legacy**: The film is a JOEN product—an initiative that brings together Aniplex, CloverWorks, Wit Studio, and Shueisha.  Fans of CloverWorks’ recent successes (e.g., *The Rising of the Shield Hero*) are eager to see how *Grotesqqque* expands the studio’s creative reach.

5. **Narrative Variety**: Each segment offers a distinct worldview—from the speculative *A(E)liens* to the whimsical *Yoroshiku★Girl* and the gothic *Nocturne: Nuit Grotesque*.  This variety appeals to fans who enjoy genre‑blending storytelling.

## Background

*Atsushi Nishigori* has been building a reputation as an auteur capable of blending surreal visuals with complex narratives.  His previous works have garnered critical acclaim, and *Grotesqqque* is his first major feature-length project.  The film’s three stories were carefully selected to showcase different facets of his directorial style, each accompanied by a unique musical score that complements the narrative tone.

The collaboration with **JOEN**—a joint venture between Aniplex, CloverWorks, Wit Studio, and Shueisha—underscores the industry’s push toward cross‑studio partnerships.  This partnership has already produced a string of high‑profile projects, and *Grotesqqque* is positioned as a flagship title for the consortium.

- **Digital Release**: Given the film’s prior Netflix streaming of *Yoroshiku★Girl* in Japan, a domestic digital release (either through a streaming platform or a home‑video launch) is a plausible future path.

- **Potential Sequels or Spin‑Offs**: The success of each segment could inspire individual spin‑offs or even a sequel anthology, especially if the audience responds positively to the distinct storytelling styles.

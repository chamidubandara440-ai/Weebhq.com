## Manga Up! Global Grows Its Catalogue with Five New Titles

Manga Up! Global has announced a significant expansion of its digital library. The service is introducing five new manga titles for readers worldwide.

## New Titles

The lineup includes **Magical Girl Recruiter Puicho!** and **Bitter Knight in My Sweet Cafe**, offering a mix of magical girl action and slice-of-life romance for fans of the genre.

## About Manga Up! Global

Manga Up! Global is Square Enix's international digital manga service, providing readers worldwide access to a growing catalogue of manga titles across various genres.

## Urgent: The One‑Punch Man Universe Expands in 2027!
The anime world just got a massive power‑up. Bandai Namco Filmworks has revealed, via a brand‑new trailer, that the second cours of **One‑Punch Man Season 3** will launch in **January 2027**. The news is fresh, the visuals are electrifying, and the music lineup—JAM Project’s return—has fans buzzing. If you thought the series was finished, think again. The next wave of heroics is coming, and it’s set to hit every major streaming platform worldwide. This is the moment every One‑Punch Man fan has been waiting for.

## What We Know
- **Release Window**: The second cours of Season 3 premieres in **January 2027**.
- **Opening Theme**: JAM Project returns to perform the new opening, titled **"Never Done – SUPREME POWER VS ULTIMATE FEAR –."**
- **Trailer & Key Visual**: A fresh trailer featuring the new key visual was released Sunday; the English‑subtitled version is streaming on Viz Media’s channel.
- **Production Team**:
  - **Studio**: J.C. Staff (returning from Season 2) animates the series; Madhouse handled Season 1.
  - **Director**: Shinpei Nagai.
  - **Script Supervisor**: Tomohiro Suzuki.
  - **Character Designers**: Chikashi Kubota (returning) with Shinjiro Kuroda and Ryōsuke Shirakawa.
  - **Art Direction**: Sakura Murakami.
  - **Color Design**: Mayumi Tanahashi.
  - **Director of Photography**: Yuki Hirose.
  - **Editing**: Masahiro Gotō.
  - **Sound Direction**: Yoshikazu Iwanami.
  - **Music Composition**: Makoto Miyazaki.
- **Streaming Platforms**:
  - Hulu (U.S.)
  - Disney+ (Canada)
  - Netflix (Latin America, Australia, New Zealand)
  - Crunchyroll (Latin America & Oceania)
- **Previous Music**: The first cours featured JAM Project with BABYMETAL performing **"Get No Satisfied!"** and Makoto Furukawa singing the ending **"Soko ni Aru Akari."**
- **Season Timeline**: Season 3’s recap aired on **October 5, 2025**; the 25th episode, the first episode of the third season, aired a week later.
- **Historical Context**: The first season ran in Japan from October to December 2015; the second season kicked off with a television special in April 2019, followed by the first episode a week later.

## Background
**One‑Punch Man** began as a webcomic by ONE and later evolved into a manga illustrated by Yūsuke Murata. The anime adaptation captured the world’s imagination with its blend of comedic absurdity and intense action. Season 1 aired in 2015, establishing a massive fanbase. Season 2, launched in 2019, was produced by Madhouse and brought higher production values. J.C. Staff took over for Season 3, promising to maintain the series’ signature style while delivering polished animation.

The franchise’s music has always been a highlight. JAM Project’s “Get No Satisfied!” and BABYMETAL’s collaboration set a precedent for high‑energy openings. The return of these artists for Season 3’s second cours is a nod to the series’ legacy and a promise of more explosive music.

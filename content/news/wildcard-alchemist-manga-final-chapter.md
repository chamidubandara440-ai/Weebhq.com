The moment the final chapter of *Wildcard Alchemist: Building the Ultimate Guild With a Divine Fluffy Beast and Random Alchemy Skill* hit Manga UP! Global’s servers this Sunday, the anime‑manga community erupted.  Fans who have followed Iluto’s journey from a dismissed alchemist to a worldwide sensation are witnessing the series’ grand finale, and the excitement is palpable.

## What We Know

* The third part of the final chapter was released on Sunday, 20 October 2026, on Manga UP! Global’s digital platform.
* The English‑language version is published by Manga UP! Global, the same service that first debuted the series in April 2025.
* The story follows Iluto, an alchemist kicked out of the guild Dragnate, who encounters Noir, a fluffy divine beast that grants him the “Random Alchemy” skill.  Iluto then assembles a dream squad, including a beautiful elf girl, to build the ultimate guild.
* Skyfarm is credited as the writer, Tentashi as the artist, and Dr. Mine for composition.
* Square Enix’s Manga UP! service published the first compiled volume in September 2025.
* The fourth and final volume will be released on November 6, 2026.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The announcement of the final chapter’s release has sent waves through the community.  For many, the series has been a blend of quirky humor and unexpected depth: a guild that throws out a “junk‑making” alchemist, a divine beast coveted by nations, and a skill that can turn the tide of any battle.  The stakes are high, and the climax promises to tie together the series’ many threads.

Social media chatter shows a mix of anticipation and nostalgia.  Fans have been speculating about how Iluto’s “Random Alchemy” will be used in the final showdown, and whether Noir’s divine nature will play a pivotal role.  The fact that the final chapter is split into three parts adds a layer of urgency—each part feels like a mini‑episode, keeping readers on the edge of their seats.

The community’s engagement is also fueled by the series’ unique blend of fantasy and satire.  The guild’s internal politics, the elf’s friendly personality, and Iluto’s relentless ambition make for a narrative that feels both familiar and fresh.

## Background

*Wildcard Alchemist* debuted on the Manga UP! service in April 2025, quickly gaining a dedicated fanbase.  Skyfarm’s writing—known for its sharp wit and layered storytelling—paired with Tentashi’s dynamic artwork, created a series that stands out in a crowded market.  The inclusion of Dr. Mine for composition added a polished structure to the narrative.

Square Enix’s involvement has been significant.  The first compiled volume hit shelves in September 2025, and the publisher has promised a high‑quality final volume on November 6.  The partnership with Manga UP! Global ensures that English‑speaking audiences receive the story in a timely and accessible format.

The premise—an alchemist’s journey from exile to glory—resonates with classic tropes while subverting expectations.  Iluto’s “Random Alchemy” skill, combined with Noir’s divine status, sets the stage for epic battles and surprising alliances.  The guild’s collapse and Iluto’s rise mirror the classic hero’s journey, but with a comedic twist that keeps readers hooked.

## What's Next

While the final chapter has already been published, the series’ conclusion on November 6 will cement its legacy.  Fans can expect the final volume to contain the complete, polished version of the last chapter, possibly with additional artwork or author notes.

Given the series’ popularity, it’s likely that Square Enix will consider a light novel adaptation or an anime adaptation in the future.  However, any such plans remain speculative, as no official announcements have been made.

For now, the focus is on savoring the final pages.  Readers are encouraged to revisit earlier volumes to catch subtle foreshadowing and to appreciate the character development that has built up to this moment.

## Closing

The release of the third part of *Wildcard Alchemist*’s final chapter marks a pivotal moment for fans and the manga industry alike.  With its blend of humor, fantasy, and heartfelt storytelling, the series has carved out a unique space.  As the final volume drops on November 6, the community will have one last chance to celebrate Iluto’s triumph and the enduring appeal of a guild that once thought it could discard a “junk‑making” alchemist.

The countdown is on—don’t miss the final chapter, and get ready to close the book on one of 2026’s most talked‑about manga stories.

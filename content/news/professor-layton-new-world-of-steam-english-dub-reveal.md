Professor Layton and The New World of Steam has just dropped a bombshell: an English dub will accompany the title’s upcoming launch on December 10. Fans who have been waiting for a full‑voice experience are in for a treat as the franchise’s beloved leads return to their roles, and a fresh set of English voices bring new characters to life. This announcement comes at a time when the series is already generating buzz with a planned 2027 remake of Professor Layton and the Curious Village and the release of a brand‑new title that expands the universe beyond the DS roots.

## What We Know

- The game’s official website has posted voice clips revealing an English dub. Christopher Robin Miller reprises his role as Professor Layton, while Lani Minella returns as Luke. New English cast members include Amber Lee Connors as Elinora, Elida Nic Olsen as Eggmuffin, Andy Barnett as Bolt Allinston, Caleb Jensen as Sheriff Bobsley, Arthur Romeo as Falcon, and Kayli Mills as Mystella.
- The release date is set for December 10 worldwide. The title will be available on Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam.
- Mouse support is available on all versions except the Switch.
- The story takes place after the events of Professor Layton and the Unwound Future, the franchise’s third Nintendo DS game. Set in the American city of Steam Bison, the plot follows Luke inviting Professor Layton to visit.
- In the Japanese version, Yō Ōizumi returns to voice Professor Layton, and Mio Imada is the new voice of Luke Triton. Additional Japanese voice actors include Riho Yoshioka as Elinora, Elida Mahiro Takasugi as Eggmuffin, Shin'ya Kote as Bolt Allinston, Kōichi Yamadera as Sheriff Bobsley, Akio Ōtsuka as Falcon, and Michaela Wako Sato as Mystella.
- Quiz Knock, a group known for creating puzzles and riddles for media, is responsible for the game’s puzzles.
- Joe Hisaishi, famed for his work on Studio Ghibli films, is composing the theme song, which will be performed by Lilas.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The announcement ignites excitement on multiple fronts. First, the return of Christopher Robin Miller and Lani Minella as Professor Layton and Luke reassures longtime fans that the core dynamic of the series remains intact. Second, the inclusion of a full English dub addresses a long‑standing demand from the international community, especially in regions where subtitles can be a barrier to immersion.

The addition of a diverse new cast—especially voices like Amber Lee Connors and Kayli Mills—signals a fresh injection of talent that could broaden the series’ appeal. Moreover, the fact that the game launches across the most popular modern platforms—Switch 2, Switch, PS5, and Steam—means it will be accessible to a wider audience than ever before.

The timing of the release is also perfect. With the 2027 remake of Professor Layton and the Curious Village on the horizon, fans are primed to revisit the franchise’s roots while also exploring its latest expansion.

## Background

Professor Layton and The New World of Steam is the newest entry in a long‑running series that began on the Nintendo DS. The franchise’s earlier titles include Professor Layton and the Curious Village, Professor Layton and the Unwound Future, and Layton’s Mystery Journey: Katrielle and the Millionaire’s Journey. The series has also seen a television adaptation, Layton Mystery Tanteisha: Katori no Nazotoki File, which aired from April 2018 to March 2019.

The current game was originally slated for a release last year but was delayed. It finally launched in Japan for the Nintendo 3DS and worldwide for iOS and Android devices in July 2017. The Switch version followed in August 2018, heading West in November 2019.

## What's Next

While the immediate focus is on the December 10 launch, the franchise’s future looks bright. In 2027, a remake of Professor Layton and the Curious Village will be released for Switch 2, Switch, PS5, and PC via Steam, promising a refreshed experience for both new players and veterans.

The involvement of Quiz Knock as the puzzle designers suggests that the game will continue the series’ hallmark of challenging, brain‑teasing riddles. Combined with Joe Hisaishi’s musical contribution, the title is poised to deliver a rich, immersive experience.

For fans who have followed the series from its DS origins, the English dub and cross‑platform release represent a milestone—bringing the beloved mysteries into the modern era with fresh voices and a polished presentation.

## Closing

Professor Layton and The New World of Steam’s English dub announcement is a major win for the franchise’s global community. With returning talent, a robust new cast, and a release that spans the most popular gaming platforms, the series is set to captivate audiences worldwide. Mark your calendars for December 10 and get ready to dive back into the world of puzzles, mysteries, and, of course, Professor Layton himself.

Hey there, manga community! Weeb here, bringing you a quick but important update from the manga world. If you've been eagerly waiting to tune into the latest chapter of *Tune in to the Midnight Heart*, you are going to have to adjust your dials for just a brief moment. 

It has been announced that the beloved manga series is officially going on a short one-week hiatus. This sudden pause in publication is due to the health of the series' creator, Masakuni Igarashi. 

While it is always tough to wait for the next installment of our favorite stories, a creator's health and well-being must always come first. The demanding schedules of the manga industry are incredibly rigorous, and taking a brief pause to rest and recuperate is highly important for mangaka.

### When Will the Manga Return?
Thankfully, the wait won't be a long one! Fans won't have to stay in suspense for too long, as Masakuni Igarashi is currently slated to resume the manga on **October 14, 2026**. 

Make sure to mark your calendars and prepare for the series' return in just a week's time. Let's all send positive vibes to Igarashi-sensei for a swift and smooth recovery so they can return to doing what they do best!

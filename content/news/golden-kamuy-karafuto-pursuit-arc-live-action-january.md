# WOWOW Drops New Golden Kamuy Live‑Action Series: Karafuto Pursuit Arc Starts Jan. 24 – Fans Rejoice!

The Golden Kamuy universe is expanding once again, and anime fans are already buzzing. On Friday, WOWOW announced that the long‑awaited live‑action series **Golden Kamuy: Karafuto Tsuiseki‑hen** (Karafuto Pursuit Arc) will premiere on **January 24 at 10:00 p.m.** This marks the next chapter in a franchise that has already taken Japan’s screens by storm with its manga, anime, and film adaptations.

## What We Know

- **Title & Premiere** – *Golden Kamuy: Karafuto Tsuiseki‑hen* will stream on WOWOW starting **January 24** at 10:00 p.m.
- **Directors** – Returning director **Kenji Katagiri** teams up with new director **Masaaki Yoshimura**.
- **Scriptwriter** – **Tetsuo Kamata** (known for *live‑action BLUE LOCK*) writes the new series.
- **Music** – **Yutaka Yamada** and **Yoshiaki Dewa** return to compose the score.
- **Cultural Advisors** – **Hiroshi Nakagawa** and **Debo Akibe** continue as Ainu language and cultural advisers.
- **Production** – **CREDEUS** produces the new series.

### Background on the Franchise

- **Manga** – *Golden Kamuy* began in Shueisha’s *Young Jump* in 2014 and wrapped up in April 2022. The series spawned four anime seasons.
- **First Live‑Action Film** – Released in Japan in January 2024, it topped the box office on opening weekend, selling **356,000 tickets** for **533,848,690 yen** (≈US$3.44 million). Netflix streamed the film starting May 2024.
- **Follow‑up Film** – *Golden Kamuy: Attack on Abashiri Prison* (Abashiri Kangoku Shūgeki‑hen) opened in Japan on **March 13** and placed #3 at the box office. Netflix began streaming it on **July 13**.
- **Key Cast** – **Kento Yamazaki** reprises Saichi Sugimoto, **Anna Yamada** plays Asirpa, and newcomers **Yū Inaba** (Tokishige Usami), **Sōkō Wada** (Toshiyuki Kadokura), and **Tetta Sugimoto** (Anji Toni) join the ensemble.
- **Crew Continuity** – The first film was directed by **Shigeaki Kubo** with script by **Tsutomu Kuroiwa**; the follow‑up film was directed by **Kenji Katagiri**, with **Ken Ochiai** and **Yōsuke Satō** joining him. **Yutaka Yamada** composed the music for both, while **Hiroshi Nakagawa** and **Debo Akibe** served as cultural advisers. The theme songs were performed by **ACIDMAN** (“Kagayakerumono”) for the first film and **10‑FEET** (“Kowarete Kieru made”) for the second.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The announcement hits at a perfect moment: the original manga’s final arc just premiered on January 5, and the live‑action series will pick up right where the anime left off. Fans can now see the climactic **Runaway Train** arc on screen, a narrative that has already thrilled millions in the manga and anime realms.

Moreover, the return of beloved crew members—**Kenji Katagiri**, **Yutaka Yamada**, and the Ainu advisers—provides a sense of continuity and authenticity that has become a hallmark of the franchise. The addition of **Masaaki Yoshimura** and **Tetsuo Kamata** promises fresh directorial vision and sharp scripting, which many viewers anticipate will elevate the storytelling.

The box‑office success of the first film, combined with Netflix’s global reach, signals that the live‑action adaptation has already captured a wide audience. The fact that the series will air on WOWOW, a premium Japanese network, ensures high production values and a prime-time slot that will attract both dedicated fans and casual viewers.

## Background (Brief Franchise Context)

*Golden Kamuy* follows the treasure‑hunt adventure of Saichi Sugimoto and Asirpa as they search for a hidden stash of gold in Hokkaido’s Karafuto region. The story blends historical intrigue, survival drama, and deep cultural exploration of the Ainu people. Its manga’s popularity led to an anime adaptation with four seasons, each faithfully translating the manga’s pacing and character depth.

The live‑action films—released in 2024 and 2025—captured the series’ gritty atmosphere and complex characters, thanks in part to the meticulous work of the cultural advisers, who ensured respectful representation of Ainu traditions. The success of those films set the stage for the upcoming series, which promises to maintain the same level of authenticity while expanding the narrative scope.

## What's Next

- **January 24 Premiere** – Watch the new series debut on WOWOW at 10:00 p.m. and follow the next chapter of Sugimoto and Asirpa’s quest.
- **Runaway Train Arc** – The series will cover the climactic “Runaway Train” storyline that the manga’s final arc introduced, providing a cinematic culmination to the long‑running saga.
- **Netflix Availability** – While the series will stream on WOWOW first, it is likely to reach a global audience via Netflix after the initial broadcast, following the pattern of the films.

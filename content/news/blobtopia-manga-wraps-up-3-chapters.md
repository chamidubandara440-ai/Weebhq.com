**In a whirlwind announcement that has sent ripples through the anime and manga community, Jun Kirarazaka has confirmed that his hit series *Blobtopia* will conclude in just three chapters.** The news came via the creator’s X (formerly Twitter) account on Friday, and it’s already become the hottest topic on fan forums and social media. For those who have been following the surreal, over‑the‑top hero saga, this means the final chapters are almost upon us, and the stakes have never been higher.

## What We Know

- **Official announcement:** Jun Kirarazaka posted on X that *Blobtopia* will end in three chapters.
- **Final chapter release:** The last chapter will drop on **November 6**, and it will be a side‑story chapter.
- **Publication history:** The series debuted on **Shonen Jump+** on **March 13** (2026). Shueisha shipped the second compiled volume on **September 4**.
- **English availability:** Both **MANGA Plus** and **Viz Media** have published the series digitally in English.
- **Story premise (as described by MANGA Plus):** "Unable to defeat the Demon King, former hero Beyond ended up flooding the world with ‘blobs.’ Guilt‑ridden, he shut himself away for years—until a turning point finally arrives…!!"
- **Creator background:** Kirarazaka previously launched *Bone Collection* in Weekly Shonen Jump (April 2020–August 2020) and published a one‑shot with the same name in August 2019. His recent work, *Do Retry*, ran from May 2023 to September 2023.

## Background

*Blobtopia* is a comedy‑drama that subverts the classic hero narrative. The protagonist, Beyond, once celebrated as a savior, is forced into exile after failing to defeat the Demon King. In a desperate bid to atone, he floods the world with “blobs,” creating a bizarre, gelatinous landscape that becomes the backdrop for the series’ satirical exploration of heroism and society. Kirarazaka’s signature over‑the‑top humor and sharp social commentary shine through as the story oscillates between absurd battles and introspective moments.

The series debuted on Shonen Jump+ on March 13, 2026, and quickly gained a following for its unique premise and quirky art style. Shueisha compiled the first two volumes, with the second volume released on September 4, 2026. Despite a relatively short run, the manga’s impact has been significant, prompting discussions about the nature of heroism in modern storytelling.

Kirarazaka’s earlier works—*Bone Collection* (April 2020–August 2020) and the one‑shot in August 2019—demonstrate his versatility across different formats. His latest venture, *Do Retry*, which ran from May 2023 to September 2023, showcased his ability to blend comedic elements with deeper emotional beats, further solidifying his reputation in the shōnen community.

Crunchyroll has just unleashed the English dub of *The Apothecary Diaries* Season 3, and the anime community is buzzing like never before. The announcement came Thursday, with the dub debuting on Friday, and the new voice cast is already making waves on social media. For fans who have followed Maomao’s journey from a humble apothecary’s daughter to a court insider, this is the moment that has been building since the first episode aired last October.

## What We Know

* Crunchyroll announced on Thursday that the English dub for Season 3 of *The Apothecary Diaries* will stream on Friday.
* The dub cast features Emi Lo as Maomao, Tang as Jinshi, Saxton as White Lady, Suzie Yeung as Shisui, Anne Yatco as Kyou‑u, Nazeeh Tarsha as Guard, Wendy Powell as Madam Oscar, Seung as Ukyou, Seth Fuentes as Lihaku, Eli Farmer as Onsou, Monica Rial as Narrator, Rebecca Danae as Meimei, Caleb Yen as Lahan, Alex Mai as Emperor Ricco, Fajardo as Lakan, Reshel Mae as Lishu, Charles Nguyen as Rikuson, Seth Magill as Uryuu, Phil Parsons as Gyokuen, Adrian Godinez as Mr. Owner, Tom Aglio as Niban, Monica Rial again as Ah‑Duo, Alex Hom as Gaoshun, Ed Escamilla as Basen, Monica Flatley as Joka, Kit Lozano as Kanan, and additional voices by Paxton Lee, Nasim Benelkour, Mauricio Ortiz‑Segura, Brandon Acosta, Daniela Valkova, and Elizabeth Hohman.
* Production staff includes Studio: Crunchyroll Studio; Producers: Justin Cook, Michael Harcourt, Nathanael Harrison; Producer: Susie Nixon; Adaptation Supervisor: Macy Anne Johnson; Adaptation: Clayton Browning; Adaptation Prep: Benjamin Tehrani; Voice Director: Shawn Gann; Assistant Voice Director: Kevin D. Thelwell; Talent Coordination: Tara A. Williams, Brittni Hurley; Engineering Managers: Gino Palencia, Brandon Peters; Engineer: Derric Benavides; Assistant Engineer: Katie Saltkill; Mixer: James Baker.
* Season 3 premiered on October 2 at 11:00 p.m. (10:00 a.m. EDT) in the Friday Anime Night block on NTV and 29 affiliated channels for its first cours. The second cours will premiere in April 2027.
* Crunchyroll streams the anime as it airs in Japan.
* The opening theme is “Kumo o Nukekazashimo e Watashidake” (I Am Alone, Breaking Through the Clouds Downwind) by yorushika, and the ending theme is “AIYOU” by Eve.
* The light novel series is published digitally by J‑Novel Club.
* The story follows Maomao, raised by an apothecary father, who is kidnapped and sold into service at the emperor’s rear palace, the hougong. She uses her medical knowledge and sharp wit to navigate court intrigue, becoming a lady‑in‑waiting to the emperor’s favorite consort and testing her food for poison.
* The first anime season premiered in October 2023 with three episodes and ran for two cours. The second season premiered in January 2025 and also ran for two cours. Both seasons were streamed by Crunchyroll with English dubs.
* A film, *Kusuriya no Hitorigoto: Bōhi no Hihо̄* (The Deceased Empress’ Treasure), will open in theaters on December 11, featuring a new story by Hyuganatsu.
* A live‑action adaptation will debut on Amazon Prime Video worldwide in 2028.
* Hyuganatsu began serializing the story on Shōsetsuka ni Narō in October 2011. Shufunotomo published the novels in print with illustrations by Shino in August 2014. Nekokurage launched a manga adaptation in Square Enix’s Monthly Big Gangan in May 2017, with Square Enix Manga & Books releasing the manga in English. Viz Media is releasing Minoji Kurata’s *The Apothecary Diaries: Maomao’s Notes on the Inner Palace* manga in print.

## Background

*The Apothecary Diaries* began as a web novel on Shōsetsuka ni Narō in 2011 and quickly gained popularity for its unique blend of medical science, court politics, and mystery. The light novels were later published in print by Shufunotomo, featuring illustrations by Touko Shino. The manga adaptation, launched in 2017, broadened the audience and helped cement the series’ status as a mainstream hit.

The anime adaptation has been a major driver of the franchise’s growth. Season 1 premiered in October 2023, with Crunchyroll providing both subtitled and dubbed versions, setting a precedent for high‑quality localization. Season 2, which began airing in January 2025, continued this trend, further expanding the fanbase. The third season’s launch on October 2, 2026, marks the franchise’s third consecutive two‑cours run, a testament to its sustained popularity.

The franchise’s expansion into film and live‑action formats—most notably the upcoming December 11 theatrical release of *The Deceased Empress’ Treasure* and the 2028 Amazon Prime Video live‑action series—demonstrates the story’s versatility and the strong demand for its content across platforms.

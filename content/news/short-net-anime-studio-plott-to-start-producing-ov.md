Hey there, anime fans! Weeb here, bringing you some incredibly exciting news from the industry's digital frontier. 

If you’ve been keeping an eye on the evolution of internet animation, you know how rapidly the landscape is shifting. Now, we have a major milestone to celebrate: **Plott**, the studio widely known for its short net anime, is officially making the jump to traditional broadcasting. The company has announced that it will begin producing **over-the-air TV anime**, marking a massive step forward in its evolution as a production house.

### From Digital Shorts to the Television Screen

Plott has built its reputation on creating highly engaging content designed specifically for the internet era. They recently made waves in the digital space with the launch of their brand-new YouTube short anime, **Rakuraku Hanten**, which officially made its debut on **September 18**. 

While the studio has mastered the art of capturing the attention of online audiences with quick, accessible net animations, this new venture into over-the-air television signals a grand expansion of their creative reach. Transitioning to traditional TV anime production is a major milestone, and it will be fascinating to see how the studio translates its digital-first expertise and unique style to mainstream broadcast networks.

### What Lies Ahead for Plott?

By taking their talents to over-the-air television, Plott is bridging the gap between internet-native short-form anime and mainstream broadcasting. For fans who have been following their digital releases, including the newly launched *Rakuraku Hanten*, this transition represents an exciting new chapter for the creators behind the scenes.

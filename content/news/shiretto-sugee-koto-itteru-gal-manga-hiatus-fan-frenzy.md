### Urgent: Shiretto Sugee koto Itteru Gal Fans, Your Manga’s Summer Break Is Official

The anime‑to‑manga world just dropped a headline that’s already making waves across every fan forum: **Shiretto Sugee koto Itteru Gal** will be on hiatus for the next four to five months, with a return slated for February or March next year.  The news came from the series’ own publisher, Shueisha, and the manga’s creator, Taichi Matsuura, who confirmed that the break will allow the illustrator, Otsuji, to produce special illustrations for *Tonari no Young Jump* and *Young Jump+*.  Fans who have been riding the wave of the series’ quirky high‑school supernatural antics are now scrambling for the exact dates and wondering what this means for the story’s momentum.

## What We Know

- **Hiatus Announcement**: The manga will pause its serialization for a 4‑5 month break, resuming in February or March of 2027.
- **Illustrator’s Side Project**: During the hiatus, Otsuji will create special illustrations for *Tonari no Young Jump* and *Young Jump+*.
- **Series Premise**: The story follows two high‑school gyaru, Mirai Misato (who can see the future) and “Heeko” Taira (who can travel between parallel universes), living a normal high‑school life while occasionally saying the strangest things.
- **Publication History**: Launched in Shueisha’s *Tonari no Young Jump* and *Young Jump+* in November 2023.  The fifth compiled volume was released on March 18, 2026, and the sixth is slated for November 18, 2026.
- **English Release**: Seven Seas publishes the manga in English, with the eighth volume released on June 23, 2026.
- **Anime Adaptation**: The manga inspired a television anime that debuted on July 8, 2026, streamed by REMOW’s “It’s Anime” free YouTube channel.
- **Creator Background**: Otsuji previously launched *My Stepmother and Stepsisters Aren’t Wicked* in 2020, with its 10th volume published on August 25, 2020.

## Background

*Shiretto Sugee koto Itteru Gal*—translated loosely as “Gals Casually Say Incredible Things”—originates from Plott’s short web anime channel *Shiritsu Paranormal Kōkō* (Paranormal High School).  The manga adaptation captures the same quirky tone, blending supernatural elements with the everyday life of a high‑school setting.  The two protagonists, Mirai Misato and Heeko Taira, are both gyaru, a subculture known for its bold fashion and confident attitude.  Their supernatural abilities—future sight and inter‑dimensional travel—are juxtaposed with their normal school routines, creating a unique narrative rhythm that fans have praised for its humor and heart.

Shueisha’s decision to serialize the manga on both *Tonari no Young Jump* and *Young Jump+* reflects a strategic move to reach a wide demographic.  The dual-platform approach ensures accessibility for both print and digital readers.  Additionally, the series’ anime adaptation, which premiered on July 8, 2026, has been streamed on the free channel “It’s Anime” by REMOW, making it easy for new viewers to jump into the story.

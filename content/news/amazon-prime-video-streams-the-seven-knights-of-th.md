The anime adaptation of **The Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom** has officially made its grand debut! 

### A Day-One Treat for Dub Fans

The series officially premiered on **October 3**, and Amazon Prime Video is making sure fans can enjoy the series exactly how they want right from the get-go. In a fantastic move for accessibility, the streaming giant is offering a **same-day English dub** alongside its release. 

Typically, anime enthusiasts are used to the "simuldub delay"—the agonizing wait of several weeks, or sometimes even months, for the English-voiced episodes to catch up with the original Japanese broadcast. However, Amazon Prime Video is breaking down those barriers, allowing fans of the English dub to jump straight into the action of *The Seven Knights of the Marronnier Kingdom* on the very same day as its debut.

Whether you prefer the subbed version or want to dive straight into the English dub, you can head over to Amazon Prime Video right now to start streaming.

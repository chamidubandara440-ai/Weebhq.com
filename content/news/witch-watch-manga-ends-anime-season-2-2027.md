**Witch Watch** has just delivered the ultimate double‑header for its fanbase: the manga’s final chapter drops in the 51st issue of *Weekly Shonen Jump*, and the anime’s second season is officially scheduled to premiere in 2027.  The announcement came with fresh teaser visuals that already have the community buzzing, and the timing couldn’t be more perfect—fans who have followed the series since its debut in 2021 are now given a clear roadmap for the story’s conclusion.

## What We Know

- **Manga finale**: The series wrapped up in the 51st issue of *Weekly Shonen Jump* on Monday, 15 Nov 2026.  The final chapter was released alongside a new teaser visual for the anime.
- **Anime season 2**: The production committee unveiled a teaser visual and confirmed that Season 2 will air in 2027.
- **English distribution**: Both *MANGA Plus* and Viz Media are publishing the series in English.  Viz Media’s first volume provides a concise synopsis: Morihito Otogi, a descendant of ogre familiars, is tasked with protecting teenage witch Nico after a prophecy threatens her.
- **Broadcast history**: The first anime season premiered on 6 April 2025 across MBS, TBS, and 26 affiliated channels.  The second part of the season began on 29 June 2025.
- **International streaming**: Crunchyroll, Netflix, ADN, Aniplus, Hulu, and Tropics streamed the series worldwide as it aired.
- **Dubbing**: The English dub debuted on 27 April 2025, with additional dubs in French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (Spain and Latin America), and Thai.
- **Theatrical screenings**: GKIDS screened the first three episodes in North America from 16‑18 March 2025.  Tropics screened the episodes in Asia starting 18 March, ADN in selected European countries in March, and Aniplus in South Korea on 21 March.
- **Creator background**: Kenta Shinohara, known for *Astra Lost in Space* and *Sket Dance*, launched the manga in February 2021.
- **Volume releases**: Shueisha published the 23rd compiled volume on 4 September 2025.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The dual nature of the announcement—ending the manga while simultaneously promising a new anime season—creates a rare sense of anticipation.  Fans have been following Morihito’s heavy‑handed protector role and Nico’s prophecy‑laden journey for nearly five years.  The manga’s conclusion offers closure, while the anime’s 2027 premiere keeps the story alive in a new medium.  Social media threads are already filled with speculation about how the anime will adapt the final arcs and whether the new season will cover the manga’s ending or explore uncharted territory.

The visual teasers themselves are a treat: they feature the dynamic duo in a more dramatic setting, hinting at a darker tone and larger stakes.  For a series that balances humor with supernatural peril, the promise of a more intense second season is a major draw.  The fact that the anime is already available in multiple languages and has had theatrical releases worldwide only adds to the global buzz.

## Background

*Witch Watch* debuted in *Weekly Shonen Jump* in February 2021, quickly establishing itself as a quirky yet emotionally resonant series.  The premise—Morihito Otogi, a lineage of ogre familiars, becomes the familiar for his childhood friend Nico, a teenage witch—offers a fresh take on the familiar‑wizard dynamic.  The series blends action, romance, and prophecy, and has been praised for its character development.

The anime adaptation began airing in April 2025, with its first season split into two parts.  The production quality and voice cast received positive reviews, and the series’ availability on major streaming platforms broadened its reach.  The English dub and multiple foreign dubs demonstrate the series’ international appeal.

Kenta Shinohara’s involvement as writer and illustrator brings a distinct style that fans of his previous works, such as *Astra Lost in Space* and *Sket Dance*, can appreciate.  His storytelling balances humor with darker themes, a hallmark that has kept viewers engaged.

## What's Next

While the manga’s final chapter provides a definitive end, the anime’s 2027 premiere raises several questions.  Will the new season adapt the concluding arcs, or will it explore side stories and character backstories that were only hinted at?  The teaser visual suggests a shift toward a more ominous atmosphere, which could mean the anime will tackle the prophecy in greater depth.

Fans can also anticipate potential new content from the production committee, such as special episodes, OVAs, or a possible film adaptation—common practice for series that enjoy a strong fanbase.  The fact that the anime has already secured international distribution and multiple dubs indicates that the studio is invested in maintaining global engagement.

Meanwhile, the manga’s final volume will likely be re‑released in collected editions, and the 23rd volume’s release on 4 September 2025 means collectors have a chance to own the complete series before the anime’s next chapter.

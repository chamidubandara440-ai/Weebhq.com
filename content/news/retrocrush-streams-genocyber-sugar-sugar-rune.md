**RetroCrush just dropped two cult‑favorite series and a classic live‑action film, and the anime community is buzzing.** In a single announcement that came out this morning, the streaming service revealed that Genocyber, the 1994 OVA series, and the 2005 Sugar Sugar Rune television anime are now available on its platform. Even more exciting, the 1983 live‑action film *The Girl Who Leapt Through Time* will join RetroCrush on December 4. The timing is perfect for fans craving fresh content after the recent wave of anime releases, and the move positions RetroCrush as a go‑to destination for nostalgic titles.

## What We Know

- **Genocyber** – The 1994 OVA series, based on Tony Takezaki’s manga, was added to RetroCrush’s catalog. Discotek Media had released the series on Blu‑ray in January 2021.
- **Sugar Sugar Rune** – The 2005 television anime, adapted from Moyoco Anno’s manga serialized in Kodansha’s *Nakayoshi* (2004‑2007), is now streaming. Tubi had begun streaming the series in January of this year.
- **The Girl Who Leapt Through Time** – The 1983 live‑action film, directed by Nobuhiko Ōbayashi with a script by Wataru Kenmotsu, will debut on RetroCrush on December 4. Cult Epics released the film on Blu‑ray on January 20 earlier this year. The movie is based on Yasutaka Tsutsui’s novel that also inspired Mamoru Hosoda’s 2006 anime.
- **Additional Viz Media titles** – RetroCrush has already added several titles announced in August, including *Vampire Knight*, *Accel World*, *Naruto the Movie: Ninja Clash in the Land of Snow*, *Sailor Moon R: The Movie*, *Bleach the Movie: Hell Verse*, and *Captain Tsubasa*.
- **Upcoming additions** – *Hunter × Hunter: Phantom Rouge* will be available on November 20, and *Hikaru no Go* on November 27.
- **Vampire Princess Miyu** – The series was added with a Japanese dub on October 2 and an English dub on October 30.

The announcement hits several high‑profile sweet spots for anime enthusiasts. ## Background

RetroCrush has positioned itself as a niche streaming service dedicated to classic anime and anime‑inspired media. Its catalog has historically focused on titles that may have slipped through the cracks of mainstream streaming giants. The addition of *Genocyber* and *Sugar Sugar Rune* aligns with this mission, as both series were once hard to find in high‑quality formats.

*Genocyber* originally aired as a six‑episode OVA in 1994. The series is known for its graphic violence and philosophical underpinnings, themes that resonate with mature anime fans. Its Blu‑ray release by Discotek Media in 2021 helped preserve the series for new generations.

*Sugar Sugar Rune* debuted in 2005 and is based on Moyoco Anno’s manga, which ran in *Nakayoshi* from 2004 to 2007. The anime’s whimsical art style and magical‑girl tropes made it a standout during its original run, and its availability on RetroCrush and Tubi has kept it in circulation.

The 1983 live‑action *Girl Who Leapt Through Time* is a landmark film adaptation of Yasutaka Tsutsui’s novel. Directed by Nobuhiko Ōbayashi and scripted by Wataru Kenmotsu, the film predates Mamoru Hosoda’s 2006 anime and remains a classic of Japanese cinema. Its Blu‑ray release by Cult Epics earlier this year has helped preserve its legacy.

Anime fans, it is time to put on your lab coats! An official anime adaptation has been announced for the intriguing light novel series, **The Science Notes by Delta and Gamma**. 

### Kanna Nakamura Lends Her Voice to the Big Reveal

To celebrate this major milestone, the official anime announcement comes with a special touch. Voice actress **Kanna Nakamura** lends her voice to the announcement, bringing an extra layer of excitement to the project's big debut. 

### Solving Life's Deepest Mysteries

For those unfamiliar with the premise of *The Science Notes by Delta and Gamma*, the story centers around a high school science club. However, this is no ordinary after-school club. Instead of basic classroom experiments, these club members dedicate their time and minds to investigating and dealing with some of life's greatest mysteries. 

With a unique premise blending high school club activities and deep, mysterious questions, this is one upcoming adaptation you will certainly want to keep on your radar. Stay tuned for more updates as they are revealed!

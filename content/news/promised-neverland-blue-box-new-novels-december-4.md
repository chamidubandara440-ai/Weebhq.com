# The Promised Neverland & Blue Box Celebrate 10th & Final Year With New Novels – December 4 Release Alert

Anime fans, the clock is ticking: on **December 4** two long‑awaited novel releases will hit the shelves, marking a decade of *The Promised Neverland* and the culmination of *Blue Box*’s story.  Shueisha’s announcement is the hottest news of the week, and if you’ve been following these series, you’ll feel the excitement as the final chapters of a beloved saga and a fresh look at the characters you love are about to be unveiled.

## What We Know

* **Authors & Titles** – Nanao, who has penned every *Promised Neverland* novel to date, is writing the new **Yakusoku no Neverland ~10‑nen‑me no Okurimono~** (The Promised Neverland: A 10th Year Gift) and the **Ao no Hako Epilogue** (Blue Box Epilogue).  Both novels are slated for release on **December 4**.
* **Content Overview** – The *Promised Neverland* novel will follow Emma and the other children after the manga’s final episode, and will also include stories about Cislo, the Lambda group, Phil, and the younger children.  The *Blue Box* epilogue will cover moments before and after the manga’s finale, such as Taiki and Chinatsu’s drive after Taiki earns his driver’s licence, and Hina’s life as a rhythmic‑gymnast university student.
* **Publication History** – Shueisha has previously released four *Promised Neverland* novels (2018‑2020) and two *Blue Box* novels (2024‑2025).  The manga’s 20th and final volume of *Promised Neverland* shipped in October 2020, while *Blue Box*’s final volume will ship on the same day as the novels.
* **Distribution** – Shueisha will publish the novels in Japan, with MANGA Plus and Viz Media handling digital and physical releases in English.  The manga’s 28th volume will also ship on December 4, and the 22nd volume will be released by Viz Media on November 3.
* **Franchise Milestones** – The *Blue Box* manga has surpassed 10 million copies in circulation as of May.  The series began serialization in April 2021 and concluded on July 13, with 27 volumes already compiled.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The excitement is palpable for several reasons:

1. **10‑Year Celebration** – *The Promised Neverland* debuted in *Weekly Shonen Jump* in August 2016 and wrapped its story in June 2020.  A decade later, fans are finally getting a novel that extends the narrative beyond the manga’s ending.
2. **Character‑Focused Stories** – Both novels promise deeper dives into side characters.  The *Promised Neverland* novel will explore Cislo, the Lambda group, and Phil, while the *Blue Box* epilogue will highlight Taiki, Chinatsu, and Hina’s post‑manga lives.
3. **Continuity & Closure** – For *Blue Box*, the epilogue arrives alongside the final volume, offering a tidy wrap‑up that many readers have been waiting for since the manga’s conclusion.
4. **Nanao’s Consistency** – Nanao has written every novel in the *Promised Neverland* line, giving fans confidence that the new stories will stay true to the original tone and lore.
5. **Cross‑Media Momentum** – The anime adaptations (two seasons for *Promised Neverland* and one for *Blue Box*) have built a massive fanbase.  The new novels are a natural extension of that momentum, keeping the community engaged.

## Background

### The Promised Neverland

- **Creators**: Kaiu Shirai (story) and Posuka Demizu (art).  The manga ran in *Weekly Shonen Jump* from August 2016 to June 2020.
- **Plot**: Emma, Norman, and the other children uncover the dark truth behind their orphanage, leading to a high‑stakes battle for survival.
- **Adaptations**: Two anime seasons (2019, 2021), a live‑action film (2020), and multiple novels.  The first novel, *A Letter from Norman*, shipped in June 2018; subsequent novels focused on different characters and arcs.
- **English Availability**: Viz Media publishes the manga in print and digitally; MANGA Plus offers digital releases.

### Blue Box

- **Creator**: Kōji Miura, who first published the story as a one‑shot in *Weekly Shonen Jump* in 2020.
- **Serialization**: Began April 2021, ending July 13.  The manga ran for 27 volumes, with the final 28th volume shipping on December 4.
- **Adaptations**: An anime adaptation premiered its first season in October 2024, running for two cours, and a second season debuted on October 4 on TBS and other channels, with Netflix streaming.
- **Novels**: The first novel, *Ao no Hako Prologue*, shipped in December 2024; the second, *Ao no Hako Interlude*, shipped in December 2025.  Both were written by Nanao.
- **English Availability**: Shueisha’s MANGA Plus and Viz Media publish the manga in English.

## What's Next

- **December 4**: The release of the *Promised Neverland* 10‑year gift novel, the *Blue Box* epilogue, and *Blue Box*’s final volume will create a massive surge in sales and fan discussions.  Readers will be able to compare the novel’s expanded scenes with the manga’s final chapters.
- **Post‑Release Discussions**: Forums and fan sites will likely dissect the new character arcs, especially Cislo’s role in the *Promised Neverland* universe and Hina’s university journey in *Blue Box*.
- **Future Projects**: While no new series are confirmed, Shueisha’s track record of continuing successful franchises (e.g., *Blue Box*’s anime seasons) suggests the possibility of spin‑offs or additional media tied to the novels.
- **Collector’s Items**: The simultaneous release of the final manga volume and the novel may prompt special editions or bundled releases, a common practice for milestone celebrations.

With the clock set to December 4, anime and manga communities worldwide are gearing up for a historic moment: the convergence of *The Promised Neverland*’s 10‑year legacy and *Blue Box*’s final chapter, both expanded into novel form.  Whether you’re a long‑time fan of Emma’s fight for freedom or a devoted follower of Taiki and Chinatsu’s high‑school adventures, these releases promise fresh perspectives and a satisfying closure.

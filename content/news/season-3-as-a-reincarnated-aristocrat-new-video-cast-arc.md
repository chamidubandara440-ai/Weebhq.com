## Urgent Opening
The world of *As a Reincarnated Aristocrat, I’ll Use My Appraisal Skill* is about to get a major power‑up. On Sunday, the anime’s official site released a brand‑new promotional video that not only teases the beginning of the Assassin arc in episode 33 but also reveals four fresh voice actors joining the cast. For a series that has already raked in over 30 million online views in Japan, this update is a must‑watch, and the community is already flooding social media with excitement. If you haven’t checked out the new trailer yet, you’re missing the next chapter of Ars Louvent’s journey to lift his country from the ashes.

## What We Know
- **New Cast Inclusions**: The promo introduces Anna Nagase as Enan Lugez, a mute girl who volunteers for military service at Canarre Castle. Kōsuke Toriumi voices Virge Sammado, a former noble who claims kinship with Ars. Ayumu Murase plays Keefe Venge, a boy with “extremely high skill caps in every stat.” These three are added to the already confirmed lineup of Sumire Morohoshi (Musia Trick), Hinata Tadokoro (Zaht Brouzdo), Toshiya Miyata (Rikuya Fujimiya), Mariya Ise (Maika Fujimiya), and Takaki Ōtomari (Takao Fujimiya).
- **Staff Changes**: Takao Kato returns to direct season 3, this time under the new studio GeyeG Pictures. Daisuke Ōhigashi, who handled episode scripts for the previous seasons, now oversees the series scripts, replacing Yasuhiro Nakanishi. Yūko Yahiro returns to design the characters, and Kujira Yumemi comes back to compose the music.
- **Music**: The opening theme is “Still Water” performed by Rico Sasaki, while the ending theme is “Hibi o Koete” by Akuruyo no Hitsuji.
- **Broadcast & Streaming**: Season 3 premiered on CBC, TBS, and 26 other affiliated stations on September 27; on BS NTV on September 29; and on AT‑X on October 1. Streaming began on U‑NEXT, d Anime Store, and Anime Hōdai on September 28, with additional platforms adding the series on October 1. Crunchyroll streams the season as it airs.
- **Licensing**: Kodansha USA Publishing has licensed the manga adaptation and the light novel series under its Vertical imprint.
- **Plot Reminder**: One day when leaving work, Ars Louvent dies and is reborn as a weak aristocrat in another world. Using his “Appraisal” skill, which lets him see the stats of others, he recruits the best people to his side in order to turn his country’s fortunes around.

## Background
*As a Reincarnated Aristocrat, I’ll Use My Appraisal Skill* began as a light novel on *Shōsetsuka ni Narō* in October 2019, written by Miraijin A and illustrated by jimmy. Kodansha published the series, and it quickly amassed more than 30 million online views in Japan. The manga adaptation debuted in Kodansha’s *Magazine Pocket* in June 2020.

The anime adaptation premiered in April 2024 as the debut series for the new “Agaru Anime” block on CBC, TBS, and 26 other affiliated networks. Season 2 aired in September 2024, with Crunchyroll streaming the series worldwide as it aired. The third season’s premiere on September 27 2026 marks the continuation of Ars’s quest to elevate his nation using his unique appraisal skill.

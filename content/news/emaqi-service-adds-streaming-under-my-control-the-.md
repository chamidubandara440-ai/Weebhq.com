Hey there, manga community! Your resident manga guru Weeb is here with some fantastic news for everyone looking to refresh their digital reading lists. If you've been using the digital manga service **emaqi**, you are about to get a massive influx of brand-new content to obsess over. 

The platform has officially expanded its library, dropping a stellar lineup of new additions that span across various genres. Leading the charge are some seriously eye-catching series that you will want to check out immediately.

### The Headliners Coming to emaqi

The service is bringing heavy hitters to its digital shelves. Fans of action, romance, and unique premises will have plenty to dive into with these major additions:

*   ***Streaming Under My Control***
*   ***The Soldier Groom and the Daikon Bride***
*   ***Shin Ikki Tousen***

Whether you're looking for the legendary martial arts action of *Shin Ikki Tousen* or the intriguing premises of *Streaming Under My Control* and *The Soldier Groom and the Daikon Bride*, there is truly something here for every kind of fan.

### Even More Manga to Explore!

But wait, there's more! Alongside these headline-grabbing series, emaqi has also added eight additional manga titles to its service to round out this major update. Among the newly available titles, readers can now dig into:

*   ***Kill Me Baby***
*   ***Song of the Thirty-Year-Olds' Blues***
*   ***Time Traveler's Disease***
*   ***A Meal by Moonlight***
*   ...and several other exciting releases!

With such a diverse mix of comedy, drama, slice-of-life, and unique narrative concepts, emaqi is making sure its subscribers are well-stocked with high-quality reading material. 

Head over to the emaqi service now to start reading these newly added titles, and let us know which one you're putting at the top of your queue!

The long‑running Black Summoner saga just hit its climax—today, the manga’s 200th and final chapter was released on Comic Gardo, marking the end of a 200‑chapter journey that has kept readers on the edge of their seats for nearly a decade. For fans who have followed Kelvin’s quest from the first page, the news feels like the culmination of a decade‑long adventure, and it’s already sparking a frenzy of discussions across forums, Twitter, and fan sites.

## What We Know
- The final chapter of **Black Summoner** (Kuro no Shōkanshi) was published on Tuesday by Comic Gardo, completing the manga’s 200‑chapter run.
- The manga is a direct adaptation of the light‑novel series written by **Doufu Mayoi** and illustrated by **Kurogin**.
- Both the light novels and the manga adaptation have been licensed for English release by **J‑Novel Club**, which will provide digital editions.
- **Yen Press** will publish the manga in physical form under its JNC imprint.
- The 200th chapter is the third and final part of the concluding arc, concluding Kelvin’s journey as the world’s strongest Summoner.
- The manga’s 26th and final compiled volume will be released by **Overlap** on October 20.
- The original light‑novel series began serialization on Shōsetsuka ni Narō in October 2014 and concluded in 2024.
- Overlap started publishing the illustrated light novels in 2016, with the 23rd volume released on March 26.
- A 12‑episode anime adaptation aired in 2022, bringing the story to a wider audience.

## Background
**Black Summoner** began as a web novel on Shōsetsuka ni Narō in October 2014. The story followed Kelvin, a transmigrated individual who traded his memories for powerful summoning abilities. As the world’s strongest Summoner, Kelvin’s first follower is the very goddess who transported him to another world. The narrative explores his transformation into a battle junkie, facing one formidable foe after another, and ultimately carving his name into history.

In 2016, Overlap partnered with the author to publish the illustrated light‑novel series, with Kurogin providing artwork that helped define the series’ visual identity. By 2018, the manga adaptation debuted on Comic Gardo, quickly gaining popularity for its dynamic art and faithful adaptation of the light novels. The manga’s 200‑chapter arc culminated in 2026, with the final chapter released online on Tuesday and the last compiled volume slated for October 20.

The franchise’s growth continued with a 12‑episode anime in 2022, which expanded the audience and cemented Black Summoner’s place in the competitive landscape of adventure‑summoner titles.

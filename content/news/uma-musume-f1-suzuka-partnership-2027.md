# Uma Musume Takes the Fast Lane: First Ever F1 Partnership at Suzuka 2027!

In a move that has sent shockwaves through the anime and racing communities alike, Cygames, the powerhouse behind the wildly popular *Uma Musume Pretty Derby*, has announced that it is the first founding partner of the 2027 FIA Formula 1 Lenovo Japanese Grand Prix. The partnership goes beyond a simple sponsorship—it will rename the legendary 130R corner at Suzuka to **“Umamusume 130R”** for an entire year, starting November 30, 2026. The announcement came on Thursday, just 0.2 hours before the news hit the feeds, and fans are already racing to the finish line of excitement.

## What We Know

- **Founding Partner Status**: Cygames has secured the title as the first founding partner of the 2027 F1 Japanese Grand Prix, a prestigious honor that places the *Uma Musume* brand front‑and‑center in the world’s most elite racing series.
- **Naming Rights**: The iconic 130R corner on the Suzuka Circuit will officially be renamed **“Umamusume 130R”** beginning on November 30, 2026, and will retain the name for the full calendar year of 2027.
- **Promotional Banner**: A large *Uma Musume* promotional banner will be displayed at the corner starting November 21, coinciding with the 25th JAF Suzuka Grand Prix.
- **Fan Zone Presence**: At the 2027 Grand Prix, which runs April 9‑11, 2027, *Uma Musume* will host a dedicated booth and a variety of displays in the GP Square Fanzone at Suzuka Circuit.
- **Ticket Sales**: Tickets for the 2027 event go on sale on November 15, 2026, at 11:00 a.m. Japan Time (November 14 at 9:00 p.m. ET) via the online MobilityStation shop.
- **Previous Racing Tie‑In**: This isn’t the first time the franchise has partnered with high‑speed motorsport. In October, *Uma Musume* collaborated with the Moto GP Grand Prix of Japan 2026 at the Motegi Circuit, with voice actor Tomoyo Takayanagi (voice of Oguri Cup) flying the checkered flag during Moto2 and Moto3 races.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The intersection of anime and motorsport is a rare and thrilling one. For *Uma Musume* fans, the partnership means their beloved horse‑girl characters will literally be part of the world’s fastest racing arena. Renaming a corner on Suzuka—a circuit that has hosted legendary battles for decades—creates a permanent, tangible link between the franchise and Formula 1.

- **Cultural Fusion**: Anime fans are accustomed to seeing their favorite characters in unexpected places, from video game cross‑overs to pop‑culture collaborations. Seeing *Uma Musume* icons on a Formula 1 track elevates the franchise to a new level of mainstream visibility.
- **Community Engagement**: The dedicated *Uma Musume* booth in the GP Square Fanzone offers fans an immersive experience—interactive displays, exclusive merchandise, and the chance to meet the voice actors in person.
- **Legacy Building**: The 130R corner is one of Suzuka’s most iconic turns. By rebranding it, *Uma Musume* is carving a legacy that future racing fans will see in race footage for years to come.

## Background

*Uma Musume Pretty Derby* is a multimedia franchise that blends the charm of anthropomorphic horse‑girls with the competitive spirit of racing. Created by Cygames, the series has expanded from a mobile game into anime, manga, and live events. The franchise’s unique blend of cut‑scene storytelling and real‑time racing mechanics has cultivated a dedicated fan base worldwide.

The partnership with Moto GP in October already showcased the franchise’s willingness to engage with motorsport. Voice actor Tomoyo Takayanagi’s appearance at Motegi was a highlight, and the event drew attention from both racing aficionados and anime enthusiasts.

Suzuka Circuit, located in Mie Prefecture, is famed for its figure‑eight layout and challenging 130R corner—a high‑speed, left‑handed sweep that tests a car’s aerodynamic efficiency and the driver’s skill. Naming this corner after *Uma Musume* is a nod to the franchise’s racing heritage and a strategic move to cement its place in motorsport lore.

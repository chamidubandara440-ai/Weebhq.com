## What We Know
- **Noa Nekada’s Death**: Toho Entertainment and TV Tokyo confirmed that actress Noa Nekada, age 23, has died. She was born on June 21, 2003, in Ishikawa Prefecture.
- **Role in Ultraman Teo**: Nekada portrayed Kanna Izumi in the ongoing live‑action Ultraman Teo tokusatsu series.
- **Series Production Status**: TV Tokyo announced that the entire series has already been filmed, including Nekada’s scenes. The show will broadcast as originally scheduled.
- **Funeral**: A funeral was held for her close family.
- **Official Statements**: The Ultraman series’ X/Twitter account shared an image and credited 円⾕プロ, the Ultraman Teo production committee, TV Tokyo, and TOHO Entertainment in the announcement.

**The wait is over!**

In a whirlwind announcement that has set the WeebHQ community on fire, the Korean webtoon powerhouse REDICE STUDIO confirmed that the wildly popular *Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint* is returning for its second season on **November 24** at 10:00 p.m. KST (8:00 a.m. EST). A brand‑new promo video, coupled with the chilling tagline *"This story is our record of survival,"* has already sparked a frenzy of speculation and excitement among fans worldwide. The stakes are higher than ever, and the countdown has begun.

## What We Know

- **Release Date & Time**: Season 2 launches on **November 24** at 10:00 p.m. KST (8:00 a.m. EST). 
- **New Promo**: REDICE STUDIO streamed an official promo video on its X (formerly Twitter) account, revealing the tone and intensity of the upcoming arc.
- **Credits**: The new season will be produced under the REDICE STUDIO and STUDIO 3B2S banners.
- **Creators**: The webtoon continues to be penned by author **singNsong** and illustrated by **Sleepy‑C**, with adaptation by **UMI**.
- **Platform & Format**: The webtoon is available in English on WEBTOON under the title *Omniscient Reader*. Yen Press’s Ize Press imprint is publishing the print version as *Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint*; the 12th volume hit shelves on October 20.
- **Novel Companion**: The original web novel, also by singNsong, is being released in print by Ize Press, with the fifth volume slated for November 24.
- **Viewership Milestone**: The combined web novel and webtoon have amassed a staggering **2.5 billion views**.
- **Related Media**: Crunchyroll announced an anime adaptation in July 2024, *Omniscient Reader: The Prophecy* premiered in South Korea in July 2025, and Com2uS is developing a game expected in 2028.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The sheer scale of the franchise’s success—over 2.5 billion views—has cultivated a passionate and diverse fanbase that spans continents. The announcement of a second season arrives just weeks after the first season’s conclusion on June 9, 2026, leaving readers hungry for the next chapter. The new promo’s stark tagline hints at deeper stakes, promising a narrative that feels both personal and epic. Moreover, the synchronization of the webtoon’s return with the print release of the fifth novel volume on the same day creates a unified celebratory moment for fans who consume the story across multiple formats.

Community chatter on Discord, Reddit, and fan forums is already buzzing with theories about how the story will evolve. Fans are also eager to see how the collaboration between REDICE STUDIO and STUDIO 3B2S will influence the pacing and visual style that made the first season a phenomenon. The anticipation is amplified by the franchise’s expanding universe, including an anime adaptation and a forthcoming game, which collectively promise a richer, more immersive experience.

## Background

*Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint* debuted on WEBTOON in May 2020, quickly distinguishing itself with its meta‑narrative structure and the unique premise of a reader who can see the story’s future. The series’ blend of action, psychological depth, and intricate world‑building has earned it a devoted following. The first season, which concluded on June 9, 2026, saw the protagonist navigate a series of increasingly complex challenges, cementing the series’ reputation for high‑stakes storytelling.

The franchise’s expansion beyond the webtoon is noteworthy. Yen Press’s Ize Press imprint brought the story to print readers, while Crunchyroll’s announcement of an anime adaptation in 2024 signaled the series’ growing influence in mainstream anime culture. The live‑action film *Omniscient Reader: The Prophecy* opened in South Korea in July 2025, further broadening the audience. The upcoming game by Com2uS, slated for 2028, indicates a long‑term vision for the franchise’s longevity.

## What’s Next

With the second season’s release date set, the community can expect a surge of related content. The simultaneous launch of the fifth novel volume suggests that the webtoon’s storyline will closely align with the novel’s narrative arc, offering readers a cohesive experience across mediums. Given the partnership with STUDIO 3B2S, fans might anticipate a refined art style and tighter pacing, building on the strengths of the first season.

The franchise’s continued growth hints at more cross‑media projects.

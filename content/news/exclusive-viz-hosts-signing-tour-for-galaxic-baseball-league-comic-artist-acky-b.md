## Viz Media Hosts Signing Tour for GalaXic Baseball League Artist

Viz Media has announced an exclusive signing tour for Acky Bright, the artist behind the hit manga series **GalaXic Baseball League**. The series features an interplanetary baseball league with real-life baseball player appearances.

## Tour Schedule

The tour kicks off on **October 13 in New York** and will span multiple cities across the United States. Fans will have the opportunity to meet Acky Bright and get their copies signed.

## About GalaXic Baseball League

GalaXic Baseball League is a unique manga that blends sports action with science fiction, featuring baseball teams from across the galaxy competing in an intergalactic tournament.

# Sanrio’s Kuromi Gets First Film – Release Set for 2027, Fans Celebrate!

The anime community is buzzing with an announcement that could change the way we see Sanrio’s roster of characters. On Halloween – which also happens to be Kuromi’s birthday – Sanrio revealed that the mischievous, black‑hat‑wearing anti‑heroine will star in her first feature‑length film, **Kuromi the Movie**. The film will be distributed by GAGA in Japan next year, with international release details to follow once confirmed.

## What We Know

- **Title & Distributor** – The official title is *Kuromi the Movie*, and GAGA will handle distribution in Japan.
- **Release Window** – The film is slated for release in 2027.
- **International Roll‑out** – The website and video message state that “release information for territories outside Japan will be announced once confirmed.”
- **Announcement Format** – Sanrio posted an English‑subtitled video message from Kuromi on Halloween, the character’s birthday, to celebrate the news.
- **Historical Context** – Kuromi first appeared in the television anime *Onegai My Melody* in April 2005, not in Sanrio’s own merchandise line. Since then she has steadily risen in popularity, consistently ranking in the top five of the Sanrio Character Ranking poll for the past six years.
- **Recent Works** – She has already starred in two series of *Kuromi’s Pretty Journey* anime shorts (2023‑2024) and a stop‑motion anime *My Melody & Kuromi* on Netflix (July 2025).

## Why Fans Are Excited

For years, fans have been rooting for Kuromi to get her own spotlight. The character’s edgy personality and unique visual design set her apart from the more wholesome Sanrio lineup. The announcement comes on a day that already holds significance for her—Halloween and her birthday—adding a layer of celebratory timing that fans have embraced.

The sheer fact that Sanrio is investing in a full‑length feature film signals a shift in how the company views its characters. Kuromi’s consistent top‑five ranking in the Sanrio Character Ranking poll demonstrates a dedicated fan base that is eager to see more of her adventures. The film’s title, *Kuromi the Movie*, has already sparked speculation about how the studio will translate her mischievous charm to the big screen.

Social media has exploded with fan art, memes, and discussion threads. Many are excited about the potential for deeper character development and the opportunity to see Kuromi’s backstory explored in a cinematic format. The fact that the distributor, GAGA, is handling the release also raises expectations for high production values and a polished final product.

## Background

Kuromi first appeared in *Onegai My Melody* in April 2005, a television anime that introduced her to audiences outside the Sanrio merchandise realm. Over the years, she has grown into a distinct character with a dedicated following. Her popularity is reflected in her consistent top‑five ranking in the Sanrio Character Ranking poll for six consecutive years.

In 2023 and 2024, Kuromi starred in *Kuromi’s Pretty Journey*, a series of anime shorts that showcased her personality in a lighter, more comedic format. The following year, she appeared in *My Melody & Kuromi*, a stop‑motion anime on Netflix released in July 2025. These projects have helped solidify her place as a staple in Sanrio’s expanding media lineup.

The announcement of *Kuromi the Movie* marks the culmination of a gradual but steady rise. From her modest debut to becoming a top‑ranking character, Kuromi’s journey reflects the evolving nature of Sanrio’s storytelling ambitions.

## What’s Next

While the film’s release date is set for 2027, fans can expect a few key developments in the coming months:

1. **Official Trailers** – Sanrio is likely to release a teaser or full trailer to give audiences a taste of the film’s tone and style.
2. **International Release Details** – The website’s note that “release information for territories outside Japan will be announced once confirmed” suggests that international distributors are being lined up. Fans in the U.S., Europe, and other regions should watch for updates.
3. **Merchandise Tie‑Ins** – Given the character’s strong merchandise history, we can anticipate a new line of Kuromi‑the‑Movie‑exclusive goods, including figures, apparel, and accessories.
4. **Cross‑Media Promotion** – Sanrio may coordinate a cross‑media campaign that includes collaborations with other anime platforms, streaming services, and possibly even a manga adaptation.

These steps will likely build momentum leading up to the film’s release, ensuring that the hype remains high and that the fan base stays engaged.

## Closing

Sanrio’s decision to give Kuromi her own feature film is a milestone for both the character and the company. It signals a willingness to push beyond traditional merchandise and into cinematic storytelling, while honoring a character that has steadily climbed the ranks of fan favorites. With GAGA handling distribution and a release slated for 2027, fans have a clear timeline to anticipate.

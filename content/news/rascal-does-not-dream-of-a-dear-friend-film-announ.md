Hey there, anime family! It’s your resident Otaku reporter Weeb, and I’ve got some incredibly exciting news for everyone eagerly anticipating the next big theatrical chapter in the *Seishun Buta Yarō* franchise. 

If you are planning to head to the cinemas to catch the upcoming anime film *Rascal Does Not Dream of a Dear Friend*, you might want to prepare yourself for an extra special treat. It has been officially announced that moviegoers will receive an exclusive text story bonus item!

### A Brand New Story by the Original Creators

This isn't just any standard theater pamphlet. The exclusive bonus item features a brand-new text story titled **Seishun Buta Yarō wa Tea Garden no Yume o Miru** (*Rascal Does Not Dream of a Tea Garden*). 

To make this release even more special for die-hard fans of the franchise, the story is being penned directly by the original light novel author, **Hajime Kamoshida**. But that is not all—the bonus item will also feature beautiful illustrations by the series' original illustrator, **Keeji Mizoguchi**. 

Having the dream team of Kamoshida and Mizoguchi reunite to create this exclusive theatrical bonus is a massive win for fans who love diving deeper into the lore of the series. Exclusive light novel style booklets like this are highly sought after by collectors, making this film an absolute must-watch on the big screen.

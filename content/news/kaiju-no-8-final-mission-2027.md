## Urgent: The Final Mission Is Coming in 2027
The moment many of us have been waiting for is finally here: *Kaiju No. 8* will conclude its story in a feature‑length film titled **Kaiju No. 8: The Final Mission**, slated for a 2027 theatrical release. Production I.G and TOHO Animation announced the news on Saturday, unveiling a high‑energy “super teaser visual” that promises a dramatic end to the saga. With the tagline *“The era of humans is over.”* the teaser sets the stage for a showdown that fans have been craving since the manga’s debut in 2020. This isn’t just another anime episode – it’s the culmination of a multi‑year journey that has spanned manga, TV, streaming, and even gaming. The stakes are higher than ever, and the entire community is buzzing.

## What We Know
- **Director and Studio** – Shigeyuki Miya is returning to helm the film, bringing his experience from the original *Kaiju No. 8* anime series. The project is a collaboration between Production I.G and TOHO Animation.
- **Film Title & Tagline** – The movie is officially titled *Kaiju No. 8: The Final Mission*. The teaser visual, designed by art director Shinji Kimura, carries the ominous tagline *“The era of humans is over.”*
- **Release Window** – The film is scheduled to open in 2027. No specific date has been announced yet, but the 2027 window confirms the story will wrap up in a cinematic format.
- **Super Teaser Visual** – A short video showcasing the teaser was released by the official English X (formerly Twitter) account for the franchise. The visual emphasizes the impending climax and sets a cinematic tone.
- **Cast Reunion** – The anime’s voice cast reunited for a “Defense Force Boot Camp in Tachikawa” event held at the Tachikawa Stage Garden in Tokyo. The event highlighted the franchise’s community engagement.
- **Anime Series History** – The first season aired on TV Tokyo in April 2024 and ran for 12 episodes. Crunchyroll streamed the series with both English subtitles and an English dub, and also streamed it live on X/ Twitter worldwide as it aired in Japan. In April 2025, Crunchyroll and Sony Pictures Entertainment screened a compilation film of the first season, *Kaiju No. 8: Mission Recon*, in U.S. and Canadian theaters.
- **Second Season** – Debuted on TV Tokyo and affiliated channels in July 2025, also streamed live on X/ Twitter and on Crunchyroll.
- **Short Series** – *Narumi’s Week at Work* debuted on September 5, consisting of four episodes that streamed weekly until September 25 on Crunchyroll.
- **Manga Status** – The manga, launched on Shueisha’s Shonen Jump+ in July 2020, concluded in July 2025. Viz Media is releasing the manga digitally and physically, while Shueisha publishes it on MANGA Plus.
- **Game Releases** – *Kaiju No. 8 the Game* launched for PC on Steam in October 2025 and for iOS and Android in August 2025.

## Background
*Kaiju No. 8* began as a manga by Naoya Matsumoto, launched on Shonen Jump+ in July 2020. The series follows a protagonist who transforms into a kaiju and seeks to fulfill his childhood dreams. The manga’s narrative depth and unique premise quickly garnered a dedicated fanbase.

The anime adaptation premiered on TV Tokyo in April 2024. With 12 episodes, it introduced the series’ world to a broader audience, aided by Crunchyroll’s global streaming and real‑time X/ Twitter broadcasts. The first season’s compilation film, *Kaiju No. 8: Mission Recon*, screened in U.S. and Canadian theaters in April 2025, further cementing the franchise’s international reach.

The second season, which debuted in July 2025, continued to expand the story’s scope. Meanwhile, the short series *Narumi’s Week at Work* offered a lighter, character‑focused perspective. The franchise’s expansion into mobile and PC gaming in August and October 2025, respectively, showcased its adaptability to various entertainment mediums.

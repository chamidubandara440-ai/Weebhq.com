# Yagi's New Hack-and-Slash Epic BARTLET FAIRY BASSLET Debuts on Shonen Jump+—Fans Rushing to Read

The anime and manga community is buzzing as Norihiro Yagi, the mastermind behind the legendary Claymore, drops a brand‑new hack‑and‑slash fantasy saga titled **BARTLET FAIRY BASSLET** on Shueisha’s Shonen Jump+ platform this Monday. With simultaneous English releases on MANGA Plus and Viz Media’s Shonen Jump, the launch is already turning into a global phenomenon. Fans are scrambling to download the first chapter, and the buzz is spreading faster than the shimmering fish that appears in the story’s opening scene.

## What We Know

- **Title & Platform**: *BARTLET FAIRY BASSLET* debuts on Shueisha’s Shonen Jump+ service.
- **Release Schedule**: The first chapter premiered Monday, with the second chapter slated for November 9.
- **English Availability**: MANGA Plus and Viz Media’s Shonen Jump are publishing the manga in English on the same day.
- **Premise**: The narrative kicks off after the protagonist, Knight‑Captain and Princess Brunhilde, defeats her nemesis and witnesses a mysterious shining fish falling from the sky.
- **Creator’s Background**: Yagi is renowned for *Claymore* (2001‑2014), *Angel Densetsu* (1993‑2000), and *Sōkyū no Ariadne* (2017‑2023). *Claymore* ran for 27 volumes and was adapted into a 2007 anime, later released on home video by Funimation.

## Why Fans Are Excited

The excitement is rooted in Yagi’s storied legacy. *Claymore* set a high bar for the hack‑and‑slash genre, blending brutal action with complex characters. The fact that Yagi is returning to a similar style immediately signals a fresh wave of intense battles and intricate world‑building. Additionally, the simultaneous English release removes the lag that often hampers international fandoms, allowing fans worldwide to engage with the story in real time.

The opening image—a shining fish dropping from the sky—has already sparked countless theories. Some speculate it hints at a supernatural element that could redefine the genre’s tropes, while others see it as a metaphor for destiny or the weight of responsibility that Brunhilde carries. The rapid spread of fan art, speculation threads, and reaction videos on social media platforms underscores the community’s eagerness to dissect every detail.

## Background

Norihiro Yagi’s career is a testament to the enduring appeal of fantasy manga. Beginning with *Angel Densetsu* in 1993, Yagi crafted a narrative that spanned 15 volumes and even inspired a 1996 OVA. He then launched *Claymore* in 2001, a series that ran for 13 years and culminated in 27 volumes. The series’ success led to a 2007 anime adaptation and home video releases by Funimation, cementing Yagi’s reputation as a master of the genre.

In 2017, Yagi introduced *Sōkyū no Ariadne* in Shogakukan’s Weekly Shonen Sunday, which concluded in 2023 with 22 volumes. This recent work demonstrated Yagi’s continued relevance and adaptability to modern manga trends while maintaining his signature blend of action and storytelling.

## What's Next

While the source material doesn’t reveal plot twists beyond the first chapter, several grounded speculations arise. The presence of a “mysterious shining fish” suggests a supernatural element that could serve as a catalyst for a larger conflict, possibly involving celestial beings or a hidden realm. Given Yagi’s track record, we can anticipate a deep exploration of character motivations, especially for Princess Brunhilde, who balances royal duties with her role as a knight‑captain.

The scheduled release of the second chapter on November 9 offers a clear timeline for fans to anticipate. With the English versions already available, international readers can stay in sync with the Japanese release, fostering a unified global fanbase that will likely converge on discussion forums and social media as new chapters drop.

## Closing

BARTLET FAIRY BASSLET’s launch marks a thrilling new chapter in Norihiro Yagi’s illustrious career. The combination of a proven creator, a fresh hack‑and‑slash premise, and simultaneous worldwide availability creates a perfect storm for fan engagement. As the second chapter approaches, the community’s anticipation is at a fever pitch—ready to dive into the next epic adventure that Yagi has meticulously crafted.
